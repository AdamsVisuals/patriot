Overview of Trekking Safaris in East Africa

Experience Africa's wilderness at its most authentic with our guided trekking safaris across East Africa's most spectacular landscapes. From the volcanic slopes of Mount Kilimanjaro to the misty forests of Rwanda's Volcanoes National Park, these immersive journeys bring you closer to nature than any vehicle safari could.

Our trekking adventures include expert guides, quality camping equipment, and carefully planned routes that showcase the diverse ecosystems of Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda, and Madagascar.

What to Expect

Authentic wilderness experiences and guided adventures on foot.

Guided Wilderness Walks

Expert-led treks through diverse landscapes with armed guides for safety. Learn about animal tracking, plant identification, and wilderness survival skills while exploring Africa's most remote regions.

Wilderness Camping

Overnight in quality tents under African skies. Enjoy campfire meals, stargazing, and the sounds of the wilderness. All camping equipment provided for comfortable outdoor living.

Mountain Challenges

Summit attempts on East Africa's iconic peaks including Kilimanjaro, Mount Kenya, and the Rwenzori Mountains. Professional guides, porters, and proper acclimatization schedules.

Wildlife Encounters

Track animals on foot in designated walking areas. Observe elephants, buffalo, and antelope from safe distances with experienced guides who understand animal behavior.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our trekking safari experiences. We're here to help prepare you for your wilderness adventure.

What fitness level is required for trekking safaris?

add

We offer treks for all fitness levels, from gentle nature walks to challenging summit attempts. Each trek description includes difficulty ratings and recommended fitness preparation.

Is it safe to walk in areas with wildlife?

add

All walking safaris are led by experienced, armed guides in designated walking areas. We maintain safe distances and follow strict safety protocols for wildlife encounters.

What equipment do I need to bring?

add

We provide camping equipment, meals, and guides. You'll need personal items like hiking boots, daypack, sleeping bag, and appropriate clothing. A detailed packing list is provided.

What's the best time of year for trekking?

add

Dry seasons (June-October and December-February) offer the best conditions. However, we operate year-round and can recommend the optimal time for your chosen destination.
