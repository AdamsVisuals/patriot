FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our combined East Africa safari experiences. We're here to help plan your perfect multi-country adventure.

How many countries can we visit on one safari?

add

Typically 2-3 countries are combined in a 10-18 day safari. Popular combinations include Kenya-Tanzania, Rwanda-Uganda, or Kenya-Tanzania-Rwanda. We design itineraries based on your interests and timeframe.

Do we need multiple visas for combined safaris?

add

Yes, each country requires its own visa. We provide detailed visa information and can assist with applications. East African Tourist Visa is available for Kenya, Uganda, and Rwanda for combined travel.

How do we travel between countries?

add

We use a combination of regional flights and road transfers depending on the route. Most transfers are by scheduled flights between major hubs, with scenic road travel for shorter distances.

What's the best time for combined East Africa safaris?

add

Dry seasons (June-October and December-March) are ideal for most countries. However, we can design itineraries year-round, considering seasonal variations and migration patterns.
