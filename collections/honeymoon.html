What to Expect

Private Game Drives

Exclusive wildlife viewing in custom safari vehicles with just your guide. Enjoy sunset drives with sundowners and intimate moments watching Africa's magnificent wildlife in complete privacy.

Romantic Dining

Candlelit bush dinners under starlit skies, private deck dining overlooking watering holes, and champagne breakfasts in scenic locations. Every meal becomes a romantic occasion.
