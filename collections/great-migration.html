Great Migration

Overview of the Great Migration Experience

Experience the world's most spectacular wildlife event as millions of wildebeest, zebra, and gazelle journey across the vast plains of East Africa. Our Great Migration safaris offer front-row seats to nature's greatest drama, from dramatic river crossings to predator-prey interactions in the heart of the Serengeti and Maasai Mara ecosystems.

Our expert guides track the migration patterns year-round, ensuring optimal viewing opportunities with luxurious accommodations and private vehicles for the ultimate wildlife spectacle.

What to Expect on Migration Safari

Unforgettable experiences and dramatic wildlife encounters during the Great Migration.

Dramatic River Crossings

Witness the most dramatic moments of the migration as thousands of wildebeest and zebra brave crocodile-infested rivers. Our expert guides position you at prime crossing points for this breathtaking natural spectacle.

Predator Action

Experience intense predator-prey interactions as lions, cheetahs, and hyenas follow the herds. Our guides know where to find the most active predator territories during migration season.

Vast Moving Herds

Marvel at the sheer scale of over 1.5 million wildebeest and hundreds of thousands of zebra and gazelle moving as one massive herd across the endless plains of the Serengeti and Maasai Mara.

Aerial Migration Views

Optional hot air balloon safaris provide breathtaking aerial perspectives of the migration, allowing you to appreciate the massive scale of this natural wonder from above as the sun rises over the African plains.
