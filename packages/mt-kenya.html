Technical Climbing Adventure

Conquer Kenya's Rooftop

Mount Kenya stands as Africa's second highest peak at 5,199 meters (17,057 feet), offering some of the continent's most rewarding technical climbs. Journey through diverse ecosystems - from equatorial forest to alpine moorland - on your way to Point Lenana or the technical peaks of Batian and Nelion. Our certified guides ensure safe ascents through this stunning volcanic landscape.
