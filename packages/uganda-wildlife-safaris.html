Ultimate Wildlife Adventure

Discover Uganda's Incredible Wildlife Diversity

Uganda offers some of Africa's most spectacular wildlife viewing experiences across its diverse ecosystems. Trek to encounter endangered mountain gorillas in Bwindi, witness the tree-climbing lions of Queen Elizabeth, explore the vast savannahs of Murchison Falls, and discover the primate-rich forests of Kibale. From great apes to the Big Five, Uganda's parks provide unforgettable wildlife encounters.
