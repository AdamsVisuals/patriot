15 Days / 14 Nights | From $4,500

15-Day Southern Madagascar Highlights Adventure

Embark on an unforgettable 15-day journey through southern Madagascar's most spectacular landscapes and diverse ecosystems - from the lush rainforests of Andasibe and Ranomafana to the dramatic canyons of Isalo and the tropical beaches of Ifaty. This carefully curated adventure offers an authentic experience showcasing Madagascar's unique biodiversity, geological wonders, and cultural heritage.

Experience the magic of Madagascar as you encounter various lemur species in their natural habitats, explore geothermal springs, hike through primary rainforests, marvel at dramatic canyon landscapes, and relax on pristine Indian Ocean beaches. Witness the incredible transition from Madagascar's central highlands through its spiny forests to its western coastline.

Our expert local guides will share their extensive knowledge of Madagascar's ecosystems, geological formations, and cultural traditions, ensuring you gain deep insights into this island nation's wonders. With comfortable accommodations and seamless logistics, you can focus entirely on this breathtaking adventure across diverse regions.

This comprehensive journey is perfect for adventure seekers and nature enthusiasts looking for diverse experiences, from rainforest hikes and wildlife spotting to geological exploration and coastal relaxation. Discover the best of southern Madagascar's natural and cultural treasures in an immersive 15-day itinerary.

Detailed 15-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Antananarivo

Activities: Airport transfer, hotel check-in, trip briefing

Afternoon/Evening
Upon your arrival at Ivato International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your English-speaking guide who will assist you with your luggage and transfer you to your hotel in Antananarivo. The journey offers your first glimpse of Madagascar's capital city, known locally as "Tana," with its bustling streets and distinctive architecture blending traditional Malagasy and colonial influences.

After settling into your hotel, enjoy a comprehensive trip briefing with your guide. They will provide detailed information about the upcoming adventure through southern Madagascar, answer any questions you may have, and ensure you're well-prepared for the journey ahead. Enjoy dinner at your hotel or a nearby restaurant, resting after your international flight.

Day 2: Antananarivo - Andasibe / Mantadia

Distance/Time: Approx. 3-4 hours drive from Antananarivo to Andasibe

Morning
After breakfast, depart Antananarivo and drive eastwards towards Andasibe, traveling through the scenic eastern slopes of Madagascar's central highlands. The journey offers beautiful views of terraced rice paddies, traditional villages, and rolling hills as you descend from the highlands toward the eastern rainforests.

Afternoon
En route, stop at Mandraka to visit the Peyrieras Reserve (also known as Madagascar Exotic), a private reptile and insect farm where you can see various chameleons, geckos, frogs, and insects in a controlled environment. This is an excellent introduction to Madagascar's unique herpetofauna before entering the rainforest region.

Evening
Arrive in Andasibe and visit VOIMMA Nature Reserve, a community-managed reserve where local villagers guide you through the forest. Take an introductory walk to spot your first lemurs in their natural habitat and listen for the iconic call of the Indri, Madagascar's largest lemur species. After dinner, enjoy a night walk to observe nocturnal animals like mouse lemurs, chameleons, and various insects that become active after dark.

Day 3: Andasibe National Park

Activities: Primary rainforest hiking, wildlife spotting

Full Day
Today is dedicated to exploring Mantadia National Park, part of the Andasibe-Mantadia protected area complex. This primary rainforest offers excellent hiking trails through pristine forest with abundant wildlife. The park is home to several lemur species including the Diademed Sifaka (one of Madagascar's most beautiful lemurs), Black and White Ruffed Lemur, and Common Brown Lemur.

As you hike through the lush vegetation, your guide will help you spot various bird species including the Madagascar Blue Pigeon, Velvet Asity, and numerous endemic vanga species. The forest is also rich in orchids, medicinal plants, and towering trees draped in moss and epiphytes. Enjoy a picnic lunch surrounded by the sounds of the rainforest, listening for the distinctive calls of different lemur species communicating through the forest canopy.

What to Expect: Moderate hiking on forest trails, excellent lemur viewing opportunities, diverse birdwatching, and immersion in pristine primary rainforest ecosystems.

Day 4: Andasibe - Antsirabe

Distance/Time: Approx. 6-7 hours drive

Morning
Enjoy an early morning visit to Analamazaotra National Park (also known as Andasibe National Park) to search for the famous Indri Indri during their most active period. These black and white lemurs, the largest living lemurs, are known for their haunting calls that can be heard up to 3 kilometers away. The morning hours offer the best chance to observe them feeding and moving through the forest canopy.

Afternoon
After lunch, begin your journey southwards to Antsirabe, Madagascar's third-largest city known for its thermal springs and relatively cool climate. The drive takes you through the scenic landscapes of Madagascar's central highlands, passing through rural communities, agricultural areas, and changing vegetation as you travel south.

Evening
Arrive in Antsirabe and check into your accommodation. Known as the "Vichy of Madagascar" due to its thermal springs established during the colonial period, Antsirabe offers a pleasant climate and interesting architectural heritage reflecting its history as a health resort.

Day 5: Antsirabe - Ranomafana

Distance/Time: Approx. 5-6 hours drive

Full Day
Continue your journey further south to Ranomafana, with several sightseeing stops along the way. The route takes you through traditional highland villages where you can observe daily life and local crafts. Stop at handicraft workshops where artisans create beautiful wood carvings, woven baskets, and other traditional items using techniques passed down through generations.

The landscape gradually changes as you travel south, with rice paddies giving way to different agricultural patterns and eventually approaching the rainforest region around Ranomafana. The journey offers opportunities to see traditional Betsileo tribe villages and learn about their agricultural practices and cultural traditions.

Evening
Arrive in Ranomafana, a town whose name means "hot water" in Malagasy, referring to the thermal springs found in the area. Settle into your accommodation and prepare for tomorrow's exploration of Ranomafana National Park.

Day 6: Ranomafana National Park

Activities: Rainforest hiking, wildlife spotting, village visit

Morning
Embark on a walking safari in Ranomafana National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its rich biodiversity. The park protects one of Madagascar's last remaining stands of eastern rainforest and is home to 12 species of lemurs, including the critically endangered Golden Bamboo Lemur and the Greater Bamboo Lemur.

As you hike through the lush forest, your guide will help you spot various lemur species, chameleons, frogs, and the park's diverse birdlife. The trails wind through beautiful rainforest scenery with streams, waterfalls, and dense vegetation creating a magical atmosphere.

Afternoon
Visit the village of Ranomafana to learn about local life and the community's relationship with the national park. The park's establishment has brought both challenges and opportunities for local communities, and your visit supports community-based tourism initiatives.

Evening
After dinner, enjoy evening walks to spot nocturnal wildlife. Ranomafana's forests come alive after dark with different species becoming active, offering a completely different wildlife viewing experience from daytime exploration.

Day 7: Ranomafana - Fianarantsoa - Anja - Tsaranoro

Distance/Time: Approx. 5-6 hours drive including stops

Morning
Depart southwards from Ranomafana, stopping in Fianarantsoa to visit historical sites. As the cultural and intellectual capital of the Betsileo tribe, Fianarantsoa features interesting architecture including the old upper town with its traditional houses and colonial-era buildings. The city is also known as Madagascar's wine-producing region and center for Catholic missions.

Afternoon
Continue to Anja Reserve, a community-managed forest reserve that provides excellent opportunities to see Ring-tailed Lemurs in their natural habitat. Take a hike through the reserve's distinctive granite formations and dry forest to observe these iconic lemurs up close. The reserve also offers beautiful panoramic views of the surrounding agricultural landscape.

Evening
Continue off-road to the remote and spectacular Tsaranoro Valley, arriving in time to appreciate the dramatic scenery as the setting sun illuminates the granite cliffs. The valley offers breathtaking views of the surrounding mountains and a true sense of wilderness isolation.

Day 8: Tsaranoro Valley - Andringitra National Park

Activities: Hiking, valley exploration, scenic viewpoints

Full Day
Enjoy a full day of excursions and explorations in the western parts of Andringitra National Park and the Tsaranoro Valley. Andringitra is known for its dramatic granite peaks, deep valleys, and diverse ecosystems ranging from montane forest to high-altitude grassland.

Options for today include hiking to scenic viewpoints overlooking the valley, exploring local villages to learn about Betsileo culture, or taking guided walks to discover the area's unique flora and fauna. The Tsaranoro Valley is particularly famous among rock climbers for its impressive granite walls, but there are also beautiful hikes suitable for all fitness levels.

What to Expect: Spectacular mountain scenery, optional hiking at various difficulty levels, cultural interactions with local communities, and immersion in one of Madagascar's most dramatic landscapes.

Day 9: Tsaranoro - Isalo

Distance/Time: Approx. 5-6 hours drive

Full Day
Depart from Tsaranoro and travel west to Isalo National Park, experiencing the dramatic transition from highland landscapes to the drier southwest region of Madagascar. The journey takes you through changing vegetation zones, from the lush valleys around Andringitra to the increasingly arid landscapes approaching Isalo.

As you approach Isalo, the scenery becomes dominated by dramatic sandstone formations, deep canyons, and unique vegetation adapted to the dry conditions. The Isalo massif rises dramatically from the surrounding plains, creating a striking landscape that has been shaped by millions of years of erosion.

Evening Highlight
Arrive at Isalo in time for sunset, when the golden light creates magical photographic conditions. Watch as the sandstone cliffs are illuminated in warm tones, creating one of Madagascar's most iconic and photogenic landscapes.

Day 10: Isalo National Park

Activities: Full day hiking, canyon exploration, natural pools

Full Day
Enjoy a full day hike through Isalo National Park, exploring its dramatic canyons, natural swimming pools, and unique sandstone formations. The park's most popular hike takes you through the Canyon des Makis, where you can spot Ring-tailed Lemurs, to the beautiful Piscine Naturelle - a natural pool surrounded by palm trees where you can take a refreshing swim.

Continue to the Canyon des Rats, another beautiful canyon with interesting rock formations and endemic vegetation. Isalo is known for its "window of Isalo" rock formation and various natural swimming holes that provide welcome relief from the heat. The park's vegetation includes endemic succulents like the elephant's foot plant (Pachypodium) and various aloe species adapted to the arid conditions.

What to Expect: Moderate hiking on established trails, dramatic canyon scenery, refreshing natural swimming pools, unique desert-adapted flora, and excellent photographic opportunities.

Day 11: Isalo - Tulear - Ifaty

Distance/Time: Approx. 5-6 hours drive including stops

Morning
Depart Isalo and continue your journey southwest towards the coast. En route, visit Zombitse National Park, a transitional forest between the dry spiny forest of the southwest and the humid forests of the east. This small but important park is home to several lemur species and is particularly noted for its birdlife, including the endangered Appert's Tetraka.

Afternoon
Continue your drive with sightseeing along the way, observing the changing landscape as you approach the coast. The vegetation becomes dominated by the unique spiny forest ecosystem, characterized by drought-adapted plants like octopus trees (Didierea madagascariensis) and various euphorbia species.

Evening
Arrive at the coastal village of Ifaty, located on a beautiful stretch of Madagascar's southwestern coastline. Check into your beach accommodation and enjoy your first sunset over the Mozambique Channel.

Day 12: Ifaty

Activities: Beach relaxation, boat excursion, snorkeling/diving

Full Day
Today is at leisure to enjoy the beautiful beaches and marine environment of Ifaty. Start with a boat excursion to explore the coastal waters of the Mozambique Channel. The area is known for its coral reefs, which support diverse marine life including tropical fish, sea turtles, and occasionally dolphins.

Enjoy snorkeling on the coral reefs to observe the underwater biodiversity, or for certified divers, there are possibilities for scuba diving to explore deeper reef areas (equipment rental and diving fees not included in basic package). The clear waters and generally calm conditions make this an excellent location for marine activities.

What to Expect: Beautiful beaches, excellent snorkeling opportunities, boat excursions along the coast, and relaxation after your journey through southern Madagascar.

Day 13: Ifaty

Activities: Beach relaxation, optional activities, leisure time

Full Day
Another day at leisure in Ifaty to enjoy the beach and coastal environment. Options for today include additional snorkeling or diving trips, visits to local Vezo fishing villages to learn about traditional fishing techniques and culture, or simply relaxing on the beautiful sandy beaches.

You might also choose to visit the nearby Reniala Reserve (Spiny Forest Reserve) to see the unique flora of Madagascar's southwestern dry forest ecosystem, including baobabs, octopus trees, and various endemic succulents. This protected area offers easy walking trails and excellent birdwatching opportunities.

What to Expect: Complete relaxation, optional cultural or natural excursions, beautiful coastal scenery, and opportunities to experience local Vezo culture.

Day 14: Ifaty - Tulear - Antananarivo

Activities: City tour, market visit, domestic flight

Morning
After breakfast, transfer to Tulear (Toliara) for a city tour and visit to the local market. Tulear is the capital of the Atsimo-Andrefana region and an important port city on Madagascar's southwest coast. Visit the market to see the variety of goods including seafood, spices, and handicrafts, and learn about the city's role as a trading center.

Afternoon
Transfer to Tulear Airport for your flight back to Antananarivo. The domestic flight returns you to Madagascar's capital, offering aerial views of the diverse landscapes you've traveled through over the past two weeks.

Evening
Upon arrival in Antananarivo, transfer to your hotel. Enjoy a farewell dinner reflecting on your incredible journey through southern Madagascar's diverse landscapes, from rainforests and highlands to canyons and coastline.

Day 15: Antananarivo - Departure

Activities: Departure transfer

Morning/Afternoon
Depending on your flight schedule, you may have some free time for last-minute souvenir shopping or additional exploration of Antananarivo. Visit the Marché Artisanal de La Digue for handicrafts, textiles, and spices to bring home as souvenirs of your Madagascar adventure.

Transfer to Ivato International Airport for your flight homewards or continuation to another destination. Bid farewell to Madagascar with unforgettable memories of diverse landscapes, unique wildlife encounters, and rich cultural experiences from your 15-day journey through the southern highlights of this remarkable island nation.
