12 Day Madagascar Lemurs, Baobabs & Tsingy Adventure

Discover the unique wildlife and breathtaking landscapes of Madagascar - from lemur-filled rainforests to dramatic limestone cathedrals

12 Days / 11 Nights | From $4,280

12-Day Madagascar Wildlife & Landscape Adventure

Embark on an unforgettable 12-day journey through Madagascar's most spectacular destinations - from the lush rainforests of Andasibe to the iconic Baobab Avenue and the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Tsingy de Bemaraha. This carefully curated adventure offers an authentic experience across diverse ecosystems, showcasing Madagascar's unique biodiversity and breathtaking landscapes.

Experience the magic of Madagascar as you encounter various lemur species in their natural habitat, walk among ancient baobab trees, and explore the dramatic limestone formations of Tsingy de Bemaraha. Witness the incredible biodiversity of Madagascar's eastern rainforests and the unique flora and fauna of its western dry forests.

Our expert local guides will share their extensive knowledge of Madagascar's ecosystems, lemur behaviors, and cultural heritage, ensuring you gain deep insights into this island nation's natural wonders. With comfortable accommodations and seamless logistics, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking adventure across these distinct regions.

This comprehensive journey is perfect for nature enthusiasts seeking diverse experiences, from rainforest hikes and lemur spotting to exploring geological wonders and coastal relaxation. Discover the best of Madagascar's wildlife and landscapes in an immersive 12-day itinerary.

Detailed 12-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Antananarivo

Distance/Time: Approx. 3-4 hours drive from Antananarivo to Andasibe

Morning
Upon your arrival at Ivato International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your English-speaking guide who will assist you with your luggage. After a brief introduction, begin your journey eastwards towards Andasibe, traveling through the scenic eastern slopes of Madagascar's central highlands.

Afternoon
En route, stop at Mandraka to visit the Peyrieras Reserve (also known as Madagascar Exotic), a private reserve where you can see various chameleons, geckos, frogs, and insects in a controlled environment. This is an excellent introduction to Madagascar's unique herpetofauna.

Evening
Arrive in Andasibe and visit VOIMMA Nature Reserve, a community-managed reserve where you'll take an introductory walk to spot your first lemurs in their natural habitat. Listen for the iconic call of the Indri, Madagascar's largest lemur species. Check into your accommodation and enjoy dinner with the sounds of the rainforest as your backdrop.

Day 2: Andasibe / Mantadia Exploration

Activities: Rainforest hiking, lemur spotting, community reserve visit

Morning
After breakfast, depart for a full day exploring the Andasibe-Mantadia National Park complex. Begin with Mantadia National Park, a primary rainforest with excellent hiking trails and abundant wildlife. Look for the Diademed Sifaka, Black and White Ruffed Lemur, and various bird species including the Madagascar Blue Pigeon and Velvet Asity.

Afternoon
Enjoy a picnic lunch in the forest before continuing your exploration. The Mantadia sector offers a more rugged and less-visited experience compared to Analamazaotra, with opportunities to see rarer lemur species and enjoy pristine rainforest scenery.

Evening
Return to your accommodation. For nature enthusiasts, there's an option to camp at Maromizaha Reserve (extra cost) to allow for night walks and very early morning exploration when many nocturnal species are active.

Day 3: Andasibe National Park

Activities: Primary rainforest hiking, wildlife spotting, optional camping

Morning
Today is dedicated to exploring the lush primary rainforest of Mantadia Reserve. This protected area features excellent hiking trails through pristine forest where you can search for various lemur species including the Diademed Sifaka, Black and White Ruffed Lemur, and Common Brown Lemur.

Afternoon
Continue your exploration, looking for other endemic species like the Giraffe-necked Weevil, various chameleons, and numerous bird species. The forest here is particularly rich in orchids and other epiphytes.

Evening
For dedicated nature lovers, there's an optional overnight camping experience at Maromizaha Reserve. This allows for night walks to spot nocturnal animals and very early morning exploration when wildlife is most active.

Day 4: Andasibe - Antananarivo

Distance/Time: Approx. 3-4 hours drive back to Antananarivo

Morning
Enjoy a final morning hike in Analamazaotra National Park (also known as Andasibe National Park) to search for the famous Indri Indri, whose haunting calls echo through the forest. Visit Lemurs' Island at Vakona Private Reserve, where you can observe several semi-tame lemur species up close in a controlled environment.

Afternoon
After lunch, begin your drive back to Antananarivo, enjoying the scenic landscapes of Madagascar's central highlands. The journey offers views of rice paddies, traditional villages, and rolling hills.

Evening
Arrive in Antananarivo and check into your hotel. Enjoy dinner and overnight stay in the capital city.

Day 5: Antananarivo - Morondava - Kirindy Reserve

Activities: Domestic flight, baobab viewing, night walk

Morning
Transfer to Ivato Airport for your domestic flight to Morondava. Upon arrival, meet your new guide and driver and begin your journey northwards through the famous "land of baobabs."

Afternoon
Stop at the iconic Baobab Avenue to photograph these ancient trees. Continue to Kirindy Reserve, a dry deciduous forest known for its unique wildlife including the Fossa (Madagascar's largest predator), various lemurs, and endemic birds.

Evening
After settling into your accommodation, embark on a night walk inside Kirindy's dry forest to spot nocturnal animals including mouse lemurs, sportive lemurs, and various reptiles and insects that are active after dark.

Day 6: Kirindy - Tsingy de Bemaraha

Distance/Time: Approx. 5-6 hours drive on rough road

Morning
After breakfast, enjoy a leisurely morning walking safari inside Kirindy Reserve. Search for the Fossa, Madagascar's apex predator, as well as various lemur species including Verreaux's Sifaka and Red-fronted Brown Lemurs. The reserve is also excellent for birdwatching.

Afternoon
Begin the challenging but rewarding drive northwards to Tsingy de Bemaraha National Park. The journey takes you through remote landscapes on red lateritic and sandy roads, offering glimpses of traditional Sakalava villages and changing vegetation.

Evening
Arrive at your accommodation near Tsingy de Bemaraha, settle in, and enjoy dinner while preparing for tomorrow's adventure in the "stone forest."

Day 7: Tsingy de Bemaraha National Park

Activities: Full day hiking in Grand Tsingy

Full Day
Early morning departure to the Grand Tsingy for a full day of exploration. Depending on your physical condition and preferences, you'll hike either the Broadway Trail (easier) or the Andamozavaky Trail (more challenging). Both offer spectacular views of the razor-sharp limestone formations.

The hike involves traversing via ferrata sections with safety harnesses, crossing suspension bridges, and climbing ladders to reach breathtaking viewpoints. Look for Decken's Sifaka lemurs navigating the sharp rocks and various endemic plant species adapted to this unique environment.

What to Expect: Challenging but rewarding hike, spectacular geological formations, unique wildlife sightings, and unforgettable panoramic views.

Day 8: Tsingy de Bemaraha National Park

Activities: Pirogue ride, cave visit, cultural sites

Morning
Take a traditional pirogue (dugout canoe) ride upstream the Manambolo River, gliding between towering limestone cliffs. The river journey offers excellent birdwatching opportunities and a different perspective of the Tsingy formations.

Afternoon
Visit a stalagmite-stalactite cave typical of the Tsingy karst landscape. Continue to see the Vazimba tombs - burial sites of Madagascar's earliest inhabitants who were known for their small stature. Learn about local legends and cultural beliefs surrounding these sacred sites.

Evening
Return to your accommodation, with time to relax and reflect on the unique geological and cultural experiences of the day.

Day 9: Tsingy de Bemaraha - Baobab Avenue - Morondava

Distance/Time: Approx. 6-7 hours drive

Full Day
Begin your journey back southwards, retracing your route through the remote landscapes of western Madagascar. The drive offers another opportunity to observe the changing vegetation and traditional village life.

Evening Highlight
Arrive at the Baobab Avenue in time for sunset, when the golden light creates magical photographic conditions. Watch as the ancient baobabs are silhouetted against the colorful sky, creating one of Madagascar's most iconic scenes.

What to Expect: Long but scenic drive, incredible sunset photography at Baobab Avenue, arrival at coastal Morondava.

Day 10: Morondava

Activities: Sunrise at Baobab Avenue, city tour, mangrove exploration

Morning
Early morning departure to capture the magical morning light at Baobab Avenue. Experience the avenue at sunrise when the soft light creates different photographic opportunities and the site is less crowded.

Afternoon
Return to Morondava for a city tour including the local market where you can observe daily life and perhaps purchase some souvenirs. Take a sightseeing pirogue trip through the surrounding mangroves, observing coastal birdlife and learning about this important ecosystem.

Evening
Relax and enjoy the amenities of your beachfront hotel. Take a swim in the ocean or pool, and enjoy a farewell dinner on your last night in western Madagascar.

Day 11: Morondava - Antananarivo

Activities: Domestic flight, shopping, leisure time

Morning
Transfer to Morondava Airport for your flight back to Antananarivo. The flight offers aerial views of Madagascar's diverse landscapes as you cross from west to east.

Afternoon
Upon arrival in Antananarivo, transfer to your hotel. The afternoon is free for shopping at the handicraft market (Marché Artisanal de La Digue) where you can purchase souvenirs such as wood carvings, textiles, vanilla, and precious stones.

Evening
Enjoy your final dinner in Madagascar, reflecting on the incredible experiences of the past 11 days.

Day 12: Antananarivo - Departure

Activities: City tour, cultural experience, departure

Morning
Enjoy a city tour of Antananarivo including a visit to the renewed Rova of Antananarivo (Queen's Palace) which offers panoramic views over the city. Learn about Madagascar's royal history and the significance of this historical site.

Experience a typical Malagasy culinary experience in town, perhaps trying local specialties like romazava (meat and leafy green stew) or ravitoto (pork with crushed cassava leaves).

Afternoon
Rest of the day at leisure until your transfer to Ivato International Airport for your flight homewards. Bid farewell to Madagascar with unforgettable memories of lemurs, baobabs, and tsingy.
