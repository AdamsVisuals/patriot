14-Day Madagascar Wildlife & Wilderness Adventure

Embark on an unforgettable 14-day journey through Madagascar's most spectacular destinations - from the lush rainforests of Andasibe and Masoala to the unique dry forests of Kirindy and the dramatic Tsingy de Bemaraha. This carefully curated adventure offers an authentic experience showcasing Madagascar's diverse ecosystems, unique wildlife, and breathtaking landscapes.

Experience the magic of Madagascar as you encounter various lemur species in their natural rainforest habitat, explore remote Masoala National Park with its pristine beaches, witness the iconic Baobab Avenue, and discover the remarkable limestone formations of Tsingy de Bemaraha. This comprehensive itinerary combines Madagascar's most iconic natural attractions into one unforgettable journey.

Our expert local guides will share their extensive knowledge of Madagascar's ecosystems, wildlife behaviors, and cultural traditions, ensuring you gain deep insights into this island nation's wonders. With comfortable accommodations and seamless logistics including domestic flights, you can focus entirely on this breathtaking adventure across diverse regions.

This comprehensive journey is perfect for nature enthusiasts and adventure seekers looking for diverse experiences, from rainforest hikes and marine exploration to geological wonders and cultural encounters. Discover the best of Madagascar's natural and cultural treasures in an immersive 14-day itinerary.

Detailed 14-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Antananarivo

Activities: Airport transfer, hotel check-in, trip briefing

Afternoon/Evening
Upon your arrival at Ivato International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your English-speaking guide who will assist you with your luggage and transfer you to your hotel in Antananarivo. The journey offers your first glimpse of Madagascar's capital city, known locally as "Tana," with its bustling streets and distinctive architecture blending traditional Malagasy and colonial influences.

After settling into your hotel, enjoy a comprehensive trip briefing with your guide. They will provide detailed information about the upcoming adventure through multiple regions of Madagascar, answer any questions you may have, and ensure you're well-prepared for the journey ahead. Enjoy dinner at your hotel or a nearby restaurant, resting after your international flight.

Day 2: Antananarivo - Andasibe / Mantadia

Distance/Time: Approx. 3-4 hours drive from Antananarivo to Andasibe

Morning
After breakfast, depart Antananarivo and drive eastwards towards Andasibe, traveling through the scenic eastern slopes of Madagascar's central highlands. The journey offers beautiful views of terraced rice paddies, traditional villages, and rolling hills as you descend from the highlands toward the eastern rainforests.

Afternoon
En route, stop at Mandraka to visit the Peyrieras Reserve (also known as Madagascar Exotic), a private reptile and insect farm where you can see various chameleons, geckos, frogs, and insects in a controlled environment. This is an excellent introduction to Madagascar's unique herpetofauna before entering the rainforest region.

Evening
Arrive in Andasibe and visit VOIMMA Nature Reserve, a community-managed reserve where local villagers guide you through the forest. Take an introductory walk to spot your first lemurs in their natural habitat and listen for the iconic call of the Indri, Madagascar's largest lemur species. After dinner, enjoy a night walk to observe nocturnal animals like mouse lemurs, chameleons, and various insects that become active after dark.

Day 3: Andasibe National Park

Activities: Rainforest hiking, wildlife spotting, national park exploration

Full Day
Today is dedicated to exploring the Andasibe-Mantadia National Park complex. Enjoy a full day of hiking through the lush rainforest, searching for various lemur species including the famous Indri Indri, Diademed Sifaka, Black and White Ruffed Lemur, and Common Brown Lemur. The park's network of trails takes you through different forest types with opportunities to see diverse wildlife.

Listen for the haunting calls of the Indri, which echo through the forest particularly in the morning hours. Your guide will help you spot various bird species, chameleons, and other endemic animals that call this rainforest home. Enjoy a picnic lunch surrounded by the sounds of the rainforest, with the opportunity to learn about the park's conservation efforts and unique ecosystems.

What to Expect: Moderate hiking on forest trails, excellent lemur viewing opportunities, diverse birdwatching, and immersion in pristine primary rainforest ecosystems.

Day 4: Andasibe - Antananarivo

Distance/Time: Approx. 3-4 hours drive back to Antananarivo

Morning
Enjoy a final morning in the Andasibe area with a hike in Analamazaotra National Park (also known as Andasibe National Park). Search for the famous Indri Indri during their most active period. These black and white lemurs are the largest living lemurs and are known for their distinctive vocalizations that can be heard up to 3 kilometers away.

Visit Lemurs' Island at Vakona Private Reserve, where you can observe several semi-tame lemur species up close in a controlled environment. This island sanctuary provides excellent photographic opportunities and allows you to see lemurs from much closer than in the wild, including species like the Common Brown Lemur and Bamboo Lemur.

Afternoon
After lunch, begin your drive back to Antananarivo, enjoying the scenic landscapes of Madagascar's central highlands one last time. The journey offers final views of rice paddies, traditional villages, and rolling hills as you ascend back to the capital.

Evening
Arrive in Antananarivo and check into your hotel. Enjoy dinner and overnight stay in the capital city, preparing for your flight to northeastern Madagascar tomorrow.

Day 5: Antananarivo - Maroantsetra

Activities: Domestic flight, city tour, vanilla workshop visit

Morning
Transfer to Ivato Airport for your domestic flight to Maroantsetra, a town in northeastern Madagascar known as the gateway to Masoala National Park. The flight offers aerial views of Madagascar's diverse landscapes as you travel from the central highlands to the northeastern coast.

Afternoon
Upon arrival in Maroantsetra, meet your local guide and enjoy a city tour of this coastal town. Visit local vanilla workshops where you'll learn about Madagascar's famous vanilla production - the country is the world's largest producer of natural vanilla. See how vanilla beans are cultivated, processed, and prepared for export, and learn why Madagascar's vanilla is considered the finest in the world.

Evening
Check into your accommodation in Maroantsetra. Note that most boat transfers to Masoala are done only in early morning due to sea conditions and wind patterns, so prepare for an early departure tomorrow.

Day 6: Maroantsetra - Masoala National Park

Activities: Boat transfer, rainforest exploration, leisure time

Morning
Early morning boat transfer from Maroantsetra to Masoala National Park. The journey by traditional boat takes you along the coastline and into the remote wilderness of the Masoala Peninsula. This UNESCO World Heritage Site is one of Madagascar's most biodiverse and least-visited protected areas.

Afternoon
Upon arrival at your lodge in Masoala, settle in and then begin your initial exploration of the rainforest. Masoala National Park protects one of the last remaining large stretches of primary rainforest in Madagascar and is home to numerous endemic species including the Red Ruffed Lemur, which is found only in this region.

Evening
The rest of the day is at leisure to enjoy your remote wilderness setting. Relax at your lodge, take a walk along nearby trails, or simply soak in the peaceful atmosphere of this pristine natural environment.

Day 7: Masoala National Park

Activities: Walking safari, marine reserve exploration, leisure time

Morning
Embark on a walking safari inside the jungle of Masoala National Park. The park's trails take you through lush rainforest where you can search for various lemur species, including the Red Ruffed Lemur that is endemic to this region. Your guide will help you spot other wildlife including various bird species, chameleons, and unique plant life.

Afternoon
The rest of the day is at leisure to enjoy the wild and turquoise lagoon of Masoala's marine reserve. Masoala National Park is unique in combining terrestrial rainforest with marine protected areas, offering both forest and coastal experiences. Options include relaxing on pristine beaches, snorkeling in crystal-clear waters, or exploring coastal trails.

What to Expect: Excellent rainforest hiking, unique wildlife viewing, beautiful coastal scenery, and opportunities for marine exploration in one of Madagascar's most remote national parks.

Day 8: Masoala - Maroantsetra - Antananarivo

Activities: Boat transfer, domestic flight

Morning
After breakfast, take a boat transfer back to Maroantsetra, enjoying final views of the Masoala Peninsula's stunning coastline. The return journey allows you to appreciate the remote beauty of this region one last time.

Afternoon
Transfer to Maroantsetra Airport for your flight back to Antananarivo. The domestic flight returns you to Madagascar's capital, crossing diverse landscapes from the northeastern rainforests back to the central highlands.

Evening
Upon arrival in Antananarivo, transfer to your hotel. Enjoy dinner and overnight stay in the capital city, preparing for your flight to western Madagascar tomorrow.

Day 9: Antananarivo - Morondava - Kirindy

Activities: Domestic flight, Baobab Avenue visit, night walk

Morning
Transfer to Ivato Airport for your domestic flight to Morondava on Madagascar's west coast. Upon arrival, meet your new guide and driver and begin your journey to Kirindy Reserve.

Afternoon
Travel through the famous "land of baobabs," stopping at the iconic Baobab Avenue to photograph these ancient trees. These majestic Adansonia grandidieri trees, some over 800 years old, create one of Madagascar's most photographed landscapes.

Evening
Arrive at Kirindy Reserve, a dry deciduous forest known for its unique wildlife including the Fossa (Madagascar's largest predator), various lemurs, and endemic birds. After settling into your accommodation, embark on a night walk inside Kirindy's dry forest to spot nocturnal animals including mouse lemurs, sportive lemurs, and various reptiles and insects that are active after dark.

Day 10: Kirindy - Tsingy de Bemaraha

Distance/Time: Approx. 5-6 hours drive on rough road

Morning
After breakfast, enjoy a morning hike at Kirindy Reserve. Search for the Fossa, Madagascar's apex predator, as well as various lemur species including Verreaux's Sifaka and Red-fronted Brown Lemurs. The reserve is also excellent for birdwatching, with opportunities to see endemic species adapted to the dry forest environment.

Afternoon
Begin the challenging but rewarding drive northwards to Tsingy de Bemaraha National Park. The journey takes you through remote landscapes on red lateritic and sandy roads, offering glimpses of traditional Sakalava villages and changing vegetation as you approach the unique karst landscapes of the Tsingy region.

Evening
Arrive at your accommodation near Tsingy de Bemaraha, settle in, and enjoy dinner while preparing for tomorrow's exploration of the "stone forest."

Day 11: Tsingy de Bemaraha National Park

Activities: Hiking, canyon exploration, rock forest walking safari

Full Day
Today is dedicated to exploring Tsingy de Bemaraha National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its extraordinary karst landscapes. The word "tsingy" comes from the Malagasy language and means "where one cannot walk barefoot," referring to the razor-sharp limestone formations.

Embark on a walking safari through the park's canyons and rock forests, traversing suspension bridges, climbing ladders, and using via ferrata systems to navigate the unique landscape. The park offers different hiking options depending on your fitness level and interests, from easier trails to more challenging routes.

Look for wildlife adapted to this unique environment, including Decken's Sifaka lemurs that navigate the sharp rocks with remarkable agility. The park is also home to various endemic plant species, birds, and reptiles that have evolved to thrive in the karst environment.

What to Expect: Challenging but rewarding hiking, spectacular geological formations, unique wildlife sightings, and unforgettable panoramic views from the limestone pinnacles.

Day 12: Tsingy de Bemaraha - Baobab Avenue - Morondava

Distance/Time: Approx. 6-7 hours drive including stops

Morning
Before departing the Tsingy area, take a traditional pirogue (dugout canoe) ride along the Manambolo River to visit stalagmite-stalactite caves. These limestone caves feature impressive formations created over thousands of years and provide a different perspective on the karst landscape.

Afternoon
Begin your journey back southwards, retracing your route through the remote landscapes of western Madagascar. The drive offers another opportunity to observe the changing vegetation and traditional village life as you travel from the Tsingy region toward the coast.

Evening Highlight
Arrive at the iconic Baobab Avenue in time for sunset, when the golden light creates magical photographic conditions. Watch as the ancient baobabs are silhouetted against the colorful sky, creating one of Madagascar's most iconic and photographed scenes. After sunset, continue to Morondava for overnight accommodation.

Day 13: Morondava - Antananarivo

Activities: City tour, market visit, domestic flight

Morning
Enjoy a city tour of Morondava, exploring this coastal town that serves as the capital of the Menabe region. Visit the local market to see the variety of goods including seafood, agricultural products, and handicrafts. Learn about Morondava's history as a trading port and its importance as a gateway to western Madagascar's natural attractions.

Afternoon
Transfer to Morondava Airport for your flight back to Antananarivo. The domestic flight returns you to Madagascar's capital, offering aerial views of the diverse landscapes you've traveled through over the past 13 days.

Evening
Upon arrival in Antananarivo, transfer to your hotel. Enjoy a farewell dinner reflecting on your incredible journey through Madagascar's diverse landscapes, from eastern rainforests and remote beaches to western dry forests and dramatic geological formations.

Day 14: Antananarivo - Departure

Activities: City tour, departure transfer

Morning
Depending on your flight schedule, enjoy a city tour of Antananarivo, visiting key landmarks that showcase Madagascar's history and culture. Options include the Rova of Antananarivo (Queen's Palace) which offers panoramic views over the city, the Andafiavaratra Palace, or the Tsimbazaza Zoo and Botanical Garden which features endemic species and exhibits about Madagascar's natural history.

You may also have time for last-minute souvenir shopping at the Marché Artisanal de La Digue, where you can find handicrafts, textiles, spices, and other mementos of your Madagascar adventure.

Afternoon
Transfer to Ivato International Airport for your flight homewards or continuation to another destination. Bid farewell to Madagascar with unforgettable memories of diverse landscapes, unique wildlife encounters, and rich cultural experiences from your 14-day journey through this remarkable island nation.
