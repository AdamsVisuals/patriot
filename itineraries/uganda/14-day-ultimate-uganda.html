14-Day Ultimate Uganda Primate & Wilderness Safari

Embark on an extraordinary 14-day comprehensive adventure through Uganda, the "Pearl of Africa," combining the once-in-a-lifetime experience of gorilla trekking with golden monkey tracking, chimpanzee encounters, Kidepo Valley wilderness, Murchison Falls, and spectacular wildlife viewing. This carefully curated luxury journey takes you from the urban energy of Kampala to the remote wilderness of Kidepo, the thundering Nile, misty forests of Kibale, Bwindi, and Mgahinga, and the savannah plains of Queen Elizabeth National Park.

Experience the thrill of coming face-to-face with endangered mountain gorillas in their natural habitat, track golden monkeys through volcanic forests, and observe wild chimpanzees in their dense rainforest home. Explore Uganda's wild frontier in Kidepo Valley National Park, witness the raw power of Murchison Falls, search for the famous tree-climbing lions of Ishasha, cruise the Kazinga Channel teeming with hippos and elephants, and relax at the stunning Lake Bunyonyi.

Our experienced guides will share their deep knowledge of Uganda's ecology, culture, and conservation efforts, ensuring a safe and enriching safari experience. With luxury lodge accommodations and expert logistical support including charter flights, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking wildlife encounters and stunning landscapes.

This comprehensive luxury safari is designed for discerning travelers seeking to experience all of Uganda's iconic wildlife and primates in one unforgettable journey. From gorilla encounters to golden monkey tracking, remote wilderness to thundering waterfalls, this itinerary offers the ultimate African primate safari experience.

Detailed 14-Day Safari Itinerary

Day 1: Welcome to Uganda – Arrival in Entebbe

Travel: 45-75 minutes from Entebbe Airport to your hotel.

Your adventure begins the moment you land at Entebbe International Airport. After a warm meet-and-greet, you'll be transferred to your accommodation. If you choose a lodge near the lake, enjoy serene views of Lake Victoria, the world's second-largest freshwater lake. Settle in and relax. In the early evening, your guide will provide a comprehensive briefing, covering trekking permits, what to pack, and what to expect from the days ahead.

Highlights: Warm Ugandan welcome, Lake Victoria views, comprehensive safari briefing.

Meals: Dinner (depending on your arrival time).

Accommodation Options

Optional Activity: If your flight arrives early, an optional city orientation tour or a visit to the Uganda Wildlife Education Centre can be arranged.

Day 2: To the Mighty Nile – Journey to Murchison Falls

Travel: ~6-8 hours by road or a scenic 1-1.5-hour charter flight.

Depart early, driving north through lush landscapes and traditional villages. You'll cross the Nile River at Karuma Bridge before entering Murchison Falls National Park. After checking into your lodge, the real adventure begins with an afternoon boat cruise to the base of the falls. Feel the mist on your face and hear the thunderous roar as you approach one of Africa's most powerful natural wonders.

Expected Sightings: Hippos in large pods, massive Nile Crocodiles basking on the banks, elephants, buffalo, and a dazzling array of water birds including the rare Shoebill stork (if you're lucky) and numerous fish eagles.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation Options

Day 3: Murchison Falls – Game Drives & The Top of the Falls

Travel: Game drives and a short hike within the park.

An early morning game drive on the northern bank of the Nile reveals the park's vast savannah. Later, take a guided hike to the Top of the Falls for a breathtaking, up-close view of the Nile's dramatic 7-meter-wide chasm. This is a photographer's dream.

Expected Sightings: Herds of elephant and buffalo, Jackson's hartebeest, Uganda kob, waterbuck, giraffe, and predators like lion or leopard. The birdlife is prolific.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Day 4: Into the Wild North – Charter to Kidepo Valley

Travel: A spectacular 2-3 hour charter flight.

Board a charter flight to the remote and rugged Kidepo Valley National Park, a hidden gem often described as Africa's most authentic wilderness. The aerial views are stunning. After arrival and lunch, embark on a short introductory game drive to acquaint yourself with this vast landscape.

Expected Sightings: Buffalo herds, ostrich, Jackson's hartebeest, brightly coloured birds.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Accommodation Options

Day 5: Kidepo Valley – Full Day Safari & Karamojong Culture

Travel: Full day of exploration within the park.

Spend the day exploring the Narus and Kidepo valleys. The park's isolation means incredible wildlife viewing with very few other vehicles. In the afternoon, enjoy an optional cultural visit to a local Karamojong community, where you'll learn about their ancient pastoral traditions, crafts, and way of life.

Expected Sightings: Large herds of buffalo, elephants, burchell's zebra, giraffe, and specialities like the cheetah and kudu. This is also a premier birding destination.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Accommodation Options

Day 6: To the Primate Capital – Fly to Kampala & Drive to Fort Portal

Travel: Charter flight to Kampala, then a ~4-5 hour drive west.

After your charter flight back to Kampala, you'll journey west through verdant tea plantations to the charming town of Fort Portal. In the evening, take a gentle Bigodi Swamp Walk, a community-run project that offers a fantastic introduction to the region's forest ecology.

Expected Sightings: Primates including black-and-white colobus and red-tailed monkeys, tropical birds, and diverse flora.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Accommodation Options

Day 7: Face to Face with Cousins – Chimpanzee Trekking in Kibale

Travel: Short drives; trekking can last 2-4 hours.

Today is for the chimpanzees! An early chimpanzee trekking experience in Kibale Forest National Park brings you into the heart of their habitat. Watch in awe as they feed, play, and swing through the canopy. You have a full hour with them once located. The afternoon Bigodi Swamp Walk (if not done the previous day) further enriches the experience.

Expected Sightings: Chimpanzees, red colobus monkeys, L'Hoest's monkeys, and incredible birdlife like the Great Blue Turaco.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Accommodation Options

Day 8: Rift Valley Scenery – To Queen Elizabeth National Park

Travel: ~3-4 hours through scenic landscapes.

A scenic drive takes you into the heart of the Albertine Rift Valley to Queen Elizabeth National Park. The park is famed for its diverse ecosystems, from savannah and wetlands to lush forests. After settling in, you may have time for an afternoon game drive or to relax with a view of the Rwenzori Mountains.

Expected Sightings: Uganda kob, waterbuck, warthog, buffalo, and elephants.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Accommodation Options

Day 9: A Wildlife Spectacle – Kazinga Channel Boat Cruise

Travel: A leisurely 2-hour boat cruise.

The Kazinga Channel boat cruise is a highlight of any Uganda safari. This natural channel connecting Lake George and Lake Edward attracts a phenomenal concentration of wildlife to its shores. It's an unrivaled opportunity for close-up photography and observing animal behavior.

Expected Sightings: Hundreds of hippos, Nile crocodiles, elephants bathing, buffalo, and an astounding variety of water birds including pelicans, kingfishers, and saddle-billed storks.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Accommodation Options

Day 10: Lions in the Trees & To the Impenetrable Forest

Travel: ~4-5 hours through changing landscapes.

Head south to the Ishasha sector of Queen Elizabeth Park, famous for its unique tree-climbing lions. Spotting these big cats lounging in the fig trees is a truly memorable sight. Afterwards, continue the journey, climbing into the highlands towards the misty Bwindi Impenetrable National Park.

Expected Sightings: Tree-climbing lions, topi, elephant, and buffalo.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Accommodation Options

Day 11: The Ultimate Encounter – Gorilla Trekking Day

Travel: Trekking time varies from 1-6 hours.

This is the day you've been waiting for. Your mountain gorilla trekking experience begins with a briefing before you set off into the dense, ancient forest with your guide and trackers. The trek can be challenging but is immensely rewarding. Once you find a gorilla family, you will spend a privileged one hour observing them—a truly humbling and magical experience.

Expected Sightings: A family of Mountain Gorillas, forest birds like the African Green Broadbill, and other primates.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Day 12: Bwindi – A Second Chance or a Deeper Dive

Travel: Variable, depending on the chosen activity.

You have options today:

  • Second Gorilla Trek: For an even deeper connection, opt for a second trek (subject to permit availability and an additional cost).
  • Guided Nature Walk: Explore a different part of Bwindi, learning about its unique ecology and medicinal plants.
  • Community & Cultural Visit: Engage with local communities, often including a moving cultural performance and interaction with the Batwa people, the original forest dwellers.

Expected Sightings: Depending on your choice: another gorilla family, rich forest biodiversity, or deep cultural insights.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Day 13: Golden Monkeys & Volcanic Vistas in Mgahinga

Travel: ~1-2 hours from Bwindi to Mgahinga.

Transfer to the smaller, volcanic Mgahinga Gorilla National Park. Here, you'll go golden monkey tracking, following these striking, agile primates through the bamboo forest. You can also opt for a hike up one of the Virunga volcanoes for spectacular cross-border views.

Expected Sightings: Troops of Golden Monkeys, stunning montane scenery, and possibly buffalo or elephant on the lower slopes.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Accommodation Options

Mountain Gahinga Lodge

Luxury • Volcanic views and luxury

Mid-Range • Beautiful lakeside location

Day 14: Farewell to the Pearl – Lake Bunyonyi & Departure to Kigali

Travel: Approx. 60–120 km to Kigali / 3 hrs approx

Relax by Lake Bunyonyi with canoeing and island visits, then begin your drive to Kigali in the afternoon with stops for crafts and photos. Arrive in the late noon or evening for onward flights.

Expected Sightings: Lake scenes, waterbirds, village life, and stunning terraced hillsides.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch.

End of Safari
