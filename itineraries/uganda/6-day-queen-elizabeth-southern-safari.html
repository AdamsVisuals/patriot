6-Day Uganda Gorilla & Wildlife Safari

Embark on an extraordinary 6-day adventure through Uganda, the "Pearl of Africa," combining the once-in-a-lifetime experience of gorilla trekking with spectacular wildlife viewing in Queen Elizabeth National Park. This carefully curated journey takes you from the urban energy of Kampala to the misty forests of Bwindi and the savannah plains of Queen Elizabeth.

Experience the thrill of coming face-to-face with endangered mountain gorillas in their natural habitat, an encounter that will leave you forever changed. Witness the incredible biodiversity of Uganda as you track chimpanzees, cruise the Kazinga Channel teeming with hippos and elephants, and search for tree-climbing lions on game drives.

Our experienced guides will share their deep knowledge of Uganda's ecology, culture, and conservation efforts, ensuring a safe and enriching safari experience. With comfortable lodge accommodations and expert logistical support, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking wildlife encounters and stunning landscapes.

This safari is designed for nature enthusiasts and adventure seekers looking to experience Africa's most iconic wildlife in one of the continent's most beautiful and biodiverse countries. From gorilla encounters to classic savannah wildlife, this itinerary offers an authentic African safari experience filled with unforgettable moments.

Detailed 6-Day Safari Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Entebbe / Kampala — Meet & Greet

Distance / Travel time: Entebbe Airport to Kampala approx. 40–50 km / Approx. 45–75 mins depending on traffic.

Welcome to the "Pearl of Africa!" Your guide will meet you at Entebbe International Airport, providing a warm welcome and a chilled bottle of water. You'll be transferred to your hotel in Kampala or a serene lakeside spot in Entebbe. After some time to rest and freshen up, a detailed pre-departure briefing will prepare you for the adventure ahead, covering everything from gorilla trekking etiquette to what to expect on game drives.

Expected Sightings & Highlights: Urban arrival; stunning views of Lake Victoria; vibrant local atmosphere.

Meals: Dinner (depending on arrival time).

Accommodation Options

Kampala Serena Hotel

Luxury • City center accommodation

Protea Hotel by Marriott

Mid-Range • Comfortable city stay

Optional Activities: A short city orientation tour or a visit to the Uganda Wildlife Education Centre if time permits.

Day 2: Kampala to Queen Elizabeth National Park

Distance / Travel time: Approx. 410 km / 6–7 hrs.

After an early breakfast, journey southwest as the cityscape gives way to rolling hills, lush banana plantations, and vibrant roadside markets. You'll cross the Equator – a perfect photo opportunity! Descend into the Albertine Rift Valley, arriving at Queen Elizabeth National Park in the early afternoon. Your first taste of African wildlife comes with an afternoon game drive on the Kasenyi Plains, known for its large herds of Uganda kob.

Expected Sightings & Highlights: Elephant, Buffalo, Uganda Kob, Waterbuck, Warthog. Scenic Equator crossing, panoramic Rift Valley views.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Accommodation Options

Mweya Safari Lodge

Luxury • Iconic peninsula location

Enganzi Lodge

Mid-Range • Scenic park views

Pro Tip: Keep your camera handy during the drive; the changing landscapes and local life provide fantastic photographic subjects.

Day 3: Queen Elizabeth — Game Drive & Kazinga Channel Cruise

Distance / Travel time: Intra-park driving; boat cruise ~2 hrs.

Awake before dawn for a magical morning game drive when predators are most active. Later, embark on the famous Kazinga Channel boat cruise. This natural channel connecting Lake George to Lake Edward boasts the highest concentration of hippos in Africa and offers an unparalleled opportunity to see elephants, buffalo, and colossal Nile crocodiles up close from the water.

Expected Sightings & Highlights: Lions, Hippos, Crocodiles, Elephants bathing, Buffalo, African Fish Eagle, Pelicans, Kingfishers.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Accommodation Options

Mweya Safari Lodge

Luxury • Iconic peninsula location

Enganzi Lodge

Mid-Range • Scenic park views

Pro Tip: The afternoon light on the Kazinga Channel is perfect for photography. Bring a telephoto lens and binoculars.

Day 4: Transfer to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

Distance / Travel time: Approx. 160–200 km / 3–4 hrs.

Enjoy a leisurely breakfast before bidding farewell to the savannah. The journey to Bwindi is incredibly scenic, climbing into the highlands past terraced farms and misty forests. This is the heart of the Albertine Rift, a biodiversity hotspot. You'll arrive at your lodge in the afternoon, with time to relax before your crucial gorilla trekking briefing.

Expected Sightings & Highlights: Stunning highland scenery, terraced farmland, Albertine Rift endemic bird species.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Accommodation Options

Mahogany Springs Lodge

Luxury • Buhoma sector

Bakiga Lodge

Mid-Range • Buhoma sector

Pro Tip: This is a good day to ensure your gorilla trekking gear (boots, rain jacket, daypack) is ready and easily accessible.

Day 5: Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi

Distance / Travel time: Trekking variable / 1–6 hrs.

This is the day you've been waiting for! After an early breakfast and transfer to the park headquarters, you'll be assigned to a gorilla family and receive a safety briefing. The trek itself is an adventure through dense, ancient forest. The moment you come face-to-face with a Mountain Gorilla family is truly life-changing. You will spend one privileged hour observing their fascinating interactions.

Expected Sightings & Highlights: Mountain Gorillas, Forest Elephants (rare), L'Hoest's Monkey, Giant Forest Hogs, unique flora.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Accommodation Options

Mahogany Springs Lodge

Luxury • Buhoma sector

Bakiga Lodge

Mid-Range • Buhoma sector

Pro Tip: Hire a local porter! They provide physical assistance and moral support, and you'll be contributing directly to the local community.

Day 6: Departure to Entebbe by Flight or Drive to Rwanda Kigali for Departure

Distance / Travel time: Flight to Entebbe: ~420 km / 1 hr. OR Drive to Kigali City: ~164 km / 4 hrs.

A relaxed morning breakfast then off to Kisoro for a flight back to Entebbe where you will connect with your flight back home. Or drive direct to Kigali city where you can also connect with your flight back home.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch.

End of Safari
