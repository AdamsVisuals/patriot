Experience gorilla trekking, chimpanzee tracking, Murchison Falls, tree-climbing lions, and the stunning landscapes of Uganda's national parks.

10 Days / 9 Nights | From $5,850

10-Day Ultimate Uganda Wildlife & Primate Safari

Embark on an extraordinary 10-day comprehensive adventure through Uganda, the "Pearl of Africa," combining the once-in-a-lifetime experience of gorilla trekking with chimpanzee tracking, Murchison Falls, and spectacular wildlife viewing. This carefully curated journey takes you from the urban energy of Kampala to the thundering Nile, misty forests of Kibale and Bwindi, and the savannah plains of Queen Elizabeth National Park.

Experience the thrill of coming face-to-face with endangered mountain gorillas in their natural habitat and track wild chimpanzees through dense rainforest. Witness the raw power of Murchison Falls, search for the famous tree-climbing lions of Ishasha, cruise the Kazinga Channel teeming with hippos and elephants, and relax at the stunning Lake Bunyonyi.

Our experienced guides will share their deep knowledge of Uganda's ecology, culture, and conservation efforts, ensuring a safe and enriching safari experience. With comfortable lodge accommodations and expert logistical support, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking wildlife encounters and stunning landscapes.

This comprehensive safari is designed for nature enthusiasts and adventure seekers looking to experience all of Uganda's iconic wildlife and primates in one unforgettable journey. From gorilla encounters to chimpanzee tracking, thundering waterfalls to serene lakes, this itinerary offers the ultimate African safari experience.

Detailed 10-Day Safari Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in the Pearl of Africa – Entebbe/Kampala

Distance / Travel time: Entebbe Airport to your hotel approx. 40–50 km / 45–75 minutes.

Your Ugandan adventure begins the moment you land at Entebbe International Airport (EBB). After clearing immigration and collecting your luggage, you will be warmly greeted by our representative who will facilitate your transfer to your preferred hotel. Take the afternoon to decompress from your travels, relax by the pool, or enjoy the views of Lake Victoria. In the early evening, you will receive a comprehensive briefing from your safari guide, covering the upcoming itinerary, gorilla trekking protocols, and answering any final questions.

Expected Sightings & Highlights: Urban scenery; panoramic views of Lake Victoria; a warm Ugandan welcome.

Meals: Dinner (included depending on your arrival time).

Optional Activities: A short city orientation tour or a visit to the Uganda Wildlife Education Centre (UWEC) on the way from the airport.

Day 2: Journey to the Thundering Nile – Murchison Falls National Park

Distance / Travel time: Road transfer approx. 330–370 km / 6-8 hours. Optional domestic flight: 1-1.5 hours.

After an early breakfast, we embark on our drive north-west. The journey itself is a cultural immersion, passing through bustling towns, lush rainforests, and vast sugarcane plantations. We stop for lunch en route. Upon entering Murchison Falls National Park, the landscape opens into classic savannah. Your first encounter with the Nile is an unforgettable afternoon boat cruise to the base of the falls. Here, you will feel the mist on your face and hear the thunderous roar as the world's longest river is forced through a narrow 7-meter gorge.

Expected Sightings & Highlights: Hippos, Nile Crocodiles, African Elephants, Cape Buffalo, African Fish Eagles, and other raptors. The sheer, raw power of Murchison Falls.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Day 3: Game Drives and A Bird's Eye View – Murchison Falls

Distance / Travel time: All activities are within the park; drives and hikes are short.

Rise early for a morning game drive on the northern bank of the Nile (Buligi Circuit). This area is renowned for its high concentration of wildlife, including the graceful Rothschild's giraffe. Keep your eyes peeled for lions, leopards, and a variety of antelope. After returning to the lodge for lunch and a siesta, we take a boat to the base of the falls and embark on a guided hike to the top. This challenging but rewarding walk offers different perspectives of the falls and culminates in a breathtaking panoramic view from the top, where you can witness the Nile's dramatic transformation.

Expected Sightings & Highlights: Rothschild's Giraffe, Lion, Elephant, Buffalo, Hartebeest, Waterbuck, Patas Monkey. The rare Shoebill Stork is sometimes spotted in the delta. The awe-inspiring "Top of the Falls" viewpoint.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Day 4: Scenic Transfer to the Foothills of the Rwenzoris – Fort Portal

Distance / Travel time: Road transfer approx. 320–400 km / 7-9 hours.

Today is a long but incredibly scenic travel day. We journey south, crossing the mighty Albert Nile and driving through the heart of rural Uganda. The landscape gradually transforms from savannah to verdant, rolling hills as we approach the "Mountains of the Moon," the Rwenzori Range. We arrive in the charming town of Fort Portal in the evening. If time allows, a short, guided walk in the nearby Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary is a perfect way to stretch your legs and spot some initial forest wildlife.

Expected Sightings & Highlights: Rural Ugandan life, changing landscapes, Black-and-White Colobus Monkeys, and vibrant birdlife in Bigodi.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Accommodation Options

Day 5: Chimpanzee Trekking & Transfer to the Savannah – Kibale to Queen Elizabeth

Distance / Travel time: Chimp trek: 2-4 hours; Road transfer to QENP: approx. 120–150 km / 3-4 hours.

This morning, we enter the dense Kibale National Park, a primate-lover's paradise. After a briefing at the park headquarters, experienced trackers will lead you through the forest in search of habituated chimpanzee families. The hour you spend observing these charismatic apes—their interactions, feeding, and play—is a truly exhilarating experience. After lunch, we drive south, descending into the Albertine Rift Valley to Queen Elizabeth National Park, where you will check into your lodge with time to relax before dinner.

Expected Sightings & Highlights: Wild Chimpanzees, Red Colobus Monkeys, Grey-cheeked Mangabeys, L'Hoest's Monkey. The diverse ecosystem of Kibale Tropical Forest.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Day 6: Wildlife & Waterways – Queen Elizabeth National Park

Distance / Travel time: Game drive: 2-3 hours; Boat cruise: 2 hours.

An early morning game drive on the Kasenyi Plains or the Queen's Mile offers fantastic opportunities to see wildlife at its most active. Search for lions, large herds of elephant, buffalo, and countless Uganda kob. In the afternoon, we embark on a classic safari experience: the Kazinga Channel boat cruise. This natural channel connecting Lakes George and Edward is a magnet for wildlife, and the boat gets you remarkably close to enormous hippo pods, bashing crocodiles, elephants bathing, and a spectacular array of waterbirds.

Expected Sightings & Highlights: Lions, Elephants, Buffalo, Hippos, Nile Crocodiles, Warthogs, Giant Kingfishers, African Skimmers, Pelicans.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Accommodation Options

Day 7: Tree-Climbing Lions & Into the Misty Highlands – To Bwindi

Distance / Travel time: Road transfer via Ishasha: approx. 200–260 km / 4-6 hours.

We depart early for the remote Ishasha sector of Queen Elizabeth National Park, famous for its unique population of tree-climbing lions. We will spend the morning searching for these magnificent cats, often seen lounging in the branches of large fig trees. After this thrilling game drive, we continue the journey south, climbing steadily into the highlands. The landscape transforms into a mosaic of terraced farms and misty valleys, signaling our arrival near the legendary Bwindi Impenetrable National Park. In the evening, you will receive a detailed briefing from your guide in preparation for tomorrow's gorilla trek.

Expected Sightings & Highlights: Tree-climbing Lions, Topi, Elephant, Buffalo. Dramatic and scenic landscape changes.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Day 8: The Ultimate Wildlife Encounter – Gorilla Trekking in Bwindi

Distance / Travel time: Trekking is variable and can last from 1 to 6 hours.

This is the day you've been waiting for. After an early breakfast and a short transfer to the park headquarters, you will be assigned to a gorilla family group and receive a final briefing from the park rangers. The trek itself is an adventure through the ancient, dense rainforest. Once the gorilla family is located, you will spend one magical and strictly enforced hour in their presence. Watching a Silverback lead his family, infants play, and mothers nurture their young is a profound, humbling, and life-changing experience that you will carry with you forever.

Expected Sightings & Highlights: Mountain Gorillas, Forest Elephants (rare), various forest monkeys and duikers, and rich birdlife including Albertine Rift endemics.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Accommodation Options

Pro Tip: Hiring a local porter is highly recommended. They assist with your daypack, provide a helpful push or pull on steep trails, and provide direct employment to the local community.

Day 9: Journey to the Lake of a Thousand Islands – Lake Bunyonyi

Distance / Travel time: Bwindi to Lake Bunyonyi: approx. 30–60 km / 1-2 hours.

After the intensity of the gorilla trek, today is dedicated to relaxation and reflection. Enjoy a leisurely breakfast before a short, scenic drive to Lake Bunyonyi, "The Place of Many Little Birds." This breathtakingly beautiful crater lake is one of the deepest in Africa and dotted with countless islands. Check into your lodge perched on the hillsides and spend the afternoon soaking in the serene atmosphere. You can take a traditional dugout canoe trip to explore the islands, go for a swim in the bilharzia-free waters, or simply relax and enjoy the stunning panoramic views from your lodge.

Expected Sightings & Highlights: Serene lake scenery, abundant waterbirds, local community life on the islands, and stunning sunset views over the terraced hillsides.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner.

Day 10: Journey's End – Departure via Kigali or Entebbe

Option A: Transfer to Kigali, Rwanda (Highly Recommended)

Distance / Travel time: Lake Bunyonyi to Kigali City/Kigali International Airport (KGL): approx. 120 km / 3 hours.

After a final breakfast overlooking the tranquil lake, we begin a short and scenic drive to the Rwandan border. After completing border formalities, we continue to Kigali. You will be dropped off at a hotel of your choice in the city or directly at Kigali International Airport (KGL) for your onward evening flight. This option is by far the most efficient and saves a full day of travel compared to driving back to Entebbe.

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch.
