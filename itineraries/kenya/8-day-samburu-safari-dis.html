Venture into Kenya’s diverse and untamed landscapes.

8 Days / 7 Nights | From $6,180

8-Day Kenya Northern Circuit & Masai Mara Safari

Embark on an extraordinary 8-day journey through Kenya's most diverse wildlife destinations, from the arid northern wilderness of Samburu to the iconic plains of the Masai Mara. This carefully curated safari offers an immersive experience across contrasting ecosystems, where unique wildlife encounters and breathtaking landscapes create unforgettable memories.

Experience the raw beauty of Kenya's northern frontier as you track the legendary "Special Five" in Samburu, witness pioneering conservation efforts in Ol Pejeta, explore the flamingo-filled waters of Lake Nakuru or the tranquil beauty of Lake Naivasha, and finally immerse yourself in the world-renowned Masai Mara. Each destination offers distinct wildlife viewing and photographic opportunities.

Our expert guides will share their deep knowledge of each ecosystem and wildlife behaviors, ensuring you gain profound insights into Kenya's natural wonders. With comfortable accommodations ranging from cozy lodges to ultra-luxury camps, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking safari experience across diverse landscapes.

This comprehensive journey is designed for those seeking an authentic African adventure that combines northern Kenya's unique wildlife with the classic savannah experience of the Masai Mara. Whether you're a first-time visitor or a seasoned safari enthusiast, this itinerary offers the perfect blend of wildlife diversity, conservation experiences, and scenic beauty.

Detailed 8-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Nairobi → Samburu

Distance/Time: ~320 km | 6–7 hrs via Nanyuki/Isiolo

The adventure begins as Nairobi's urban rhythm fades into the open northern wilderness. Upon arrival, the Ewaso Nyiro River glistens in the late afternoon sun, greeting you to the arid splendor of Samburu. Embark on your first game drive to encounter the legendary "Special Five": reticulated giraffe, Grevy's zebra, beisa oryx, gerenuk, and Somali ostrich. Predators move stealthily along dry riverbeds, a subtle thrill amidst the tranquility. As the sun sets, painting the sky in shades of amber and violet, you settle into your lodge, enveloped by the northern wilderness.

Day 2: Full Day in Samburu

Explore Samburu National Reserve and Buffalo Springs National Reserve

At dawn, the dryland plains awaken. Today, immerse yourself in the arid ecosystem, where luggas hide leopards and lions. Elephants traverse the plains with quiet authority, dik-dik flit among shrubs, and dryland birds enliven the skies. Scenic viewpoints offer sweeping panoramas, fully capturing Samburu's stark and elegant beauty. Evening brings a pastel-hued sky, a serene prelude to nightfall.

Day 3: Samburu → Ol Pejeta

Distance/Time: ~150 km | 3–4 hrs

Depart through rolling hills and acacia-studded landscapes to Ol Pejeta, a pioneering conservation area. Afternoon brings a visit to the Sweet waters Chimpanzee Sanctuary, a heartwarming refuge for rescued chimps. Your PM game drive presents encounter with black and white rhinos and elusive predators. Optional night drives allow glimpses of nocturnal species such as aardwolves, bat-eared foxes, and genets.

Day 4: Ol Pejeta → Lake Nakuru / Lake Naivasha

Distance/Time: Ol Pejeta → Nakuru ~180 km | 4–5 hrs; → Naivasha ~200 km | 4–5.5 hrs

Begin with a final AM drive in Ol Pejeta for last rhino sightings. Continue to the Rift Valley lakes. At Nakuru, enjoy a PM game drive focusing on rhinos, lions, and giraffes. At Naivasha, glide through the serene waters on a boat safari, spotting hippos and fish eagles, or take a gentle walk on Crescent Island amidst freely roaming wildlife.

Full-Day Safari:

Depending on your base:

• Naivasha: Boat safari and Crescent Island Walk; optional Hell's Gate cycling or hiking.

This day blends gentle exploration with serene reflection, preparing you for the adventure-filled plains of the Masai Mara.

Day 6: Rift Valley → Masai Mara

Distance/Time: Naivasha → Masai Mara ~240 km | 4.5–6 hrs

Depart early for the iconic Masai Mara. Arriving in the golden afternoon light, head out on a sunset game drive across the plains. Observe elephants, giraffes, and lions as they awaken to the twilight rhythm. The vast savannah creates an immersive backdrop for your safari photography and storytelling.

Day 7: Full Day in Masai Mara

Full-Day Safari:

Wake to the Mara in all its glory. Optional dawn balloon rides offer panoramic views of endless plains. AM and PM game drives provide intimate encounters with lions, cheetahs, hippos, and crocodiles. From July to October, the plains may host the awe-inspiring Great Migration. Each moment here is vivid, immersive, and unforgettable.

Day 8: Masai Mara → Nairobi – Departure

Distance/Time: Drive 5–6 hrs or Fly 45 min

Your final sunrise drive captures the Mara's golden light, with elephants moving gracefully across the plains. After breakfast, return to Nairobi by road or air for your onward journey. Carry with you the memories of Africa's untamed beauty, serene lakes, dramatic plains, and majestic northern wilderness.
