7-Day Nairobi, Amboseli, Lake Naivasha & Maasai Mara Luxury Safari

Embark on an unforgettable 7-day luxury safari journey through Kenya's most spectacular destinations, combining the majestic backdrop of Mount Kilimanjaro in Amboseli with the dramatic landscapes of Hell's Gate National Park and the world-renowned wildlife spectacle of the Maasai Mara. This carefully curated itinerary offers a comprehensive experience of Kenya's diverse ecosystems and iconic landmarks.

Begin your adventure with a comfortable overnight in Nairobi before journeying to Amboseli National Park, famous for its large elephant herds and breathtaking views of Africa's highest peak. Continue to Lake Naivasha and Hell's Gate National Park, where you can walk and cycle among wildlife, before concluding in the legendary Maasai Mara, home to the Great Migration and abundant Big Five sightings.

Our expert guides will provide deep insights into each unique ecosystem, ensuring you gain profound understanding of Kenya's wildlife and landscapes while enjoying unparalleled game viewing opportunities. With luxurious accommodations at premier lodges including Crowne Plaza Airport Hotel, Amboseli Serena Safari Lodge, Lake Naivasha Sopa Lodge, and Mara Serena Safari Lodge, you'll experience the perfect blend of wilderness adventure and refined comfort.

This comprehensive journey is ideal for wildlife enthusiasts seeking diverse landscapes, photography lovers, and those wanting to experience Kenya's most celebrated national parks in one unforgettable luxury safari experience.

Detailed 7-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Nairobi

Arrival & Relaxation

Afternoon/Evening
Upon your arrival at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport, a warm welcome awaits you as our friendly representative will be there to greet you with a smile and escort you to the Crowne Plaza Airport Hotel. This splendid hotel, known for its comfort and hospitality, offers an inviting atmosphere where you can unwind and recharge.

Leisure Time
The rest of your day is yours to enjoy as you settle into your cozy surroundings, take in the delightful amenities, and perhaps indulge in a refreshing drink or a delicious meal. This time is perfect for relaxation and preparation as you eagerly anticipate the thrilling safari adventure that awaits you the following day!

Whether it's lounging by the pool, enjoying a soothing spa treatment, or simply soaking in the serene ambiance of the hotel, each moment spent here will help build excitement for the unforgettable experiences ahead!

Activities: Airport transfer, hotel check-in, relaxation

What to Expect: Comfortable accommodation, excellent amenities, and preparation for your safari adventure.

Day 2: Nairobi to Amboseli National Park

Distance/Time: ~240 km | Approx. 4-5 hours drive

Morning
Your dedicated driver guide will meet you at your hotel early in the morning. After a warm introduction and comprehensive briefing about the upcoming safari adventure, we'll embark on a scenic journey to the renowned Amboseli National Park.

Afternoon
Upon arrival, enter the park and enjoy an exciting game drive en route to your accommodation within the heart of the park. On arrival at the Amboseli Serena Lodge, you'll check in and have a delightful lunch. After lunch, take some time for a brief rest before setting out on an exhilarating afternoon game drive.

Evening
During your game drive, you'll encounter awe-inspiring sights such as elegant wildebeests and zebras, regal lions, cunning hyenas, graceful antelopes, and magnificent elephants—all set against the breathtaking backdrop of Mount Kilimanjaro's majestic peaks.

As evening falls, return to your lodge where a sumptuous dinner awaits you. After dinner, unwind by gathering around a cozy bonfire with refreshing drinks or retire comfortably to your room for a restful night's sleep, ensuring you're rejuvenated for another day of exploration ahead.

Activities: Scenic drive to Amboseli, afternoon game drive

What to Expect: First views of Mount Kilimanjaro, elephant herds, and diverse wildlife in Amboseli's unique ecosystem.

Day 3: Amboseli National Park

Full Day Exploration

Morning
If the weather graces us with clear skies, awaken to a breathtaking view of Mt. Kilimanjaro, Africa's majestic crown jewel. Begin your day with an invigorating early morning game drive, the perfect time to witness predators in action amidst the cool dawn air.

Midday
After this thrilling experience, return for a hearty breakfast at the lodge. After breakfast you can relax and enjoy luxurious amenities such as our inviting swimming pool. Enjoy lunch at the lodge while taking in the spectacular views of the park and Mount Kilimanjaro.

Afternoon
After lunch, embark on more exhilarating game drives through the park, offering you an unparalleled opportunity to observe wildlife and magnificent elephant herds in their natural habitat up close. Don't miss out on visiting our observation point—a perch offering stunning panoramic views over the wildlife-rich expanse below that remains vibrant throughout the year.

Evening
As evening descends, return to the lodge for a delightful dinner followed by relaxation around a warm bonfire beneath African stars or retreat into your cozy room for a restful night's sleep.

Activities: Morning and afternoon game drives, lodge relaxation

What to Expect: Optimal Kilimanjaro views, elephant sightings, and diverse wildlife photography opportunities.

Day 4: Amboseli to Lake Naivasha & Hell's Gate National Park

Distance/Time: ~280 km | Approx. 5-6 hours drive

Morning
Commence your day with an early breakfast in Amboseli before embarking on a scenic drive to the enchanting Lake Naivasha. En route, make a brief stop in Mai Mahiu to savor the breathtaking vistas at the Great Rift Valley viewpoint—a true feast for the eyes.

Afternoon
Arrive at Lake Naivasha just in time for a delightful lunch at Lake Naivasha Sopa Lodge, followed by some well-deserved relaxation. Once rejuvenated, prepare for an exhilarating visit to Hell's Gate National Park.

Evening
Experience nature's wonders up close with a guided walk amidst diverse wildlife, where graceful giraffes and majestic buffalo roam freely across the verdant plains. For those seeking adventure, cycling through this stunning landscape is available at an additional charge.

Should time allow further exploration, consider indulging in serene boat rides on Lake Naivasha—an optional treat that promises unforgettable views from tranquil waters. Round off your eventful day by returning to your lodge come evening; reflecting upon memorable encounters crafted throughout this remarkable journey.

Activities: Scenic drive, Great Rift Valley viewpoint, Hell's Gate National Park walking safari

What to Expect: Dramatic Rift Valley landscapes, walking among wildlife, and optional lake activities.

Day 5: Lake Naivasha to Maasai Mara Game Reserve

Distance/Time: ~180 km | Approx. 4-5 hours drive

Morning
Following a delightful breakfast, you'll depart from Lake Naivasha and embark on a scenic drive to the renowned Masai Mara National Reserve. The journey takes you through picturesque landscapes and Maasai homesteads.

Afternoon
Your arrival will be perfectly timed for a sumptuous lunch at Mara Serena Safari Lodge. Afterwards, indulge in some leisure time before setting off on an exhilarating afternoon game drive.

Evening
This iconic reserve is home to the illustrious Big 5 and other remarkable creatures such as cheetahs, servals, hyenas, bat-eared foxes, black-backed and side-striped jackals, hippos, crocodiles, baboons, warthogs, topi, elands among others. Additionally, bird enthusiasts will find paradise here with sightings of eagles; vultures; ostriches; storks; little grebe; pelicans; cormorants & darters.

As dusk gently descends upon the savannah horizons you'll make your way back to your lodge for dinner. Post-dinner options include unwinding around a cozy bonfire with refreshing beverages or retreating into your private quarters while being serenaded by nature's symphony of nocturnal sounds from the African bushveld—a truly enchanting end to an adventurous day!

Activities: Scenic drive to Maasai Mara, afternoon game drive

What to Expect: First impressions of the vast Mara ecosystem, abundant wildlife sightings, and spectacular sunset views.

Day 6: Full Day Maasai Mara Game Reserve

Full Day Exploration

Morning
Begin your day with a hearty breakfast before departing from the lodge, equipped with delicious packed lunches for an exhilarating full-day exploration in Africa's premier wildlife sanctuary. For those seeking an extraordinary experience, an early morning balloon safari can be arranged at an extra cost.

Midday
Your journey will cover various iconic destinations within the vast Masai Mara National Reserve. Around midday, pause for a rejuvenating lunch beneath the shade of a majestic acacia tree at a scenic spot within the reserve.

Afternoon
After lunch, continue your thrilling game drives, soaking in the rich tapestry of flora and fauna this renowned park offers. A visit to a Maasai village can be organized at an additional cost, providing cultural insights into the local Maasai community.

Evening
As evening descends, return to your lodge. After indulging in a delectable dinner, you may choose to relax beside a warm and inviting bonfire, sharing stories of the day's adventures, or retire to your comfortable room for a peaceful night's rest.

Activities: Full day game drives in Maasai Mara, optional cultural visit

What to Expect: Abundant wildlife sightings, potential Great Migration viewing, and spectacular African landscapes.

Day 7: Departure

Distance/Time: ~270 km | Approx. 5-6 hours drive to Nairobi

Morning
As your exhilarating safari adventure concludes today, you will enjoy a delightful breakfast before embarking on a scenic drive back to Nairobi. The journey provides final opportunities to spot wildlife as you exit the reserve and scenic views of the Kenyan countryside.

Afternoon
Upon arrival in Nairobi, you'll be treated to an exquisite lunch at the renowned Carnivore restaurant, known for its exceptional culinary experience and mouth-watering array of meats.

Departure
Afterward, we will ensure your seamless transition either to your city hotel or directly to Jomo Kenyatta International Airport according to your departure schedule, filled with incredible memories of your 7-day luxury safari through Kenya's most spectacular wildlife destinations.

Activities: Scenic return journey, lunch at Carnivore Restaurant

What to Expect: Final wildlife sightings and a memorable dining experience at one of Africa's most famous restaurants.
