9 Day Amboseli Safari

Explore Amboseli’s elephant herds and Samburu’s unique wildlife

9 Days / 8 Nights | From $7,080

9-Day Kenya Classic Safari Circuit

Embark on an extraordinary 9-day journey through Kenya's most iconic wildlife destinations, from the Kilimanjaro-framed plains of Amboseli to the arid wilderness of Samburu, the legendary Masai Mara, and the tranquil waters of Lake Naivasha. This carefully curated safari offers an immersive experience across Kenya's most diverse ecosystems, creating unforgettable memories at every turn.

Experience the breathtaking contrast of landscapes as you witness elephants against Mount Kilimanjaro's backdrop, track the legendary "Special Five" in Samburu's northern frontier, immerse yourself in the predator-rich plains of the Masai Mara, and unwind on the serene waters of Lake Naivasha. Each destination offers unique wildlife encounters and photographic opportunities.

Our expert guides will share their deep knowledge of each ecosystem and wildlife behaviors, ensuring you gain profound insights into Kenya's natural wonders. With comfortable accommodations ranging from cozy lodges to ultra-luxury camps, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking safari experience across diverse landscapes.

This comprehensive journey is designed for those seeking the ultimate Kenyan safari experience, combining classic destinations with northern frontier adventures. Whether you're a first-time visitor or a seasoned safari enthusiast, this itinerary offers the perfect blend of wildlife diversity, scenic beauty, and cultural immersion.

Detailed 9-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Nairobi → Amboseli

Distance/Time: ~220 km | 4–5 hrs via Emali (road)

Leave Nairobi behind and journey into the expansive plains of Amboseli, framed by the towering presence of Kilimanjaro. Arrive in time for a late afternoon marsh circuit game drive, witnessing elephants meandering through wetlands, hippos lounging in pools, and vibrant waterbirds populating the skies. The setting sun bathes the savannah in amber and violet, providing the perfect backdrop for your first safari photographs.

Day 2: Amboseli – Full Day

Dawn light spills over Kilimanjaro as you explore Amboseli's wetlands and plains. Enjoy morning and afternoon game drives, tracking elephants and other wildlife, or climb Observation Hill for sweeping panoramas. Dust-bathing elephants, intimate herd interactions, and abundant birdlife create a sensory feast. Capture the ethereal light reflecting off marshes as the day unfolds.

Day 3: Amboseli → Samburu

Flight: Amboseli → Nairobi → Samburu ~2–3 hrs total
Road Option: Amboseli → Samburu ~530 km | 9–10 hrs (long overland day)

For comfort and efficiency, flights via Nairobi are recommended. Descend into Samburu's arid northern wilderness, where the Ewaso Nyiro River winds gracefully through ochre plains. Embark on a riverside game drive to meet the legendary 'Special Five'—reticulated giraffe, Grevy's zebra, beisa oryx, gerenuk, and Somali ostrich. Lions and leopards move stealthily along the luggas, while the sunset paints the sky in fiery hues for an unforgettable first evening.

Day 4: Samburu – Full Day

Immerse yourself fully in Samburu's unique ecosystem, exploring both Samburu and Buffalo Springs National Reserves. Observe predators along dry riverbeds, elephants wading through the river, and dik-dik flitting among acacias. Birdsong punctuates the stillness as the sun rises over a landscape of muted ochres and greens. Evening brings soft pastel skies, the perfect counterpoint to the day's vivid wildlife encounters.

Day 5: Samburu → Masai Mara

Flight: Samburu → Nairobi → Masai Mara ~2–3 hrs total
Road Option: Samburu → Masai Mara ~500 km | 9–10 hrs (overland)

Descend into the golden plains of the Masai Mara. Afternoon is dedicated to a sunset game drive as herds of elephants, giraffes, and antelope wander the Mara. Lions stir along the riverbanks, and the vast, undulating savannah stretches to the horizon, creating a cinematic tableau that marks your first encounter with the Mara's magic.

Day 6: Masai Mara – Full Day

Dawn over the Mara is golden and exhilarating. Spend a full day exploring the diverse sectors of the reserve, with options for AM/PM drives or a full-day excursion with picnic. Optional hot-air balloon safari at sunrise offers panoramic vistas over sprawling plains and the winding Mara River. Track big cats, hippos, crocodiles, and marvel at the Mara's biodiversity in every glance. Cultural visits to Maasai villages enrich the experience.

Day 7: Masai Mara – AM & PM Drives

A more relaxed day allows you to focus on species you may have missed, such as leopards, and to linger near lion prides. The Mara's rhythm—the whisper of the wind, the distant call of raptors, the silent elegance of stalking predators—creates moments of profound connection to the wild.

Day 8: Masai Mara → Lake Naivasha

Distance/Time: ~240 km | 4.5–6 hrs (road)

A scenic drive south brings you to Lake Naivasha, where tranquility replaces the Mara's drama. Afternoon boat safari allows close-up encounters with hippos and myriad waterbirds. Crescent Island invites gentle walking amidst freely roaming antelopes and exotic birds. Optional cycling or lakeshore strolls offer reflective moments, while the lake shimmers under soft evening light.

Day 9: Lake Naivasha → Nairobi – Departure

Distance/Time: ~95 km | 1.5–2 hrs

Enjoy a leisurely morning on the lake with optional boat rides or walks. Transfer back to Nairobi for your departure, carrying memories of Africa's majestic contrasts—from Kilimanjaro's snowy peaks and Samburu's arid elegance, to the Mara's predator-rich plains and Naivasha's serene waters. Each moment a vivid brushstroke in your safari story.
