Experience diverse landscapes, rich cultures, and unforgettable wildlife encounter

15 Days / 14 Nights | From $9,200

15-Day Kenya Ultimate Safari Experience

Embark on an extraordinary 15-day journey through Kenya's most iconic wildlife destinations, from the rugged northern frontier of Samburu to the conservation haven of Ol Pejeta, the flamingo-filled shores of Lake Nakuru, the tranquil waters of Lake Naivasha, the legendary Masai Mara, the Kilimanjaro-framed plains of Amboseli, and the vast wilderness of Tsavo. This comprehensive safari offers an immersive experience across Kenya's most diverse ecosystems, creating unforgettable memories at every turn.

Experience the breathtaking contrast of landscapes as you witness the "Special Five" in Samburu, encounter rare rhinos in Ol Pejeta, marvel at pink flamingo flocks, enjoy serene boat safaris, immerse yourself in predator-rich plains, capture elephants against Kilimanjaro's majestic backdrop, and explore Kenya's largest national park in Tsavo.

Our expert guides will share their deep knowledge of each ecosystem and wildlife behaviors, ensuring you gain profound insights into Kenya's natural wonders. With comfortable accommodations ranging from cozy lodges to ultra-luxury camps, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking safari experience across diverse landscapes.

This ultimate journey is designed for those seeking the complete Kenyan safari experience, combining northern frontier adventures with classic southern destinations and the raw wilderness of Tsavo. Whether you're a first-time visitor or a seasoned safari enthusiast, this itinerary offers the perfect blend of wildlife diversity, scenic beauty, and cultural immersion.

Detailed 15-Day Itinerary

Days 1–2: Nairobi → Samburu – The Call of the Northern Frontier

Distance/Time: ~320 km | 6–7 hrs

Your journey begins northward through fertile farmlands, rolling coffee estates, and the rugged rise of Mount Kenya before the land opens into the untamed beauty of Samburu. The air is warmer, drier, and scented with wild sage and dust.

Here, the Ewaso Nyiro River slices through an arid wilderness of acacia woodland and red-earth hills. On your first afternoon, unwind and let the landscape work its quiet magic. The next day, set out for a full exploration of Samburu and Buffalo Springs, home to the rare "Special Five": the Beisa oryx, Grevy's zebra, Somali ostrich, gerenuk, and reticulated giraffe.

Watch elephants gathering at the riverbanks, leopards slinking between granite outcrops, and the sun melting into an amber horizon — the true rhythm of Africa.

Day 3: Samburu → Ol Pejeta – Conservation at Its Heart

Distance/Time: ~150 km | 3–4 hrs

After breakfast, descend toward the green foothills of Mount Kenya, where the high-altitude plains of Ol Pejeta Conservancy await. This world-renowned reserve blends thrilling wildlife experiences with meaningful conservation efforts.

In the afternoon, visit the Sweetwaters Chimpanzee Sanctuary, the only place in Kenya to see these intelligent primates, and continue on a game drive in search of the Big Five including black and white rhinos.

As night falls, an optional night game drive invites you to witness a completely different world: elusive leopards on the move, civets darting through the grass, and the eerie laughter of hyenas echoing across the darkened plains.

Day 4: Ol Pejeta → Lake Nakuru – The Valley of Light

Distance/Time: ~180 km | 4–5 hrs

Travel southwest into the Great Rift Valley a breathtaking descent through forested escarpments and open plains until you reach Lake Nakuru, shimmering like liquid silver under the African sun.

This afternoon, set out on a lakeside game drive where both black and white rhinos roam freely. The shores are alive with pelicans, cormorants, and flocks of pink flamingos when the conditions are right.

In the acacia woodlands, lions may be seen resting in the tall grass while colobus monkeys leap gracefully among the branches. The lake's still surface mirrors the sky, creating the perfect canvas for photographers as dusk paints the horizon in strokes of violet and gold.

Day 5: Lake Nakuru → Lake Naivasha – Reflections of Tranquility

Distance/Time: ~80 km | 1.5–2 hrs

A short morning drive brings you to the tranquil Lake Naivasha, a freshwater haven surrounded by acacia forests and volcanic escarpments. The atmosphere here is serene a gentle pause between the thrill of game drives.

Glide across the lake on a boat safari, watching pods of hippos laze in the shallows while African fish eagles circle overhead, their haunting cries echoing across the water. Later, take a guided walk on Crescent Island, a private sanctuary where zebras, giraffes, and antelope roam freely around you.

For the active traveler, an optional bicycle ride through Hell's Gate National Park offers a rare chance to cycle among wildlife through dramatic gorges and steaming vents — nature in its raw, unfiltered form.

Days 6–8: Naivasha → Masai Mara – Africa's Beating Heart

Distance/Time: ~240 km | 4.5–6 hrs by road
Optional Flight: Naivasha → Masai Mara (~1 hr)

Journey southwest through the Great Rift Valley to the legendary Masai Mara, where golden savannas stretch endlessly beneath vast African skies.

Your arrival feels cinematic herds of wildebeest dotting the plains, elephants moving in silent processions, and perhaps a pride of lions basking in the afternoon light.

Spend the next two days immersed in this wildlife paradise, exploring the Mara's diverse landscapes from the Olare Motorogi Conservancy to the Talek and Mara Rivers. Witness the dance of predators and prey, the elegant stride of cheetahs, and, if timed with the migration, the thundering spectacle of wildebeest crossings.

Enhance your stay with a sunrise hot-air balloon safari — a breathtaking flight over the plains followed by a champagne breakfast in the wild. Each evening, return to your campfire as stars flood the sky, and the night hums with distant roars and the heartbeat of Africa.

Days 9–11: Masai Mara → Amboseli – Under the Gaze of Kilimanjaro

Option 1 – Flight: via Nairobi (~2–3 hrs total)
Option 2 – Road: ~467 km | 7–9 hrs via Narok & Emali

Fly from the Mara to Amboseli National Park, where the horizon is dominated by the majestic peak of Mount Kilimanjaro. Few sights on Earth compare to watching herds of elephants crossing the plains beneath Africa's tallest mountain a timeless, soul-stirring tableau.

Spend your afternoons exploring Amboseli's mosaic of habitats marshes shimmering with birdlife, open grasslands alive with giraffes and zebra, and acacia woodlands where lions rest in the shade.

On your final morning here, savor the stillness as the first light paints Kilimanjaro in pink and gold a moment of pure serenity and awe.

Days 12–14: Amboseli → Tsavo West & East – The Red Heart of Kenya

Distance/Time: Amboseli → Tsavo West ~150 km | 3–4 hrs; Tsavo West ↔ Tsavo East ~3–4 hrs

From the shadow of Kilimanjaro, continue to the vast, primal wilderness of Tsavo, Kenya's largest and oldest national park a place of legend and immense scale. In Tsavo West, explore the magical Mzima Springs, where hippos and crocodiles drift in clear waters fed by underground rivers. Wander across the haunting landscape of the Shetani Lava Flow, a frozen sea of black volcanic rock formed by ancient eruptions.

Then journey eastward to the sun-drenched plains of Tsavo East, famed for its red-dust elephants and wide-open horizons. The Aruba Dam and Galana River draw wildlife in abundance from stately giraffes and buffalo to the stealthy cats of the savanna. Your days here unfold slowly, filled with quiet wonder and the sense of being truly off the map.

Day 15: Tsavo → Nairobi – The Journey Home

Distance/Time: ~330 km | 6–7 hrs by road
Optional Flight: Tsavo → Nairobi (~1 hr)

After breakfast, enjoy a relaxed drive back to Nairobi, retracing your journey through Kenya's ever-changing landscapes from red-earth wilderness to fertile farmlands and the city skyline beyond.

As you arrive, one truth remains clear: you have not just visited Kenya, you've felt it. From the dust on your boots to the rhythm in your heart, this is a safari that stays with you long after you've left the savanna behind.
