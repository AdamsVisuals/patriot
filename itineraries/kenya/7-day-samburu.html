7-Day Samburu, Ol Pejeta, Lake Nakuru & Maasai Mara Luxury Safari

Embark on an extraordinary 7-day luxury safari journey through Kenya's most diverse wildlife destinations, combining the unique northern species of Samburu National Reserve with groundbreaking conservation at Ol Pejeta, the flamingo-filled shores of Lake Nakuru, and the world-renowned wildlife spectacle of the Maasai Mara. This carefully curated itinerary offers a comprehensive experience of Kenya's varied ecosystems and conservation successes.

Begin your adventure in Samburu National Reserve, home to the "Samburu Special Five" and located north of the equator. Continue to Ol Pejeta Conservancy to witness pioneering conservation efforts including the last two northern white rhinos, then explore Lake Nakuru's famous pink flamingo shores before concluding in the legendary Maasai Mara, home to the Great Migration and abundant Big Five sightings.

Our expert guides will provide deep insights into each unique ecosystem and conservation initiative, ensuring you gain profound understanding of Kenya's wildlife protection efforts while enjoying unparalleled game viewing opportunities. With luxurious accommodations at premier lodges including Sarova Shaba Game Lodge, Serena Sweetwaters Tented Camp, Sarova Lion Hill Lodge, and Mara Serena Safari Lodge, you'll experience the perfect blend of wilderness adventure and refined comfort.

This comprehensive journey is ideal for wildlife enthusiasts seeking diverse species, conservation advocates, and those wanting to experience Kenya's most celebrated national parks and conservancies in one unforgettable luxury safari experience.

Detailed 7-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Nairobi to Samburu National Reserve

Distance/Time: ~350 km | Approx. 5-6 hours drive

Morning
You will start your safari in Nairobi and take a scenic drive to Samburu National Park, passing through lush tea, coffee and sisal plantations. Don't forget to stop and admire the majestic Mount Kenya with its snow-capped peaks!

Afternoon
When you arrive at Samburu National Reserve, get ready for an unforgettable experience. It is home to some of the most unique wildlife found anywhere on Earth! Plus, thanks to the Ewaso Nyiro River running nearby, you'll find plenty of shady trees and water sources that attract these amazing animals.

You will be crossing into the Northern Hemisphere as you enter Samburu. This is sure to be a memorable moment too! After lunch, you'll set off on your afternoon game drives inside this pristine wonderland.

Evening
When evening arrives, you'll return back to the lodge for dinner and a good night's rest at Sarova Shaba Game Lodge, beautifully situated along the Ewaso Nyiro River.

Activities: Scenic drive to Samburu, afternoon game drive, equator crossing experience

What to Expect: Introduction to northern Kenya's unique ecosystem, first sightings of Samburu specials, and comfortable riverside accommodation.

Accommodation

Day 2: Samburu National Reserve

Full Day Exploration

Morning
After breakfast, you'll be ready to explore all the wonders of Samburu National Reserve, a rugged and striking landscape fed by the gorgeous Ewaso Nyiro River. This special area north of the equator is home to some unique wildlife like oryx, gerenuk, Somali ostrich, Gravy's zebra and reticulated giraffe. Make sure to bring out your inner nature photographer!

Midday
You will take a break for lunch and a little rest once the sun's cooled off before heading back out again for more game drives.

Afternoon
Your afternoon exploration continues along the riverbanks and acacia woodlands, searching for the elusive leopard and observing elephant families coming to drink. The unique geology and vegetation of Samburu create perfect photographic opportunities throughout the day.

Evening
Once evening approaches, you'll return to cozy up at your lodge with dinner and sweet dreams until the morning comes around again, listening to the sounds of the African night from your comfortable accommodation.

Activities: Morning and afternoon game drives in Samburu

What to Expect: Sightings of the "Samburu Special Five," excellent photography opportunities, and diverse wildlife along the river ecosystem.

Accommodation

Sarova Shaba Game Lodge

Luxury • Riverside accommodation in Samburu

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 3: Samburu to Ol Pejeta Conservancy

Distance/Time: ~200 km | Approx. 3-4 hours drive

Morning
After breakfast depart Samburu and drive to Ol Pejeta Conservancy. On the way you can stop at the equator marker for photographs and to experience this unique geographical phenomenon.

Afternoon
You will arrive in time for lunch at Serena Sweetwaters Tented Camp. After short rest you will proceed for a late afternoon game drive in this pioneering conservation area.

Evening
Serena Sweetwaters Tented Camp is located in a prime location in Ol Pejeta Conservancy. The conservancy is one of the few places where you can have night game drives at an extra cost. Other optional activities include lion tracking, camel ride, bush lunch/dinner etc. Interaction with the only two remaining northern white rhinos needs to be arranged in advance.

Activities: Scenic drive to Ol Pejeta, equator crossing, afternoon game drive

What to Expect: Transition to different ecosystem, introduction to Ol Pejeta's conservation work, and diverse wildlife sightings.

Accommodation

Serena Sweetwaters Tented Camp

Luxury • Premium tented accommodation with excellent conservancy access

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 4: Ol Pejeta to Lake Nakuru National Park

Distance/Time: ~150 km | Approx. 3 hours drive

Morning
Depart Ol Pejeta after breakfast and head to Lake Nakuru National Park, driving through the scenic central highlands with views of the Aberdare Mountains.

Afternoon
On arrival at Lake Nakuru National Park, you will enter and have en route game drive as you head to your lodge located inside the park. Arrive in time for lunch at Sarova Lion Hill Lodge. After a short period of relaxation you continue with game drives in Lake Nakuru National Park.

Evening
The park is famous for the flocks of the greater and lesser Flamingo that gather around the lake numbering around 2 million, which turns the lake pink. This has given the lake a nickname; the pink lake. The endangered rhino and Rothschild giraffe are found in the park. Other animals found in the park include African buffalos, zebras, impalas, olive baboons, vervet monkeys, waterbucks, hyenas, jackals, hippopotamus among many others.

Activities: Scenic drive to Lake Nakuru, afternoon game drive

What to Expect: Flamingo sightings, rhino viewing, and diverse birdlife in this compact but wildlife-rich national park.

Accommodation

Sarova Lion Hill Lodge

Day 5: Lake Nakuru to Maasai Mara National Reserve

Distance/Time: ~300 km | Approx. 5-6 hours drive

Morning
You will leave Lake Nakuru in the morning after early breakfast and drive to Masai Mara National Reserve, descending into the Great Rift Valley with spectacular views along the way.

Afternoon
You will arrive in time for lunch at Mara Serena Safari Lodge. After lunch and a brief rest, you will embark on your afternoon game drive feasting your eyes on the marvels that Masai Mara has to offer.

Evening
All members of the big 5 are found in this reserve. Other animals seen here include cheetah, serval, hyena, bat-eared foxes, black-backed and side-striped jackals, hippo, crocodile, baboons, warthog, topi, eland among others. Different bird species are also found. They include eagles, vultures, ostriches, storks, little grebe, pelicans, cormorants & darter. In the evening you will retire to your lodge, have dinner and fall asleep to the sounds of the African bush.

Activities: Scenic drive to Maasai Mara, afternoon game drive

What to Expect: First impressions of the vast Mara ecosystem, abundant wildlife sightings, and spectacular sunset views.

Accommodation

Mara Serena Safari Lodge

Luxury • Hilltop lodge with panoramic Mara views

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 6: Full Day Maasai Mara National Reserve

Full Day Exploration

Morning
After breakfast, you'll leave the lodge carrying packed lunches and embark on a full-day game viewing in Africa's finest park. A balloon safari can be arranged on this day early in the morning at extra cost for those seeking a spectacular aerial perspective.

Midday
You will drive to various places in the expansive Masai Mara National Reserve, exploring different habitats and searching for wildlife. Around midday, you'll stop for lunch at a scenic spot within the reserve.

Afternoon
Continue your exploration of the Mara with afternoon game drives across different sectors of the reserve. The vast savannah plains provide unparalleled opportunities to witness the Great Migration (seasonal), large prides of lions, cheetahs on the hunt, and massive herds of herbivores.

Evening
In the late afternoon, you will have the option of visiting a Maasai cultural village as you head back to the lodge in the evening. After dinner, you may choose to relax beside a warm bonfire or retire to your comfortable room.

Activities: Full day game drives in Maasai Mara, optional cultural visit

What to Expect: Abundant wildlife sightings, potential Great Migration viewing, and cultural experiences.

Accommodation

Mara Serena Safari Lodge

Luxury • Hilltop lodge with panoramic Mara views

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 7: Maasai Mara to Nairobi

Distance/Time: ~270 km | Approx. 5-6 hours drive to Nairobi

Morning
After breakfast you will depart the lodge and drive leisurely towards Nairobi. The journey provides final opportunities to spot wildlife as you exit the reserve and scenic views of the Kenyan countryside.

Afternoon
On arrival you will be taken to the famous Carnivore Restaurant for a complimentary lunch, known for its exceptional culinary experience and mouth-watering array of meats.

Departure
You will later be dropped at your city hotel or the airport according to your departure timings, filled with incredible memories of your 7-day luxury safari through Kenya's most spectacular wildlife destinations.

Activities: Scenic return journey, lunch at Carnivore Restaurant

What to Expect: Final wildlife sightings and a memorable dining experience at one of Africa's most famous restaurants.
