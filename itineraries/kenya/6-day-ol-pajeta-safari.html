Experience Kenya's premier wildlife destinations from rhino conservation to the Great Migration plains

6 Days / 5 Nights | From $3,189

6-Day Ol Pejeta, Lake Nakuru & Maasai Mara Luxury Safari

Embark on an extraordinary 6-day luxury safari journey through Kenya's most iconic wildlife destinations, combining groundbreaking conservation efforts at Ol Pejeta Conservancy with the breathtaking birdlife of Lake Nakuru and the world-renowned wildlife spectacle of the Maasai Mara. This carefully curated itinerary offers a comprehensive experience of Kenya's diverse ecosystems and conservation successes.

Begin your adventure at Ol Pejeta Conservancy, home to the world's last two northern white rhinos and the pioneering Sweetwaters Chimpanzee Sanctuary. Continue to Lake Nakuru National Park, famous for its flamingo-lined shores and thriving rhino population, before concluding in the legendary Maasai Mara, where the Great Migration unfolds and the Big Five roam freely across vast savannah plains.

Our expert guides will provide deep insights into each unique ecosystem and conservation initiative, ensuring you gain profound understanding of Kenya's wildlife protection efforts while enjoying unparalleled game viewing opportunities. With luxurious accommodations at premier lodges including Sweetwaters Serena Tented Camp, Sarova Lion Hill Lodge, and Mara Serena Safari Lodge, you'll experience the perfect blend of wilderness adventure and refined comfort.

This comprehensive journey is ideal for wildlife enthusiasts seeking diverse ecosystems, conservation advocates, and those wanting to experience Kenya's most celebrated national parks and conservancies in one unforgettable luxury safari experience.

Detailed 6-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Nairobi to Ol Pejeta Conservancy

Distance/Time: ~200 km | Approx. 3-4 hours drive from Nairobi

Morning
Our driver will pick you from the airport or hotel in Nairobi. After a comprehensive briefing about your safari, you will depart Nairobi for Ol Pejeta Conservancy, driving through the scenic central highlands with views of Mount Kenya on clear days.

Afternoon
Upon arrival, check in at Sweetwaters Serena Tented Camp and enjoy lunch. After a short rest, proceed for a late afternoon game drive in the conservancy, your first opportunity to encounter the remarkable wildlife of this pioneering conservation area.

Evening
Return to camp for dinner and overnight. Serena Sweetwaters Tented Camp is located in a prime location in Ol Pejeta Conservancy, offering excellent wildlife viewing opportunities right from the camp.

Activities: Scenic drive to Ol Pejeta, afternoon game drive

What to Expect: Introduction to Ol Pejeta's conservation work, first wildlife sightings, and comfortable accommodation in the heart of the conservancy.

Accommodation

Sweetwaters Serena Tented Camp

Lunch, Dinner

Day 2: Full Day Ol Pejeta Conservancy

Full Day Exploration

Morning
After breakfast, embark on a morning game drive exploring the diverse habitats of Ol Pejeta Conservancy. This is prime time for wildlife viewing, with excellent opportunities to spot rhinos, elephants, and predators during their most active hours.

Midday
Return to camp for lunch and relaxation, enjoying the camp's facilities and potentially watching wildlife from the comfort of your tent or the common areas.

Afternoon
Your afternoon exploration continues with visits to key conservation sites including the Sweetwaters Chimpanzee Sanctuary - the only place in Kenya to see chimpanzees. Learn about the conservancy's groundbreaking work in wildlife protection and community engagement, including their pioneering rhino conservation programs.

Evening
Return to camp for dinner and overnight, reflecting on the incredible conservation success stories and wildlife encounters of the day. The conservancy offers optional night game drives at extra cost for those interested in nocturnal wildlife.

Activities: Morning and afternoon game drives, visit to Sweetwaters Chimpanzee Sanctuary

What to Expect: Rhino sightings, chimpanzee encounters, and insights into innovative conservation practices.

Accommodation

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 3: Ol Pejeta to Lake Nakuru

Distance/Time: ~150 km | Approx. 3 hours drive

Morning
Depart Ol Pejeta after breakfast and drive to Lake Nakuru National Park. On the way, you'll have a brief stop at the equator marker in Nyahururu, where you can experience this unique geographical phenomenon and take photographs.

Afternoon
Arrive in time for lunch at Sarova Lion Hill Lodge. The safari lodge features a classic aesthetic and timeless design with a sense of calm and relaxation, offering the most scenic and spectacular views of the lake and national park. After lunch and a short rest, embark on an afternoon game drive in the park.

Evening
Lake Nakuru National Park is renowned for its flamingo populations that color the lake shores pink, as well as its thriving population of both black and white rhinos. During your game drive, you'll encounter diverse wildlife including buffalo, white colobus monkeys, impalas, hippos, waterbucks, and numerous bird species.

Activities: Scenic drive to Lake Nakuru, equator crossing experience, afternoon game drive

What to Expect: Flamingo sightings, rhino viewing, and diverse birdlife in this compact but wildlife-rich national park.

Accommodation

Sarova Lion Hill Lodge

Luxury • Scenic lodge with panoramic lake views

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 4: Lake Nakuru to Maasai Mara

Distance/Time: ~300 km | Approx. 5-6 hours drive

Morning
Depart Lake Nakuru after breakfast and drive to the world-famous Maasai Mara Game Reserve. The journey takes you through the Great Rift Valley escarpment, offering spectacular views and insights into rural Kenyan life.

Afternoon
Arrive in time for lunch at Mara Serena Safari Lodge, spectacularly perched on a hilltop with sweeping views across the Mara plains. After lunch and a short rest, embark on your first thrilling evening game drive in the Maasai Mara.

Evening
Nowhere in Africa is wildlife more abundant than in the Maasai Mara National Reserve. It is one of the best destinations in the world for wildlife viewing in their natural environment. There are over 95 species of mammals, amphibians and reptiles and more than 400 bird species recorded in the reserve. Return to the lodge in the evening for dinner and overnight.

Activities: Scenic drive to Maasai Mara, afternoon game drive

What to Expect: First impressions of the vast Mara ecosystem, abundant wildlife sightings, and spectacular sunset views.

Accommodation

Mara Serena Safari Lodge

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 5: Maasai Mara Full Day

Full Day Exploration

Morning
After breakfast, proceed for a full day of game viewing in Kenya's finest wildlife park. The extensive game drives take you in search of Africa's Big Five and a plethora of other wildlife inhabiting this vast Kenyan game reserve. Morning is ideal for predator activity and excellent photographic opportunities.

Midday
Enjoy a picnic lunch at a scenic spot within the reserve or return to the lodge for lunch, depending on your preference and wildlife movements.

Afternoon
Continue your exploration of the Mara with afternoon game drives across different sectors of the reserve. The vast savannah plains provide unparalleled opportunities to witness the Great Migration (seasonal), large prides of lions, cheetahs on the hunt, and massive herds of herbivores.

Evening
Return to the lodge for dinner and overnight, reflecting on the incredible wildlife spectacles witnessed throughout the day. Optional activities include visiting a Maasai village to learn about their culture or a hot air balloon safari (both at extra cost).

Activities: Full day game drives in Maasai Mara

What to Expect: Abundant wildlife sightings, potential Great Migration viewing, and spectacular African landscapes.

Accommodation

Mara Serena Safari Lodge

Luxury • Hilltop lodge with panoramic Mara views

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 6: Departure

Distance/Time: ~270 km | Approx. 5-6 hours drive to Nairobi

Morning
After breakfast at the lodge, depart the Maasai Mara and begin your leisurely drive back to Nairobi. The journey provides final opportunities to spot wildlife as you exit the reserve and scenic views of the Kenyan countryside.

Afternoon
Upon arrival in Nairobi, you'll be treated to lunch at the world-famous Carnivore Restaurant, renowned for its mouth-watering array of meats and vibrant atmosphere. This culinary experience provides the perfect finale to your safari adventure.

Departure
Following lunch, you'll be dropped off either at your city hotel or directly at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport, filled with incredible memories of your 6-day luxury safari through Kenya's most spectacular wildlife destinations.

Activities: Scenic return journey, lunch at Carnivore Restaurant

What to Expect: Final wildlife sightings and a memorable dining experience at one of Africa's most famous restaurants.
