5 Day Samburu & Ol Pejeta Safari

Explore the unique wildlife of Samburu National Reserve and the conservation success of Ol Pejeta Conservancy

5 Days / 4 Nights | From $2,657

5-Day Samburu & Ol Pejeta Safari Experience

Embark on an unforgettable 5-day journey through two of Kenya's most remarkable wildlife destinations, combining the unique northern species of Samburu National Reserve with the groundbreaking conservation efforts of Ol Pejeta Conservancy. This carefully curated safari offers a comprehensive experience of Kenya's diverse ecosystems, from the semi-arid landscapes of the north to the innovative wildlife protection initiatives in the central highlands.

Experience the raw beauty of the African wilderness as you encounter the "Samburu Special Five" - rare species found only in northern Kenya, and witness the incredible conservation success stories at Ol Pejeta, including the world's last two northern white rhinos and the Sweetwaters Chimpanzee Sanctuary. Each destination offers unique wildlife encounters and insights into different approaches to wildlife protection.

Our expert guides will share their deep knowledge of each ecosystem and conservation efforts, ensuring you gain profound insights into Kenya's natural wonders and wildlife protection initiatives. With comfortable accommodations at well-appointed camps including Samburu Intrepids and Sweetwaters Serena Tented Camp, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking safari experience while enjoying excellent amenities and service.

This comprehensive journey is perfect for wildlife enthusiasts seeking unique species, conservation advocates, and those wanting to experience the diversity of Kenya's wildlife and innovative protection efforts in one unforgettable trip.

Detailed 5-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Nairobi to Samburu National Reserve

Distance/Time: ~350 km | Approx. 5-6 hours drive from Nairobi

Morning
You will be picked up early in the morning and after introduction, embark on an exhilarating drive to the captivating Samburu National Reserve. The journey north takes you through changing landscapes, offering glimpses of rural Kenya and distant views of Mount Kenya on clear days.

Afternoon
Upon arrival, you'll be welcomed at your lodge with a delightful lunch that sets the tone for the day ahead. Post-lunch, brace yourself for an afternoon brimming with excitement as you explore the reserve through thrilling game drives.

Here, you'll encounter a diverse array of wildlife including zebras, majestic lions, swift cheetahs, magnificent elephants, towering giraffes, and the rare Blue-legged Somali Ostriches among many others. This is your first opportunity to spot the unique "Samburu Special Five" that make this reserve so special.

Evening
As dusk approaches, return to your lodge where a sumptuous dinner awaits you. End your day enveloped in nature's splendour, promising an unforgettable night under Africa's starry skies.

Activities: Scenic drive to Samburu, afternoon game drive

What to Expect: Introduction to Samburu's unique ecosystem, sightings of northern specials, and spectacular stargazing opportunities.

Day 2: Samburu National Reserve

Full Day Exploration

Morning
Today, you will have invigorating morning game drives, taking advantage of the cool dawn hours when wildlife is most active. The morning light creates perfect conditions for photography and wildlife observation along the riverbanks and acacia woodlands.

Midday
Return to the lodge for a delightful lunch break, enjoying the camp's amenities and potentially watching wildlife visiting the nearby river while you dine.

Afternoon
As you journey along the enchanting Ewaso Ngiro River during your afternoon game drive, keep an eye out for majestic elephants, formidable buffalo, and the unique antics of creatures such as dik-diks, warthogs, and olive baboons. This is also prime time to search for the reticulated giraffe, Grevy's zebra, and gerenuk that make Samburu so unique.

Evening
As evening approaches and the golden sun sets on the horizon, return to your lodge to relax and reflect on a day brimming with laughter and awe-inspiring encounters in this extraordinary wilderness.

Activities: Morning and afternoon game drives

What to Expect: Optimal wildlife viewing conditions, sightings of the "Samburu Special Five," and diverse wildlife along the river ecosystem.

Accommodation

Samburu Intrepids Camp

Luxury • Premium tented camp by the Ewaso Ngiro River

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 3: Samburu to Ol Pejeta Conservancy

Distance/Time: ~200 km | Approx. 3-4 hours drive

Morning
Begin your day with a hearty breakfast at the lodge before embarking on a scenic drive to the renowned Ol Pejeta Conservancy. The journey takes you south through the Laikipia plateau, offering changing landscapes and views of Mount Kenya.

Afternoon
Upon arrival, allow us to assist you in checking into your comfortable camp, where a delightful lunch awaits. Take some time to unwind and recharge before setting out for an exhilarating late afternoon game drive, offering you the chance to witness Africa's majestic wildlife in their natural habitat.

Evening
As the evening draws near, return to the lodge for a sumptuous dinner and indulge in an evening of relaxation beneath the enchanting African skies.

Serena Sweetwaters Tented Camp is located in a prime location in Ol Pejeta Conservancy. The conservancy is one of the few places where you can have night game drives at an extra cost. Other optional activities include lion tracking, camel ride, bush lunch/dinner etc. Viewing of the world's remaining Northern white rhino needs to be arranged in advance.

Activities: Scenic drive to Ol Pejeta, afternoon game drive

What to Expect: Transition to different ecosystem, introduction to Ol Pejeta's conservation work, and diverse wildlife sightings.

Accommodation

Sweetwaters Serena Tented Camp

Luxury • Premium tented accommodation with excellent conservancy access

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 4: Full Day Ol Pejeta Conservancy

Full Day Exploration

Morning
Begin your day with a delightful breakfast and prepare for an unparalleled adventure in the Ol Pejeta Conservancy. Your morning game drive offers excellent opportunities to spot wildlife during their most active hours.

Renowned for its ground-breaking conservation initiatives, Ol Pejeta offers you an exclusive opportunity to witness awe-inspiring wildlife up close. From majestic black and white rhinos to the iconic Big Five and the endearing Sweetwaters chimpanzees, this conservancy presents a truly unique experience that stands out from any other destination.

Midday
Return to camp for a leisurely lunch break, enjoying the camp's facilities and potentially arranging optional activities for the afternoon.

Afternoon
Your afternoon game drive continues your exploration of this remarkable conservancy. Visit the Sweetwaters Chimpanzee Sanctuary, the only place in Kenya to see chimpanzees, and learn about the conservancy's pioneering work in wildlife protection and community engagement.

Evening
Return to camp for dinner and overnight, reflecting on the incredible conservation success stories and wildlife encounters of the day.

Activities: Morning and afternoon game drives, visit to Sweetwaters Chimpanzee Sanctuary

What to Expect: Rhino sightings, chimpanzee encounters, and insights into innovative conservation practices.

Accommodation

Sweetwaters Serena Tented Camp

Luxury • Premium tented accommodation with excellent conservancy access

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 5: Ol Pejeta to Nairobi

Distance/Time: ~200 km | Approx. 3-4 hours drive back to Nairobi

Morning
Your safari adventure comes to a close today, marking the end of an unforgettable journey through the wild. After enjoying a hearty breakfast at your lodge, you will set off on a leisurely drive towards Nairobi.

This scenic route allows you to take in the last glimpses of the stunning landscapes and wildlife that have surrounded you during your stay. The morning departure provides potential final wildlife sightings as you exit the conservancy.

Afternoon
Once you arrive in Nairobi, you'll indulge in a delicious lunch at the renowned Carnivore Restaurant, famous for its mouth-watering array of meats and vibrant atmosphere.

Departure
Following this culinary experience, you'll be dropped off either at your city hotel or directly at the airport, ready to reflect on all the incredible memories you've made during this remarkable safari.

Activities: Scenic return journey, lunch at Carnivore Restaurant

What to Expect: Final wildlife sightings and a memorable dining experience at one of Africa's most famous restaurants.

Breakfast, Lunch
