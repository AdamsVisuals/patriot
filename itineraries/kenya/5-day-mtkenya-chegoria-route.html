5-Day Mount Kenya Trek via Chogoria Route

Embark on an unforgettable 5-day trekking adventure up Mount Kenya, Africa's second-highest peak, via the scenic Chogoria Route. This carefully curated journey takes you through diverse ecosystems, from lush montane forests to alpine moorlands and dramatic glacial valleys, culminating in a sunrise summit of Point Lenana (4,985m).

Experience the raw beauty of Mount Kenya's wilderness as you traverse through breathtaking landscapes including Lake Ellis, Lake Michaelson, and the stunning Gorges Valley. Each day offers spectacular views, unique alpine environments, and the challenge of high-altitude trekking in one of Africa's most magnificent mountain ranges.

Our experienced guides will share their deep knowledge of the mountain's ecology, geology, and history, ensuring a safe and rewarding trekking experience. With comfortable camping arrangements and expert logistical support, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking mountain adventure.

This trek is designed for outdoor enthusiasts and adventure seekers looking to conquer one of Africa's most iconic peaks. Whether you're an experienced hiker or seeking a challenging mountain adventure, this itinerary offers an authentic wilderness experience filled with unforgettable moments and stunning panoramas.

Detailed 5-Day Trekking Itinerary

Day 1: Nairobi to Chogoria Camp (3,000m)

Trekking time: 4 hours

After an early breakfast, you will meet our team in Nanyuki and be driven to the start of the route. The first day's walk takes you up through the forest for about four hours to a clearing where you camp in a pocket of forest above the Kenya Wildlife Service entrance at Chogoria Bandas.

If it is a clear afternoon you can generally get a good sense of the landscape from this point; where the Afro-alpine forest ends and the moorland begins. The afternoon is a good opportunity to walk around the area and acclimatize.

Accommodation

Chogoria Camp

Mountain Camp • Forest clearing campsite

Day 2: Chogoria Camp (3,000m) to Lake Ellis (3,460m)

Hiking time: 3 hours

Distance: About 12 km

Habitat: Montane forest

The walk starts with spectacular views of the rolling hills ahead and perhaps even a glimpse of the snow-capped peaks of Batian (5,199m) and Nelion (5,188m) high above.

This is a relatively short hike and you will arrive in camp a little earlier than the previous day, which gives you a great chance to sit back and relax after lunch and simply enjoy the views around Lake Ellis. We go by the general rule of thumb to "climb high and sleep low" to allow for better acclimatization. An evening walk up to nearby Mugi Hill can be a wonderful way to end the day and will hopefully make your ascent a lot easier as well. You can even try your hand at a spot of fly-fishing in the Lake.

Accommodation

Lake Ellis Camp

Mountain Camp • Lakeside campsite

Day 3: Lake Ellis (3,460m) to Lake Michaelson (4,100m)

Hiking time: 5 hours

Distance: About 15 km

Habitat: Moorland

This day is a beautiful walk which takes you up along the ridge overlooking the Gorges valley and later on in the day Lake Michelson comes into view. We will break for lunch overlooking the Lake with a spectacular view, and then continue on a gentle traverse and descend to Lake Michaelson camp site.

You can rest in the afternoon or go for a wander around the Lake, taking in the stunning alpine scenery and the dramatic peaks that surround this high-altitude valley.

Accommodation

Lake Michaelson Camp

Mountain Camp • High-altitude lakeside campsite

Day 4: Lake Michaelson (4,100m) to Austrian Hut (4,670m)

Hiking time: 4 hours

Distance: About 15 km

Habitat: Alpine desert

You will depart at around 9:00 am for a beautiful day's walk around the peaks. There are many little tarns that you will encounter on your traverse. You will arrive at the Austrian Hut where you will have lunch.

In the afternoon you can wander around along the edge of the Lewis Glacier and investigate the ice caves being formed as the glacier melts. This is your final acclimatization before the summit attempt, and an opportunity to experience the unique glacial environment of Mount Kenya.

Accommodation

Austrian Hut

Mountain Hut • High-altitude base camp

Day 5: Austrian Hut (4,670m) to Point Lenana (4,985m) to Old Moses

Hiking time: 8-10 hours

Distance: 6 km ascent - 21 km descent

Habitat: Stone scree and ice-capped summit

An early departure at 5:30 am for a sunrise summit is the best way to experience this climb and enjoy the expansive views of the mountainside, valleys and country beyond - even the summit of Kilimanjaro - whilst sipping a nice warm drink.

The descent takes you down the southern side along the Sirimon route where you will have brunch in the Mackinder Valley. Then it's a final walk down to Old Moses for pick-up. You will be back in Nairobi between 5 and 6 o'clock, having conquered one of Africa's most magnificent peaks.
