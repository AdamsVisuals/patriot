12 Day Samburu & Masai Mara

Experience diverse landscapes, rich cultures, and unforgettable wildlife encounter

12 Days / 11 Nights | From $7,800

12-Day Kenya Classic Safari Circuit

Embark on an extraordinary 12-day journey through Kenya's most iconic wildlife destinations, from the rugged beauty of Samburu to the rhino sanctuary of Ol Pejeta, the flamingo-filled shores of Lake Nakuru, the tranquil waters of Lake Naivasha, the legendary Masai Mara, and the Kilimanjaro-framed plains of Amboseli. This carefully curated safari offers an immersive experience across Kenya's most diverse ecosystems, creating unforgettable memories at every turn.

Experience the breathtaking contrast of landscapes as you witness the "Special Five" in Samburu's northern frontier, encounter rare rhinos in Ol Pejeta, marvel at the pink flocks of Lake Nakuru, enjoy serene boat safaris at Lake Naivasha, immerse yourself in the predator-rich plains of the Masai Mara, and capture elephants against Mount Kilimanjaro's majestic backdrop in Amboseli.

Our expert guides will share their deep knowledge of each ecosystem and wildlife behaviors, ensuring you gain profound insights into Kenya's natural wonders. With comfortable accommodations ranging from cozy lodges to ultra-luxury camps, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking safari experience across diverse landscapes.

This comprehensive journey is designed for those seeking the ultimate Kenyan safari experience, combining northern frontier adventures with classic southern destinations. Whether you're a first-time visitor or a seasoned safari enthusiast, this itinerary offers the perfect blend of wildlife diversity, scenic beauty, and cultural immersion.

Detailed 12-Day Itinerary

Days 1–2: Nairobi → Samburu & Full Day Exploration

Distance/Time: ~320 km | 6–7 hrs

Drive north through rolling highlands, passing through farmland and acacia-studded plains, until the arid beauty of Samburu unfolds. On arrival, step into a world of golden riverbanks where elephants drink alongside Grevy's zebras and reticulated giraffes. The afternoon is your first chance to glimpse the elusive Special Five: the gerenuk, Somali ostrich, Grevy's zebra, Beisa oryx, and the gerenuk's shy cousin.

Day 2 begins at the first blush of dawn, when the dryland predators awaken. Lions roar softly in the distance, while the sunlight illuminates the scarlet dust of the savannah. Morning and afternoon drives weave through riverine forests and open plains, each bend of the path revealing new wildlife wonders.

Days 3–4: Samburu → Ol Pejeta (2 Nights)

Distance/Time: ~150 km | 3–4 hrs

Travel south to the lush expanses of Ol Pejeta Conservancy, where rhinos roam and lions rule the plains. On Day 3, visit the Sweetwaters Chimpanzee Sanctuary, observing playful chimpanzees in a natural forest habitat. As dusk falls, embark on an optional night drive, where eyes gleam in the torchlight and nocturnal life stirs in the shadows.

Day 4 offers a full immersion into the conservancy: track rhinos grazing beside zebras, witness lion prides in majestic repose, and marvel at cheetahs sprinting across open plains. This sanctuary is a living, breathing kaleidoscope of African wildlife.

Day 5: Ol Pejeta → Lake Nakuru

Distance/Time: ~180 km | 4–5 hrs

Descend into the Rift Valley, where escarpments drop dramatically to reveal lakes that sparkle like liquid jewels. At Lake Nakuru, flamingos blanket the shoreline in clouds of pink, while rhinos graze in the open grasslands, undisturbed. The afternoon drive weaves through lakeshore and woodland habitats, offering the chance to spot giraffes nibbling acacias, buffalo in stately herds, and elusive leopards slipping silently through the brush.

Day 6: Lake Nakuru → Lake Naivasha

Distance/Time: ~80 km | 1.5–2 hrs

A short, scenic drive brings you to Lake Naivasha, a freshwater paradise fringed by papyrus reeds. Glide silently across placid waters on a boat safari, spotting hippos surfacing lazily, fish eagles hovering overhead, and waterbucks grazing the shoreline. Stroll along Crescent Island, where zebras and giraffes roam freely, or, for the adventurous, cycle through the dramatic cliffs and geothermal features of Hell's Gate National Park. This day is a gentle, immersive pause, a moment to absorb Kenya's natural rhythms.

Days 7–9: Naivasha → Masai Mara (2 Full Days)

Distance/Time: ~240 km | 4.5–6 hrs

Journey south to the legendary Masai Mara. On arrival, the vast, golden savannah stretches endlessly, dotted with acacia trees and dotted with wildlife. Afternoon game drives reveal lions lounging in shade, elephants meandering past, and gazelles bounding across open plains.

Days 8 and 9 are dedicated entirely to exploration. Choose early morning and late afternoon drives or full-day excursions with picnic lunches beneath the acacias. Optional hot-air balloon rides at dawn provide a bird's-eye view of the sweeping plains, Mara River crossings, and the seasonal spectacle of wildebeest migrations. Every hour is a chance for discovery: cheetahs sprinting in pursuit of prey, hippos wallowing in streams, and eagles circling high above.

Days 10–11: Masai Mara → Amboseli

Option 1: Road Transfer via Amboseli Corridor - Distance/Time: ~467 km | 7–9 hours
Option 2: Scenic Flight via Nairobi - Flight Time: ~2–3 hours total

Option 1: Travel south through the scenic Amboseli corridor, passing rolling hills, open savannahs, and Maasai villages. The journey offers glimpses of rural life and occasional wildlife sightings along the way. Upon arrival, the plains of Amboseli open beneath Mount Kilimanjaro, with afternoon drives through marshes and open grasslands revealing elephant herds, zebras, and birdlife.

Option 2: For a faster, breathtaking option, take a scenic flight over Kenya's patchwork of savannahs, rivers, and rolling hills. Arrive in Amboseli in time for lunch and an afternoon game drive. Witness elephants grazing with Kilimanjaro as a dramatic backdrop, explore marsh loops teeming with birdlife, and climb Observation Hill for panoramic vistas.

Day 12: Amboseli → Nairobi – Farewell to the Wild

Distance/Time: ~220 km | 4–5 hrs

Rise early for a final safari drive through Amboseli's golden plains. Witness elephants, giraffes, and lions bathed in dawn light, then embark on a scenic drive back to Nairobi. Rolling hills, rural villages, and distant escarpments pass by, offering a gentle transition from the wild to the city. Reflect on a journey rich in wildlife encounters, breathtaking landscapes, and the unforgettable rhythms of Kenya's wilderness. Arrive in Nairobi for onward flights or hotel drop-off, concluding your twelve-day safari adventure.
