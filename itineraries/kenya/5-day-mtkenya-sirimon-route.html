5-Day Mount Kenya Trek via Sirimon Route

Embark on an unforgettable 5-day trekking adventure up Mount Kenya, Africa's second-highest peak, via the popular Sirimon Route. This carefully curated journey takes you through diverse ecosystems, from lush montane forests to alpine moorlands and dramatic glacial valleys, culminating in a sunrise summit of Point Lenana (4,985m).

Experience the raw beauty of Mount Kenya's wilderness as you traverse through breathtaking landscapes including the Mackinders Valley, Harris Tarn, and the stunning Gorges Valley. Each day offers spectacular views, unique alpine environments, and the challenge of high-altitude trekking in one of Africa's most magnificent mountain ranges.

Our experienced guides will share their deep knowledge of the mountain's ecology, geology, and history, ensuring a safe and rewarding trekking experience. With comfortable hut accommodations and expert logistical support, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking mountain adventure.

This trek is designed for outdoor enthusiasts and adventure seekers looking to conquer one of Africa's most iconic peaks. Whether you're an experienced hiker or seeking a challenging mountain adventure, this itinerary offers an authentic wilderness experience filled with unforgettable moments and stunning panoramas.

Detailed 5-Day Trekking Itinerary

Day 1: Nairobi – Old Moses Camp (3,300m)

Leave Nairobi by 8:00 am and proceed to Mt Kenya National Park's Sirimon gate which normally takes 4 hours by transport. From Sirimon Gate follow the track uphill for 10 km forking left at the junction to reach Old Moses camp which is situated at 3,300m. The ascent from Sirimon gate to Old Moses is 690m.

This first day introduces you to the mountain environment as you climb through the forest zone into the moorland, with stunning views of the surrounding landscape unfolding as you gain elevation.

Accommodation

Old Moses Camp

Mountain Camp • First acclimatization camp

Day 2: Old Moses Camp (3,300m) to Shipton Camp (4,250m)

Hiking time: 6 hours

Distance: About 16 km

Habitat: Montane forest

After breakfast, leave at 8:00 am, follow the directions for the direct route, until you reach the fork after one hour. The track becomes a path, and swings right and south round the head of a large valley, crossing several small streams to reach the crest of the ridge between the Otulili and Liki North Valleys, then ascend the west side of the Valley, over a ridge, then down into the Mackinders Valley.

It will take roughly 6 hours to reach Shipton camp at 4,250m. This day offers spectacular views of the main peaks and takes you deeper into the high-altitude alpine zone.

Accommodation

Shipton Camp

Mountain Camp • High-altitude base

Day 3: Shipton Camp (4,250m) to Point Lenana (4,985m) down to Mintos Hut (4,250m)

Hiking time: 4-6 hours

Distance: About 9 km

Habitat: Moorland

Wake up at 2:00 am, have tea and biscuits, then leave at 3:00 am for the Lenana summit to watch the sunrise. This section is a long, hard slog, and the path is indistinct in places. Continue walking across a tough scree, until you come across Harris Tarn.

From Harris Tarn, continue up the North face of Point Lenana 4,985m, reaching the summit after about 45 minutes for the sunrise at 6:30 am. After enjoying the views and taking your pictures, descend down to Mintos Hut 4,250m for your breakfast. This summit day provides breathtaking panoramic views of the surrounding landscape.

Accommodation

Mintos Hut

Mountain Hut • Post-summit accommodation

Day 4: Mintos Hut (4,250m) to Meru Mount Kenya Bandas (2,900m)

Hiking time: 5 hours

Distance: About 15 km

Habitat: Semi-desert & Alpine desert

After breakfast descend to the Gorges Valley via Lake Michelson and Vivian falls to Meru Mount Kenya Bandas, following the Valley. It will take 5 hours to reach the bandas.

This descent day allows you to experience different aspects of the mountain as you pass through the Gorges Valley with its stunning geological formations and waterfalls, gradually returning to lower altitudes and warmer temperatures.

Accommodation

Meru Mount Kenya Bandas

Mountain Bandas • Comfortable mountain huts

Day 5: Meru Mount Kenya Bandas (2,900m) to Nairobi

Hiking time: 3 hours

Distance: 7 km

Habitat: Rainforest

After morning breakfast, continue walking down through the bamboo and indigenous forest to reach Bairunyi to meet up with your transport back to Nairobi.

This final descent takes you through the beautiful rainforest zone of Mount Kenya, completing your circular route and providing a gentle conclusion to your mountain adventure before returning to Nairobi.

End of Trek
