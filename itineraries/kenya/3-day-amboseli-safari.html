3 Day Amboseli National Park Safari

Explore the Famous Amboseli National Park with its majestic elephants and stunning Mount Kilimanjaro views

3 Days / 2 Nights | From $1,290

3-Day Amboseli National Park Safari Experience

Embark on an unforgettable 3-day journey to Amboseli National Park, one of Kenya's most iconic wildlife destinations renowned for its breathtaking views of Mount Kilimanjaro and spectacular elephant populations. This carefully curated safari offers an immersive experience in one of Africa's most photographed landscapes, where the continent's highest peak provides a stunning backdrop to incredible wildlife encounters.

Experience the raw beauty of the African wilderness as you witness large herds of elephants roaming the plains against the majestic silhouette of Mount Kilimanjaro, track the Big Five across the park's diverse ecosystems, and immerse yourself in the unique environment that gives Amboseli its Maasai name meaning "salty dust."

Our expert guides will share their deep knowledge of the ecosystem and wildlife behaviors, ensuring you gain profound insights into Amboseli's natural wonders. With comfortable accommodation at the luxurious Amboseli Serena Safari Lodge, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking safari experience while enjoying premium amenities and service.

This comprehensive journey is perfect for wildlife enthusiasts, photographers, and those seeking an authentic African adventure combined with stunning mountain scenery. Whether you're captivated by elephant behavior, mountain landscapes, or simply seeking a memorable safari experience, this itinerary offers a perfect introduction to Kenya's wildlife wonders and natural beauty.

Detailed 3-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Nairobi to Amboseli National Park

Distance/Time: ~240 km | Approx. 4-5 hours drive from Nairobi

Morning
You will be picked up in the morning from Nairobi. After a short briefing about your safari, our journey will take you on a drive through the picturesque landscape to reach the renowned Amboseli National Park just in time for lunch.

The scenic drive takes you through rural Kenyan landscapes, offering glimpses of local life and culture as you approach the world-famous park known for its incredible elephant populations and stunning mountain backdrop.

Afternoon
After savoring a delightful meal, you'll have some leisure time to relax before we set out on an exhilarating afternoon game drive. This experience promises close encounters with diverse wildlife thriving in their natural surroundings.

Nestled beneath the majestic shadow of Mount Kilimanjaro, Africa's tallest peak, Amboseli is one of Kenya's premier national parks. Its name derives from the Masai language meaning "salty dust," aptly reflecting its unique environment that offers unparalleled opportunities to observe large herds of elephants up close – truly one of Africa's most magnificent sights.

Evening
As dusk settles, we will return to our comfortable lodge where you can enjoy a sumptuous dinner followed by a restful overnight stay, with the silhouette of Mount Kilimanjaro creating a magical backdrop to your first evening in the wilderness.

Activities: Scenic drive to Amboseli, afternoon game drive

What to Expect: First views of Mount Kilimanjaro, initial wildlife sightings including elephant herds, and introduction to Amboseli's unique ecosystem.

Day 2: Amboseli National Park

Full Day Exploration

Morning
Awake to the breathtaking panorama of Mount Kilimanjaro, weather permitting. Embark on a thrilling early morning game drive, the prime time to catch wildlife at their liveliest as they thrive in the coolness of dawn.

The morning light creates perfect conditions for photography, with Mount Kilimanjaro providing a stunning backdrop for capturing unforgettable images of wildlife in their natural habitat. This is when predators are often most active, and the park's famous elephant herds begin their daily movements.

Midday
After your adventure, return to the lodge for a delightful breakfast and unwind while taking advantage of amenities such as the refreshing swimming pool. Enjoy lunch at the lodge while relishing the spectacular views of the surrounding wilderness.

Afternoon
After lunch, prepare for yet another exhilarating afternoon game drive through this magnificent park where Mount Kilimanjaro provides a stunning backdrop for capturing unforgettable photographs. Witness majestic creatures including the Big Five, herds of elephants, and an abundance of bird life that enriches this African wilderness paradise.

A visit to an observation point will offer you a captivating bird's-eye view over this vast wildlife habitat teeming with life below, providing excellent opportunities to spot wildlife from an elevated perspective.

Evening
As evening descends, you'll head back to the lodge for dinner followed by some leisure options – perhaps lounging by a bonfire with drinks or retreating into your cosy room for rest and relaxation, surrounded by the sounds of the African night.

Activities: Early morning game drive, afternoon game drive, observation point visit

What to Expect: Optimal wildlife viewing conditions, spectacular Kilimanjaro views, elephant herd encounters, and diverse birdlife sightings.

Accommodation

Day 3: Amboseli National Park to Nairobi

Distance/Time: ~240 km | Approx. 4-5 hours drive back to Nairobi

Morning
As your thrilling Amboseli safari adventure draws to a close, you'll embark on one final early morning game drive to soak in the breathtaking sights of the African wilderness. This last opportunity allows you to capture final photographs of wildlife against the magnificent backdrop of Mount Kilimanjaro and bid farewell to the majestic elephants that make Amboseli so special.

Midday
Once you've returned to the lodge and enjoyed a hearty breakfast, it's time to depart for Nairobi at a leisurely pace. The return journey gives you time to reflect on your incredible safari experiences and the unforgettable moments spent in one of Africa's most iconic national parks.

Afternoon
Upon reaching Nairobi, indulge in an exquisite lunch at the renowned Carnivore restaurant, famed for its delectable offerings and unique dining experience featuring various types of meat cooked over an open flame.

Departure
After your delightful meal, you will be transported with care either to your city hotel or directly to the airport, tailored precisely according to your departure schedule, marking the end of your memorable Amboseli National Park safari experience.

Activities: Final morning game drive, scenic return journey, lunch at Carnivore Restaurant

What to Expect: Last wildlife sightings in optimal morning light, scenic landscapes, and a memorable dining experience at one of Africa's most famous restaurants.
