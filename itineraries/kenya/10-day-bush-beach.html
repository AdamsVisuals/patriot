10-Day Bush & Beach Safari

Experience the ultimate Kenya adventure from wildlife safaris to pristine Indian Ocean beaches

10 Days / 9 Nights | From $3,970

10-Day Amboseli, Tsavo West, Tsavo East & Mombasa Luxury Bush & Beach Safari

Embark on the ultimate 10-day luxury bush and beach safari combining Kenya's most spectacular wildlife destinations with the pristine shores of the Indian Ocean. This comprehensive itinerary offers the perfect blend of thrilling wildlife encounters in Amboseli and Tsavo National Parks followed by complete relaxation on Mombasa's famous white sand beaches.

Begin your adventure in Amboseli National Park, famous for its large elephant herds and breathtaking views of Mount Kilimanjaro. Continue to Tsavo West National Park to explore its diverse landscapes and visit the rhino sanctuary, then journey to Tsavo East to witness the famous red elephants and the world's longest lava flow. Conclude your safari with three nights of pure relaxation at Sarova Whitesands Beach Resort, where you can unwind by the Indian Ocean and enjoy various water sports and coastal activities.

Our expert guides will provide deep insights into each unique ecosystem, ensuring you gain profound understanding of Kenya's wildlife while enjoying unparalleled game viewing opportunities. With luxurious accommodations at premier lodges including Amboseli Serena Safari Lodge, Kilaguni Serena Safari Lodge, Ashnil Aruba Lodge, and Sarova Whitesands Beach Resort & Spa, you'll experience the perfect blend of wilderness adventure and coastal luxury.

This comprehensive journey is ideal for travelers seeking both wildlife excitement and beach relaxation, photography enthusiasts, and those wanting to experience Kenya's diverse landscapes from savannah plains to tropical coastline in one unforgettable luxury experience.

Detailed 10-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Nairobi to Amboseli National Park

Distance/Time: ~240 km | Approx. 4-5 hours drive

Morning
Your dedicated driver-guide, alongside an office representative, will warmly greet you at your hotel or the airport. Following a concise orientation about your upcoming safari adventure, you'll embark on a scenic drive to the majestic Amboseli National Park.

Afternoon
Upon arrival, settle into your accommodation at Amboseli Serena Safari Lodge and unwind as you anticipate a delightful lunch. Post-lunch, venture into the park on an exciting quest for its renowned large elephant herds and other captivating wildlife.

Evening
Conclude your day back at the hotel with a sumptuous dinner and a restful overnight stay, readying yourself for more adventures ahead amidst the stunning backdrop of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Activities: Scenic drive to Amboseli, afternoon game drive

What to Expect: First views of Mount Kilimanjaro, elephant herds, and diverse wildlife in Amboseli's unique ecosystem.

Day 2: Full Day Amboseli National Park

Full Day Exploration

Morning
You will wake up to amazing views of Mount Kilimanjaro if the weather permits. Start your day with a morning game drive in this wonderful park where Mount Kilimanjaro's background offers excellent photographic scenery.

Midday
Return to the lodge for breakfast and lunch, taking time to enjoy the lodge's amenities and the spectacular views of Africa's highest peak.

Afternoon
Continue your exploration with an afternoon game drive, searching for the Big Five, herds of elephants, and the prolific birdlife in this African wilderness park. Another exciting place will be the observation point where you will have a bird's eye view of the expansive park below teeming with wildlife.

Evening
There will also be an optional Masai village visit to learn about local culture. Return to the lodge for dinner and overnight stay.

Activities: Morning and afternoon game drives, optional cultural visit

What to Expect: Optimal Kilimanjaro views, elephant sightings, and diverse wildlife photography opportunities.

Day 3: Amboseli to Tsavo West National Park

Distance/Time: ~150 km | Approx. 3-4 hours drive

Morning
After enjoying a hearty breakfast, prepare to embark on your journey to the stunning Tsavo West National Park, known for its dramatic landscapes and diverse wildlife.

Afternoon
Upon arrival, immerse yourself in the incredible sights and sounds of wildlife before checking into Kilaguni Serena Safari Lodge where a delectable lunch awaits you. The adventure doesn't stop there; an afternoon game drive promises even more excitement with a notable visit to the rhino sanctuary – truly an experience that will leave lasting memories.

Evening
As dusk settles, return to the hotel for a tranquil and restful night's sleep, listening to the sounds of the African wilderness.

Activities: Scenic drive to Tsavo West, afternoon game drive, rhino sanctuary visit

What to Expect: Dramatic landscapes, rhino sightings, and diverse wildlife in Tsavo West's unique ecosystem.

Day 4: Tsavo West National Park

Full Day Exploration

Morning
On this day, you will have morning and afternoon game drives exploring the extensive diverse habitats of Tsavo West which offers an ideal area to search for pride of Lions, Cheetahs, Giraffes, Zebras, Hippos, Wildebeests and many more.

Midday
In between game drives, you can relax at the lodge watching animals as they come to the water hole or spoil yourself at the lodge's spa with minimal cost. Other activities include bush breakfast or cocktail sundowner at extra cost.

Afternoon
Visit Mzima Springs, an underground water seepage from Chyulu Hills, which is a good place to watch the Hippos. There is also wealth and diversity of birds' species here for bird-lovers.

Evening
Return to the lodge for dinner and overnight, reflecting on the diverse wildlife encounters of the day.

Activities: Morning and afternoon game drives, Mzima Springs visit

What to Expect: Diverse wildlife sightings, hippo viewing at springs, and excellent birdwatching opportunities.

Accommodation

Kilaguni Serena Safari Lodge

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 5: Tsavo West to Tsavo East National Park

Distance/Time: ~100 km | Approx. 2-3 hours drive

Morning
After breakfast depart for Tsavo East National Park, Kenya's largest protected area known for its famous red elephants and vast wilderness.

Afternoon
Upon reaching the park, a thrilling game drive awaits as we journey to your cozy lodge nestled inside the park. Savor a delicious lunch at Ashnil Aruba Lodge upon arrival and take a moment to relax before setting off for an afternoon game drive.

Evening
Discover the wonders of Kenya's largest protected area, teeming with magnificent wildlife such as majestic elephants covered in red dust, elusive rhinos, powerful buffalos, regal lions, graceful leopards, playful hippos, stealthy crocodiles, elegant waterbucks, captivating lesser kudus, and a plethora of bird species filling the skies with their melodic tunes.

Activities: Scenic drive to Tsavo East, afternoon game drive

What to Expect: Famous red elephants, diverse wildlife, and vast wilderness landscapes.

Accommodation

Ashnil Aruba Lodge

Luxury • Riverside lodge in Tsavo East National Park

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 6: Tsavo East National Park

Full Day Exploration

Morning
On this day you will have a morning and afternoon game drives in the captivating wilderness of Tsavo East. You are likely to witness the majestic sight of dust-red elephants playfully wallowing and spraying each other with water from the palm-shaded Galana River, creating a scene straight out of a safari dream.

Midday
Return to the lodge for lunch and relaxation, enjoying the lodge's facilities and potentially watching wildlife from the comfort of your accommodation.

Afternoon
Let yourself be awed by the 300-kilometer long Yatta Plateau, the world's longest lava flow, adding an element of excitement to your adventure like no other in Africa. This experience promises to be truly exceptional and memorable for you as you explore this unique landscape.

Evening
Return to the lodge for dinner and overnight, reflecting on the incredible wildlife sightings and geological wonders witnessed throughout the day.

Activities: Morning and afternoon game drives, Yatta Plateau viewing

What to Expect: Red elephant herds, unique geological formations, and diverse wildlife in Kenya's largest park.

Accommodation

Ashnil Aruba Lodge

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 7: Tsavo East to Mombasa

Distance/Time: ~250 km | Approx. 4-5 hours drive

Morning
After breakfast you will be driven to the coastal town of Mombasa, transitioning from the wilderness to the tropical coastline of the Indian Ocean.

Afternoon
On arrival, you will be taken to Sarova Whitesands Beach Resort and Spa located on the pristine beach shore of the Indian Ocean, nestled among towering palms, just half an hour's drive from Mombasa's City Centre. The rest of the afternoon will be spent at leisure, allowing you to settle into your beachfront accommodation.

Evening
Enjoy dinner at the resort and your first evening listening to the sounds of the Indian Ocean waves.

Activities: Scenic drive to Mombasa, beach resort check-in, leisure time

What to Expect: Transition to coastal environment, pristine beaches, and resort amenities.

Day 8 & 9: Beach Relaxation at Sarova Whitesands

Beach Leisure Days

Full Days
The remaining days of your stay will be at your leisure, allowing you to fully relax and unwind at the lodge. If you're seeking a bit more adventure, there's an opportunity to partake in various water sports for an additional fee.

The North Coast boasts pristine white sandy beaches framed by lush forests and caressed by surf-worthy waves, making it an incredibly popular destination. Additionally, Mombasa town offers numerous entertainment venues for those looking to explore local culture and nightlife.

Enjoy the resort's extensive facilities including multiple swimming pools, spa treatments, various dining options, and direct access to the beautiful beach. These days are yours to design according to your preferences - whether that means complete relaxation or exploring the coastal attractions.

Activities: Beach relaxation, water sports, spa treatments, local exploration

What to Expect: Complete relaxation, beach activities, and coastal experiences.

Day 10: Departure

Return Journey

Morning
After breakfast, you will be transferred to the Moi International Airport for your flight to Nairobi.

Afternoon
You will be picked up at the Jomo Kenyatta International Airport and be hosted for lunch at the famous Carnivore Restaurant, known for its exceptional dining experience.

Departure
After this culinary experience, you will be dropped at your city hotel or the airport for your flight back home, filled with incredible memories of your 10-day bush and beach safari through Kenya's most spectacular wildlife destinations and pristine coastline.

Activities: Flight to Nairobi, lunch at Carnivore Restaurant

What to Expect: Smooth transitions and a memorable final dining experience.
