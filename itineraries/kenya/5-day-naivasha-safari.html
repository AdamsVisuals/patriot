5 Day Lake Naivasha, Nakuru & Masai Mara Safari

Explore the stunning lakes of Naivasha and Nakuru combined with the world-famous Masai Mara Game Reserve

5 Days / 4 Nights | From $2,657

5-Day Lake Naivasha, Nakuru & Masai Mara Safari Experience

Embark on an unforgettable 5-day journey through Kenya's most diverse and spectacular wildlife destinations, combining the stunning lake ecosystems of Naivasha and Nakuru with the world-renowned Masai Mara Game Reserve. This carefully curated safari offers a comprehensive Kenyan experience, from walking safaris in dramatic landscapes to classic game drives in Africa's most celebrated wildlife sanctuary.

Experience the raw beauty of the African wilderness as you explore Hell's Gate National Park on foot, witness the vibrant birdlife and rhino populations at Lake Nakuru, and immerse yourself in the breathtaking predator-rich plains of the Masai Mara. Each destination offers unique wildlife encounters and photographic opportunities in dramatically different landscapes.

Our expert guides will share their deep knowledge of each ecosystem, ensuring you gain profound insights into Kenya's natural wonders. With comfortable accommodations at well-appointed lodges including Lake Naivasha Sopa, Sarova Lion Hill, and Sarova Mara Game Lodge, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking safari experience while enjoying excellent amenities and service.

This comprehensive journey is perfect for wildlife enthusiasts seeking diverse experiences, photographers capturing different landscapes, and those wanting to experience the best of Kenya's wildlife destinations in one unforgettable trip.

Detailed 5-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Nairobi – Lake Naivasha

Distance/Time: ~90 km | Approx. 2 hours drive from Nairobi

Morning
You will be picked up by your driver-guide at the airport or your city hotel in the morning and be driven to Lake Naivasha. On the way, you will have a brief stop at the viewpoint to see the Great Rift Valley.

The scenic drive offers stunning views of the Rift Valley escarpment, one of the world's most significant geological features, before descending to the floor of the valley where Lake Naivasha is located.

Afternoon
On arrival, you will check-in at your head lodge and have lunch. After lunch and a short rest, you will embark on an afternoon walking safari at Hell's Gate National Park.

The park has a variety of wildlife. Giraffe, eland, hartebeest, buffalo and other small gazelles and antelopes are common in the grassy plains. Also available but rarely seen are lions, cheetahs and leopards.

Evening
Back to the lodge in the evening for dinner and overnight stay, surrounded by the tranquil beauty of Lake Naivasha and its surrounding landscapes.

Activities: Scenic drive with Rift Valley viewpoint, walking safari at Hell's Gate National Park

What to Expect: Spectacular Rift Valley views, unique walking safari experience, and diverse wildlife in dramatic landscapes.

Day 2: Lake Naivasha – Lake Nakuru National Park

Distance/Time: ~70 km | Approx. 1.5 hours drive

Morning
You will have an optional early morning boat ride at Lake Naivasha and after breakfast be driven to the beautiful Lake Nakuru National Park. The boat ride offers opportunities to see hippos, waterbirds, and possibly fish eagles in their natural habitat.

Afternoon
On arrival, you will have game drives. Later check-in at your head lodge and have lunch. After lunch and a short rest, you will embark on an afternoon tour of the beautiful Lake Nakuru National Park.

Located on the floor of the Rift Valley and considered bird watchers paradise, Lake Nakuru National Park hosts a huge variety of birds. Also found here is endangered Rothschild giraffe, white and black rhino among other wildlife. Other attractions include baboon cliff, lion hill and Makalia water falls.

Evening
Back to the lodge in the evening for dinner and overnight, with memories of the day's incredible wildlife sightings.

Activities: Optional boat ride, game drives in Lake Nakuru National Park

What to Expect: Spectacular birdlife, rhino sightings, and diverse wildlife in a stunning lake setting.

Day 3: Lake Nakuru – Masai Mara National Reserve

Distance/Time: ~280 km | Approx. 5-6 hours drive

Morning
After breakfast at the lodge, you will depart for Masai Mara. The journey takes you through picturesque landscapes as you transition from the lake region to the vast savannahs of the Mara.

Afternoon
On arrival, you will have an en-route game drive as you proceed to the lodge where you will check in and have lunch. After lunch and a short rest, you will have an afternoon game drive.

The Masai Mara is the most famous reserve due to its large population of wildlife. It is one of the few places where all of the "Big Five" are found. This park also boasts 470 species of birds and rare animals like roan antelopes and nocturnal bat-eared foxes. You will also see the beautiful lilac-breasted roller which is Kenya's national bird.

Evening
Back to the lodge in the evening for dinner. After dinner you can relax around a fireplace being entertained with Masai songs or head to your room.

Activities: Scenic drive to Masai Mara, afternoon game drive

What to Expect: First impressions of the iconic Masai Mara, initial wildlife sightings, and cultural entertainment.

Day 4: Full Day Masai Mara National Reserve

Full Day Exploration

Morning
Our morning game drive starts soon after breakfast for a full day when we again head deep into the wild in search of its thrills and spills. A balloon safari can be arranged for this day at an additional cost.

Like the evening game drive, morning drives are very rewarding in terms of wildlife and riveting scenery. You can expect immense multitudes of the Big Five, hippos, giraffes, antelopes, gazelles, impalas, and wildebeests. The birds aren't shy either as the kingfisher, hornbill, secretary bird, and Kori bustard are expected to appear.

Midday
At noon, we gather under the signature acacia tree in the Mara for a delicious packed lunch. This provides a perfect opportunity to relax and absorb the magnificent surroundings.

Afternoon
Afterwards, our game drive continues with an afternoon to evening game drive. Every turn within the Mara reveals new sets of attractions to admire. If we are not fussing over the wildlife, sunburned Savannah, or bird variety, then we are admiring the scenery.

Evening
We return to the lodge in the evening for dinner and overnight, reflecting on the incredible wildlife encounters of the day.

Activities: Full-day game drive with packed lunch, optional hot air balloon safari

What to Expect: Extensive wildlife viewing, potential predator sightings, and spectacular Mara landscapes.

Day 5: Masai Mara to Nairobi

Distance/Time: ~280 km | Approx. 5-6 hours drive back to Nairobi

Morning
After breakfast you will depart the lodge and drive leisurely towards Nairobi. The morning departure provides potential final wildlife sightings as you exit the reserve.

Afternoon
On arrival you will be taken to the famous Carnivore Restaurant for complimentary lunch. This iconic establishment is celebrated for its unique dining experience, offering an array of succulent meats cooked to perfection.

Departure
You will later be dropped at your city hotel or the airport according to your departure timings, marking the end of your unforgettable 5-day Kenyan safari adventure.

Activities: Scenic return journey, lunch at Carnivore Restaurant

What to Expect: Final views of the Kenyan landscape and a memorable dining experience at one of Africa's most famous restaurants.
