8-Day Bush & Beach Safari

Experience Kenya's wildlife wonders and pristine Indian Ocean beaches in one unforgettable journey

8 Days / 7 Nights | From $3,285

8-Day Lake Naivasha, Lake Nakuru, Masai Mara & Diani Beach Luxury Safari

Embark on an extraordinary 8-day luxury bush to beach safari combining Kenya's most spectacular wildlife destinations with the pristine shores of Diani Beach. This perfectly balanced itinerary offers the ultimate Kenyan experience, blending thrilling wildlife encounters with complete tropical relaxation on the Indian Ocean coastline.

Begin your adventure with a walking safari in Hell's Gate National Park, followed by exploration of Lake Nakuru's famous flamingo-filled shores. Continue to the world-renowned Masai Mara for incredible Big Five sightings and the Great Migration spectacle, before concluding your journey with three nights of pure relaxation at Baobab Beach Resort on Diani's famous white sand beaches.

Our expert guides will provide deep insights into each unique ecosystem, ensuring you gain profound understanding of Kenya's wildlife while enjoying unparalleled game viewing opportunities. With luxurious accommodations at premier lodges including Lake Naivasha Sopa Lodge, Sarova Lion Hill Lodge, Sarova Mara Game Camp, and Baobab Beach Resort & Spa, you'll experience the perfect blend of wilderness adventure and coastal luxury.

This comprehensive journey is ideal for travelers seeking both wildlife excitement and beach relaxation, photography enthusiasts, and those wanting to experience Kenya's diverse landscapes from savannah plains to tropical coastline in one unforgettable luxury experience.

Detailed 8-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Nairobi to Lake Naivasha & Hell's Gate National Park

Distance/Time: ~90 km | Approx. 2 hours drive

Morning
You will be picked up in the morning by your driver guide, who will accompany you on an exciting journey to Hell's Gate National Park. Upon your arrival at this remarkable destination, you'll embark on a walking safari that allows you to immerse yourself in the stunning natural beauty of the park.

Afternoon
Hell's Gate is unique not only for its breath-taking landscapes but also for its diverse wildlife and rich variety of flora. This park is notably the only one in Kenya where outdoor activities such as climbing, walking, and biking are permitted, offering visitors an unparalleled opportunity to explore its rugged terrains up close.

Evening
Around midday, you'll make your way back to Lake Naivasha Sopa Lodge for a well-deserved lunch after your morning adventures. Following lunch, you'll have a leisurely afternoon ahead of you—perfect for unwinding or indulging in optional activities such as serene boat rides on the lake at your own expense.

Activities: Scenic drive to Lake Naivasha, Hell's Gate walking safari

What to Expect: Unique walking safari experience, diverse wildlife sightings, and beautiful lake views.

Accommodation

Lake Naivasha Sopa Lodge

Luxury • Beautiful lakeside accommodation

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 2: Lake Naivasha to Lake Nakuru National Park

Distance/Time: ~60 km | Approx. 1.5 hours drive

Morning
After enjoying a hearty breakfast, you'll embark on an exciting drive to Lake Nakuru National Park, a stunning destination known for its breath-taking landscapes and diverse wildlife. Upon your arrival at the park, you'll officially enter its gates and set off on thrilling game drives that will immerse you in the vibrant ecosystem of the area.

Afternoon
As midday approaches, you will check in at Sarova Lion Hill Lodge—a comfortable retreat where you can relax and recharge. Here, a delicious lunch awaits you, featuring local cuisine that highlights the flavours of Kenya. After savouring your meal and taking some time to rest and refresh yourself, you'll head back into the park for more game drives.

Evening
This second round offers another opportunity to spot elusive wildlife and capture stunning photographs against the backdrop of Lake Nakuru's shimmering waters. In the evening, as the sun sets over this picturesque landscape painting it with hues of orange and pink, you'll make your way back to the lodge for dinner.

Activities: Scenic drive to Lake Nakuru, morning and afternoon game drives

What to Expect: Flamingo sightings, rhino viewing, and diverse birdlife in this compact but wildlife-rich national park.

Accommodation

Sarova Lion Hill Lodge

Luxury • Scenic lodge with panoramic lake views

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 3: Lake Nakuru to Masai Mara Game Reserve

Distance/Time: ~300 km | Approx. 5-6 hours drive

Morning
After enjoying a delicious breakfast at the lodge, you will set off for the breath-taking Masai Mara. The anticipation builds as you travel toward this renowned wildlife reserve, famed for its stunning landscapes and diverse animal life.

Afternoon
Upon arrival, you'll enter the reserve, where your adventure begins with an exhilarating game drive en route to Sarova Mara Game Camp. This is where you'll have your first taste of the incredible wildlife that calls this area home, with opportunities to spot majestic elephants, graceful giraffes, and perhaps even a pride of lions basking in the sun.

Evening
Once you reach the lodge, you'll check in and be treated to a satisfying lunch that showcases local flavours. After a brief rest to recharge your batteries and soak in the gorgeous surroundings, it's time for another thrilling game drive. As evening falls and the sun sets over the savannah painting the sky in hues of orange and pink, you'll return to the lodge for dinner.

Activities: Scenic drive to Masai Mara, afternoon game drive

What to Expect: First impressions of the vast Mara ecosystem, abundant wildlife sightings, and spectacular sunset views.

Accommodation

Sarova Mara Game Camp

Luxury • Authentic tented camp experience in the Mara

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 4: Full Day in Masai Mara National Reserve

Full Day Exploration

Morning
After enjoying a hearty breakfast to fuel your adventurous day, you will depart from the lodge with a packed lunch in hand, ready to embark on an exhilarating full-day game drive in the breath-taking Masai Mara National Reserve. If you're feeling particularly adventurous, an early morning balloon safari can be arranged for this day, offering a unique perspective of the vast savannah as you gently float above the landscape.

Midday
As midday approaches and the sun hangs high in the sky, you'll take a well-deserved break under the shade of a magnificent acacia tree. Here, you can savour your packed lunch while soaking in the sounds of nature around you—perhaps the distant roar of a lion or the playful chatter of monkeys nearby.

Afternoon
Once you've recharged your energy with some delicious food and restful moments amid nature's splendour, you'll set off again for an afternoon game drive. This is another opportunity to spot more incredible wildlife—be it herds of elephants roaming majestically or cheetahs sprinting gracefully across open plains.

Evening
For those who wish to immerse themselves further into local culture, a visit to a nearby Masai village can also be arranged at an extra cost. As late afternoon rolls around, you'll make your way back to your lodge where you can unwind after an eventful day filled with adventure and exploration.

Activities: Full day game drives in Masai Mara, optional cultural visit

What to Expect: Abundant wildlife sightings, potential Great Migration viewing, and cultural experiences.

Accommodation

Sarova Mara Game Camp

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 5: Masai Mara to Diani Beach

Flight to Coast

Morning
Today marks your final day in the captivating wilderness of the Masai Mara. After enjoying a hearty breakfast at your lodge, you'll set off for an adventure-filled morning as you depart for the Keekorok airstrip. The anticipation of catching a local flight to Ukunda on the stunning South Coast adds an exciting twist to your day.

Afternoon
Upon arrival at Ukunda Airport, a friendly driver will be waiting to whisk you away to Baobab Beach Resort, where a delicious lunch awaits you—an opportunity to savour local flavours and perhaps reflect on your incredible experiences in the bush.

Evening
Once you've had your fill, the afternoon is yours to enjoy as you wish. Picture yourself lounging at the beach, feeling the warm sand beneath your toes and listening to the gentle lapping of waves against the shore—a perfect way to unwind after days filled with thrilling wildlife encounters.

Activities: Morning game drive, flight to coast, beach relaxation

What to Expect: Transition from wilderness to tropical paradise, beachfront luxury, and coastal relaxation.

Accommodation

Baobab Beach Resort & Spa

Luxury • Beachfront paradise on Diani Beach

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 6 & 7: Diani Beach Relaxation

Beach Leisure Days

Full Days
These two days will be all about relaxation and enjoying the breathtaking beauty of Baobab Beach Resort at Diani Beach. You have plenty of options to fill your time however you please. If you're in the mood, you can simply soak up the sun on the soft, golden sands while listening to the soothing sound of waves lapping gently against the shore.

For those seeking a bit more excitement, there's an array of thrilling water sports and activities available at your own expense, from jet skiing to snorkelling in vibrant coral reefs teeming with marine life. Alternatively, if you're feeling adventurous and want to explore a bit more of what this beautiful region has to offer, consider taking a tour around Diani's charming coastal town.

Here, you can immerse yourself in local culture, savour delicious seafood at beachfront restaurants, or even shop for unique crafts and souvenirs made by local artisans. The possibilities are endless!

Activities: Beach relaxation, water sports, spa treatments, local exploration

What to Expect: Complete relaxation, beach activities, and coastal experiences.

Accommodation

Baobab Beach Resort & Spa

Luxury • Beachfront paradise on Diani Beach

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner

Day 8: Diani to Nairobi - Departure

Return Journey

Morning
Your bush and beach safari adventure concludes today, marking the end of an unforgettable journey filled with stunning landscapes and vibrant wildlife. After enjoying a hearty breakfast at your coastal resort, it's time to bid farewell to the serene shores and embark on your transfer to Ukunda Airport.

Afternoon
Once you arrive at Ukunda Airport, you'll catch your flight back to Nairobi, where a new culinary experience awaits you. Upon landing in Nairobi, prepare yourself for a delicious meal at the renowned Carnivore Restaurant.

Departure
This unique dining establishment is famous for its wide array of meats served in an all-you-can-eat style, allowing you to indulge in flavours that showcase the best of local cuisine. After satisfying your appetite with this iconic feast, you'll be conveniently dropped off either at the airport for your international flight or at your city hotel if you're extending your stay.

Activities: Flight to Nairobi, lunch at Carnivore Restaurant

What to Expect: Smooth transitions and a memorable final dining experience.

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch
