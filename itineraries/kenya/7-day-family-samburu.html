7-Day Luxury Family Safari

Experience Kenya's ultimate family adventure from northern specials to the Great Migration plains

7 Days / 6 Nights | From $6,025 per adult | $3,398 per child

7-Day Samburu & Maasai Mara Luxury Family Safari

Embark on an extraordinary 7-day luxury family safari journey through Kenya's most spectacular wildlife destinations, combining the unique northern species of Samburu with the world-renowned wildlife spectacle of the Maasai Mara. This carefully curated family-focused itinerary offers an unparalleled experience of Kenya's diverse ecosystems with exclusive activities designed specifically for families and children.

Begin your adventure in Samburu National Reserve, home to the "Samburu Special Five" and guided by authentic Samburu warriors who share their ancient wisdom and cultural traditions. Continue to the legendary Maasai Mara, where your family will enjoy private game drives in Mara North Conservancy and participate in the exclusive Warrior Academy program, creating unforgettable memories and learning experiences for all ages.

Our family-friendly approach ensures children are engaged and educated throughout the journey, with specialized activities, kid-friendly guides, and accommodations designed with families in mind. With luxurious stays at Saruni Samburu's family villa and exclusive access to Mara North Conservancy, your family will experience the perfect blend of wilderness adventure, cultural immersion, and refined comfort.

This comprehensive family journey is ideal for families seeking meaningful wildlife encounters, cultural experiences, and educational adventures that will create lifelong memories for both children and parents alike.

Detailed 7-Day Family Safari Itinerary

Days 1-3: Samburu National Reserve | 3 Nights

Flight Time: ~1 hour from Nairobi to Samburu

Day 1 - Morning
Our safari starts early morning from Nairobi. Our driver will pick you up from your Nairobi hotel and transfer you and your family to Wilson Airport for your domestic flight to Samburu, where you will check in at Saruni Samburu's family villa for the next 3 nights.

Accommodation & Experience
Saruni Samburu's six luxury villas are open and spacious, heralding spectacular views over Kenya's Northern Frontier District. The warm and welcoming 'Samburu' hospitality makes for an intimate and exceptional family experience. Guests can enjoy the vast panoramas from the dining and lounge area, the infinity pool and the Samburu Wellbeing Space.

Family Activities & Cultural Immersion
You will be guided from start to finish by Samburu warriors who are passionate about their land and their culture, learning first-hand about their fascinating customs and traditions and gaining ancient, local knowledge about the land and the animals. More in store for you during the Warrior Academy session. It is truly an authentic experience for the whole family.

Included Experiences
Your experiences here include day and night game drives, bush meals, guided walks, birdwatching, picnics, Samburu cultural visits and a 30 minutes complimentary neck and back welcome massage once during your stay for each adult.

Activities: Flight to Samburu, afternoon game drive, cultural orientation

Family Highlights: Warrior Academy sessions, cultural interactions, family villa accommodation

Days 4-6: Maasai Mara | 3 Nights

Flight Time: ~1.5 hours from Samburu to Maasai Mara

Day 4 - Morning
Today, you will depart Samburu on your flight in the morning and arrive in the Masai Mara an hour and a half later. You will check in and enjoy a chilled out afternoon to settle into your new surroundings.

Afternoon Game Drive
Enjoy an exclusive game drive later in the afternoon in Mara North Conservancy – over 30,000 acres of plentiful, protected land bordering the Masai Mara National Reserve. Enjoy a completely different and less-explored corner of the Mara ecosystem in total privacy, brimming with wildlife perfect for family viewing.

Day 5 - Full Mara Experience
Early morning game drives to look for the big cats and other predators of the Mara, returning to the camp or lodge for lunch and treatments at the Maasai Wellbeing Space. The conservancy offers exceptional wildlife viewing with fewer vehicles, creating an intimate family safari experience.

Warrior Academy & Cultural Learning
Enroll in our Warrior's Academy for the afternoon. Encounter Africa's storybook wildlife up close and refine your bush skills, as you study the giant crocodiles and hippos of the Mara River. Discover the ancient wisdom of the Maasai learning first-hand about their customs and local knowledge of the animals and landscape and traditional plants used for medicine.

Special Family Experience
Weather permitting, experience a bush dinner in the middle of nature under the vast African sky, with Maasai warriors singing and dancing around the fire – a magical experience the whole family will cherish forever.

Activities: Flight to Maasai Mara, private conservancy game drives, Warrior Academy

Family Highlights: Private conservancy access, Maasai cultural experiences, bush dinners

Day 7: Departure

Flight Time: ~1 hour from Maasai Mara to Nairobi

Morning
Enjoy one final morning game drive or a leisurely breakfast overlooking the Mara plains, taking in the last moments of your incredible family safari adventure.

Transfer & Departure
You will be transferred to the nearby airstrip for your flight back to Nairobi's Wilson Airport. Upon arrival, our representative will meet you and transfer you to either Jomo Kenyatta International Airport for your international flight or to your Nairobi hotel if you're extending your stay.

Farewell
As you depart, you'll carry with you not just photographs, but lifelong memories of wildlife encounters, cultural exchanges, and family bonding moments that will be cherished for generations to come.

Activities: Final game drive, flight to Nairobi

Family Highlights: Last wildlife sightings, reflection on family adventures
