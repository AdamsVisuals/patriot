8-Day Kenya & Tanzania Cross-Border Safari Adventure

Embark on an unforgettable 8-day cross-border safari that takes you through Kenya and Tanzania's most iconic wildlife destinations. This carefully curated journey combines prime wildlife viewing with cultural immersion in Africa's most breathtaking landscapes, from the legendary Maasai Mara to the spectacular Serengeti.

Experience the raw power of nature as you follow the path of millions of wildebeest and zebra during their annual migration. Witness incredible wildlife encounters in both Kenya's Maasai Mara and Tanzania's Serengeti National Park, two of Africa's most famous safari destinations. Each day offers unparalleled game viewing opportunities and cultural experiences.

Our expert guides will share their deep knowledge of animal behavior and migration patterns, ensuring you're positioned for the most incredible wildlife spectacles. With comfortable accommodations and seamless logistics, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking safari experience across two of East Africa's premier wildlife destinations.

This journey is designed for wildlife enthusiasts and photographers seeking to witness nature's greatest events across international borders. Whether you're tracking the Big Five or experiencing Maasai culture, this itinerary offers an authentic African adventure filled with unforgettable moments.

Detailed 8-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Nairobi, Kenya

Welcome to Nairobi, the bustling gateway to East Africa. Upon arrival at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport, our representative will warmly greet you and assist with your transfer to your hotel. Depending on your arrival time, you may relax at your lodge or explore the vibrant city visit the Giraffe Centre, meet orphaned elephants at the David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust, or enjoy an authentic Kenyan dinner at the Carnivore Restaurant.

Your adventure begins here, in the heart of Africa.

Day 2: Journey to Maasai Mara National Reserve

After breakfast, depart for the legendary Maasai Mara National Reserve, one of Africa's most famous safari destinations. Drive through scenic valleys and rolling hills dotted with Maasai villages before arriving at your camp in time for lunch. In the afternoon, head out on your first game drive, where you may encounter elephants, lions, giraffes, and zebras roaming freely across the golden plains.

As the sun sets, return to your lodge for dinner and storytelling around the campfire.

Day 3: Full Day in the Maasai Mara

Today is dedicated to exploring the untamed beauty of the Mara. After an early breakfast, set off for a full-day safari, following the tracks of predators and the herds of the Great Migration (seasonal). The Mara is home to all of Africa's "Big Five" lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, and rhino as well as hundreds of bird species.

In the afternoon, visit a traditional Maasai village, where you'll learn about the customs, dances, and way of life of this proud, colorful tribe.

Day 4: Another Day in the Wild

Rise early for an optional hot air balloon safari (extra cost) a magical experience floating over the Mara at sunrise, watching herds of animals from above as the morning mist fades. After breakfast, spend the day on game drives exploring different corners of the reserve, from open grasslands to acacia woodlands and winding rivers.

Every moment in the Mara brings new surprises a cheetah sprinting across the plains, a lion's roar echoing through the distance, or a herd of elephants crossing the Mara River.

Day 5: From the Mara to the Serengeti (Tanzania)

After breakfast, bid farewell to Kenya as you travel south toward Tanzania. Cross the Isebania border, where your Tanzanian guide will meet you and continue the journey to Serengeti National Park. As you enter the Serengeti, the vast plains seem to stretch endlessly to the horizon a true wilderness teeming with wildlife.

Arrive at your camp in time for dinner and a relaxing evening under the African night sky, filled with the sounds of the bush.

Day 6: Exploring the Serengeti

Enjoy a full day of game drives in the Serengeti, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Africa's greatest wildlife sanctuaries. This is the land of endless plains and dramatic wildlife encounters lions basking on rocks, cheetahs scanning the horizon, and herds of wildebeest grazing as far as the eye can see.

Depending on the time of year, you may witness the Great Migration, one of nature's most extraordinary spectacles.

Day 7: Another Day in the Serengeti

Spend another unforgettable day in the Serengeti, focusing on the central Seronera region, known for its rich concentration of predators. Enjoy picnic lunches in the bush and soak up the sights and sounds of this pristine wilderness.

Your guide will share insights into animal behavior, ecosystems, and conservation efforts that protect this magnificent landscape. As evening falls, return to camp for dinner and reflection on the wonders you've witnessed.

Day 8: Fly to Arusha & Departure

After breakfast, transfer to Seronera Airstrip for your scenic flight to Arusha. On arrival, you'll be met and transferred to your hotel or to Kilimanjaro International Airport for your onward journey.

This marks the end of an extraordinary adventure one that captures the true spirit of Africa, from the rolling savannahs of the Mara to the endless plains of the Serengeti.
