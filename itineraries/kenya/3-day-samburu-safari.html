3 Day Samburu National Reserve Safari

Explore the Famous Samburu National Reserve with its unique wildlife and stunning landscapes

3 Days / 2 Nights | From $2,874

3-Day Samburu National Reserve Safari Experience

Embark on an unforgettable 3-day journey to Samburu National Reserve, one of Kenya's most unique and fascinating wildlife destinations renowned for its distinctive northern species and dramatic semi-arid landscapes. This carefully curated safari offers an immersive experience in one of Africa's most specialized ecosystems, where the Ewaso Ng'iro River creates an oasis teeming with wildlife in an otherwise arid environment.

Experience the raw beauty of the African wilderness as you encounter the "Samburu Special Five" - rare species found only in northern Kenya including the reticulated giraffe, Grevy's zebra, Beisa oryx, Somali ostrich, and gerenuk. Track predators and elephants across the reserve's rugged terrain and witness the incredible adaptation of wildlife to this unique environment.

Our expert guides will share their deep knowledge of the ecosystem and wildlife behaviors, ensuring you gain profound insights into Samburu's natural wonders. With comfortable accommodation at the luxurious Samburu Intrepids, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking safari experience while enjoying premium amenities and service by the peaceful Uaso Nyiro River.

This comprehensive journey is perfect for wildlife enthusiasts seeking unique species, photographers, and those looking for an authentic African adventure combined with stunning semi-arid landscapes. Whether you're captivated by rare wildlife, riverine ecosystems, or simply seeking a memorable safari experience, this itinerary offers a perfect introduction to Kenya's northern wildlife wonders.

Detailed 3-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Nairobi to Samburu National Reserve

Distance/Time: ~350 km | Approx. 5-6 hours drive from Nairobi

Morning
Start your early morning journey from Nairobi to the breathtaking Samburu National Reserve. As you drive, take in the stunning views of Kenya's lush countryside and quaint farms. You'll also get a glimpse of Mount Kenya, the country's tallest peak, on your way.

The journey north takes you through changing landscapes, from the central highlands into the drier northern frontier district, offering a fascinating transition in scenery and climate as you approach this unique wildlife sanctuary.

Afternoon
You'll arrive just in time for lunch at Samburu Intrepids, a luxurious spot by the peaceful Uaso Nyiro River within the reserve. After a satisfying meal and some downtime, gear up for thrilling game drives through this incredible wildlife area.

On these drives, you'll have an amazing chance to see animals like zebras, lions, cheetahs, elephants, giraffes—and even rare species like Somali Ostriches with their unique blue legs. Enjoy every moment as you witness nature up close.

Evening
By evening, head back to your lodge where you're treated to a delicious dinner that promises culinary delight; then relax around a cozy bonfire or unwind under Africa's starry skies for an unforgettable night.

Activities: Scenic drive to Samburu, afternoon game drive

What to Expect: First views of Mount Kenya, introduction to Samburu's unique ecosystem, and initial sightings of the reserve's special wildlife species.

Day 2: Samburu National Reserve

Full Day Exploration

Morning
Start your day with an early morning game drive in the reserve, when the cool dawn hours offer a great chance to see wildlife at their liveliest. This is prime time for spotting animals as they come out from their nighttime hideaways.

The morning light creates perfect conditions for photography and wildlife observation, with predators often active and the reserve's unique species beginning their daily routines along the riverbanks and acacia woodlands.

Midday
Afterward, head back to camp for a tasty breakfast and enjoy some downtime in the peaceful surroundings and amenities of our camp, like the swimming pool. The camp's location by the river provides excellent opportunities to watch wildlife coming to drink while you relax.

Enjoy lunch at the camp while taking in the spectacular views of the surrounding wilderness and the wildlife activity along the river.

Afternoon
After lunch, get ready for another exciting adventure with an afternoon game drive through the reserve. You'll have rare opportunities to see unique wildlife such as reticulated giraffes, Grevy's zebras, Beisa oryxes, Somali ostriches, gerenuks, and vulturine guineafowls—animals you won't find just anywhere.

The afternoon drive also offers chances to spot elephants, lions, leopards, and the diverse birdlife that thrives in this unique ecosystem, with over 450 species recorded in the reserve.

Evening
As evening falls, return to your lodge for a delicious dinner followed by relaxing moments under a sky full of stars. Thanks to Samburu's location near the equator, you'll be treated to stunning views of starry skies—a perfect end to an amazing day on safari.

Activities: Early morning game drive, afternoon game drive, camp relaxation

What to Expect: Optimal wildlife viewing conditions, sightings of the "Samburu Special Five," excellent birdwatching opportunities, and spectacular stargazing.

Day 3: Samburu National Reserve to Nairobi

Distance/Time: ~350 km | Approx. 5-6 hours drive back to Nairobi

Morning
Today marks the bittersweet conclusion of your awe-inspiring safari adventure, a journey filled with breath-taking landscapes and unforgettable wildlife encounters. After indulging in a delightful breakfast, you'll set off on a scenic drive back to the vibrant city of Nairobi.

As you traverse the picturesque roads, take a moment to soak in the panoramic views of rolling hills and lush greenery that characterize this beautiful region. The morning departure allows for potential final wildlife sightings as you exit the reserve.

Midday
The return journey gives you time to reflect on your incredible experiences with the unique wildlife of northern Kenya and the unforgettable moments spent in this specialized ecosystem.

Afternoon
Upon your arrival in Nairobi, prepare to tantalize your taste buds with a delectable lunch at the renowned Carnivore Restaurant. This iconic dining establishment is famous for its unique concept of serving a variety of succulent meats, grilled to perfection and carved right at your table. The experience is not just about eating; it's about savouring an unforgettable culinary adventure that highlights the rich flavours of African cuisine.

Departure
After this delightful gastronomic treat, you'll have two options: you can choose to be escorted to your hotel for some much-deserved relaxation after an action-packed trip or head directly to the airport for your onward journey home. Whichever path you take, may it be filled with safe travels and wonderful memories as you transition from this remarkable adventure back into everyday life!

Activities: Final game viewing during departure, scenic return journey, lunch at Carnivore Restaurant

What to Expect: Last wildlife sightings, scenic landscapes, and a memorable dining experience at one of Africa's most famous restaurants.
