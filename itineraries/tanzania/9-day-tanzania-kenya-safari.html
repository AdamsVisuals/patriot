10 Day Kenya Tanzania Safari

Experience the best of East Africa with this epic cross-border safari through Kenya's Masai Mara and Tanzania's Serengeti, Ngorongoro Crater, and Amboseli National Park.

10 Days / 9 Nights | From $3,085

10-Day Kenya Tanzania Cross-Border Safari

Embark on an unforgettable 10-day cross-border safari adventure that combines the best of Kenya and Tanzania's most iconic wildlife destinations. This epic journey takes you through world-famous national parks and conservation areas, offering a comprehensive East African safari experience that will leave you with memories to last a lifetime.

Experience the raw beauty of the African wilderness as you explore Kenya's Masai Mara, home to the Great Migration and abundant wildlife. Cross into Tanzania to discover the endless plains of the Serengeti, descend into the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater—a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and explore the diverse ecosystems of Lake Manyara National Park. Return to Kenya to witness the majestic elephants of Amboseli National Park against the backdrop of Mount Kilimanjaro.

This cross-border adventure offers unique cultural experiences, including optional visits to Maasai villages and the historic Olduvai Gorge archaeological site. Each day brings new wildlife encounters, breathtaking landscapes, and opportunities to witness Africa's most iconic animals in their natural habitats.

This journey is designed for adventurous travelers seeking a comprehensive East African experience. With comfortable accommodations and expert guides throughout, you'll explore two countries and multiple ecosystems while creating unforgettable safari memories.

Detailed 10-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival Day - Nairobi

Arrive at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO) in Nairobi, where you'll be warmly welcomed by our representative and transferred to your accommodation. After check-in, you'll have time to relax and settle in after your journey. In the evening, enjoy a welcome dinner followed by a safari briefing to prepare you for the exciting adventure ahead. Overnight stay at your Nairobi hotel.

Accommodation

Villa Rosa Kempiski

Comfortable accommodation with all amenities

Day 2: Nairobi - Masai Mara National Reserve (265 km, 5 to 6 hours)

Your African safari adventure begins at 7:30 am when we pick you up from your hotel, starting the journey towards the world-famous Masai Mara. On the way, you'll observe the beautiful Kenyan countryside, passing through villages, vast plains, and stunning mountain views. We will stop for a traditional Kenyan lunch before continuing to Masai Mara. Upon arrival, you'll immediately embark on your first thrilling game drive, where you can start spotting the abundant wildlife. From elephants and lions to baboons and buffalo, the park's diverse inhabitants will offer you your first glimpses of Africa's wild animals. As dusk sets in, you'll be taken to your accommodation for dinner and rest.

Accommodation Options

Masai Mara Luxury Lodge

Luxury • Premium accommodation with excellent game viewing

Day 3: Full Day in Masai Mara National Reserve

After an early breakfast, you'll head into the heart of the Masai Mara, a land shared by the Maasai people and a plethora of wildlife. During the morning game drive, your guide will assist in spotting iconic animals, including the Big Five—lions, elephants, buffalo, rhinos, and leopards—along with other fascinating species that inhabit this incredible park. Lunch will be a picnic in the park, after which you'll have the chance to visit a traditional Maasai village (optional activity, with an extra fee of $20 per person). Here, you'll experience their vibrant culture, greeted with traditional songs and dances, and explore their homes and way of life. In the evening, you'll be taken back to your accommodation for dinner and a restful night.

Accommodation Options

Masai Mara Sopa Lodge

Masai Mara Luxury Lodge

Day 4: Masai Mara National Reserve to Serengeti National Park (370 km, 6 to 7 hours)

An early rise gives you the opportunity to experience the savanna in the soft light of dawn. After your game drive, enjoy breakfast before heading toward the Isbania border, where you'll cross into Tanzania. As you drive through the scenic Tanzanian countryside towards the Ikoma Gate of Serengeti National Park, you'll have a game drive en route, giving you the chance to spot animals on your journey. Upon arrival at your lodge or camp, you can relax with a delicious dinner before resting for the night.

Accommodation Options

Acacia Central Camp

Mid-Range • Comfortable tented camp in Serengeti

Day 5: Full Day in Serengeti National Park

Today, you have the option to choose between a full-day game drive or a morning game drive followed by a break and an afternoon/evening game drive. Both options will provide ample time to witness the incredible wildlife of the Serengeti, including the Big Five, zebras, giraffes, hippos, cheetahs, and much more. Lunch will be served at your accommodation if you opt for the morning and evening safari or as a picnic in the park if you decide to explore for the full day. After your game drive, you'll return to your camp or lodge for dinner.

Accommodation Options

Acacia Central Camp

Mid-Range • Comfortable tented camp in Serengeti

Day 6: Serengeti National Park to Ngorongoro Crater (160 km, 3 to 4 hours)

After breakfast, you'll begin your journey to the Ngorongoro Crater. Along the way, you can visit the historic Olduvai Gorge, an archaeological site known for its significance in the study of early humans (extra fee of $45 per person). Lunch will be served as a picnic during the journey, and upon reaching Ngorongoro Crater, you'll embark on an afternoon game drive. This unique, biodiverse area is home to a variety of animals, including buffalo, wildebeest, zebras, waterbucks, and gazelles, all within the stunning caldera. You'll spend the night at your chosen accommodation either inside or on the rim of the crater.

Accommodation Options

Ngorongoro Farm Lodge

Mid-Range • Comfortable lodge with farm experience

Day 7: Ngorongoro Crater - Lake Manyara National Park (64 km, 1 to 2 hours)

Start your day with breakfast before heading to Lake Manyara, passing through the breathtaking Great African Rift Valley. Lunch will be served picnic-style along the way, surrounded by nature. Lake Manyara is known for its alkaline waters and lush surroundings, creating a different atmosphere compared to previous parks. The abundant tree cover may allow you to spot the park's famous tree-climbing lions. Other wildlife, including baboons, elephants, and hippos, can also be seen here. In the evening, you'll return to your accommodation for dinner and rest.

Accommodation Options

Manyara Wildlife Safari Camp

Mid-Range • Comfortable tented camp near Lake Manyara

Day 8: Lake Manyara National Park to Amboseli National Park (315 km, 5 to 6 hours)

After breakfast, you'll leave Lake Manyara and head to Arusha for a lunch break, marking the end of your safari in Tanzania. From here, you'll cross the Namanga border back into Kenya and proceed to Amboseli National Park, famous for its views of Mount Kilimanjaro. On your way to your accommodation, you'll be taken on a short game drive, where you can spot the iconic wildlife of Amboseli. Dinner will be served at your lodge or camp.

Accommodation Options

Amboseli Sopa Lodge

Mid-Range • Comfortable lodge with mountain views

Day 9: Full Day in Amboseli National Park

After breakfast, you'll set out for a day of exploration across Amboseli, with your expert guide helping you track and identify the many species that inhabit the park. The highlight of the park is its population of elephants, along with zebras, giraffes, lions, and hippos. Lunch will be served picnic-style in the park, giving you a chance to take in the sights and sounds of the African savanna. After a full day of safari, return to your accommodation for dinner and an evening of relaxation.

Accommodation Options

Amboseli Sopa Lodge

Mid-Range • Comfortable lodge with mountain views

Day 10: Amboseli National Park to Nairobi (250 km, 4 to 5 hours)

On your final day of safari, you'll wake early to watch the sun rise over the plains, with the snow-capped peak of Mount Kilimanjaro in the distance. This is your last opportunity to enjoy the incredible wildlife of Africa before departing. After your final game drive, you'll begin your journey back to Nairobi, where you'll enjoy a traditional lunch on the way. Upon arrival in Nairobi, your safari experience concludes, and you can request a drop-off at your hotel, residence, or the airport, depending on your travel plans.

End of Itinerary
