6 Day Tanzania Wildlife Safari

Experience the wild beauty of Tanzania's most iconic national parks, from Tarangire's elephant herds to the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater.

6 Days / 5 Nights | From $2,695

6-Day Tanzania Classic Safari Experience

Embark on an unforgettable 6-day journey through Tanzania's most iconic wildlife destinations, offering the perfect introduction to the country's incredible biodiversity and stunning landscapes. This carefully curated safari combines the best of northern Tanzania's national parks, providing exceptional wildlife viewing opportunities and cultural experiences.

Experience the majestic elephant herds of Tarangire National Park, explore the cultural richness of Mto wa Mbu, witness the tree-climbing lions of Lake Manyara, marvel at the endless plains of the Serengeti, and descend into the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater—one of the world's largest intact volcanic calderas and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Each day brings new wildlife encounters and breathtaking landscapes that will leave you in awe. From the towering baobab trees of Tarangire to the dramatic Rift Valley escarpment, and from the vast Serengeti plains to the enclosed paradise of Ngorongoro Crater, this itinerary offers a comprehensive Tanzanian safari experience.

This journey is designed for wildlife enthusiasts, photographers, and families seeking an authentic African adventure. With comfortable accommodations and expert guides throughout, you'll create memories that will last a lifetime while exploring Tanzania's most celebrated natural wonders.

Detailed 6-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrive Arusha

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), you will be warmly welcomed by your guide and transferred to your accommodation. After check-in, you'll have time to relax and settle in. In the evening, enjoy a delicious dinner followed by a pre-safari briefing to prepare you for the exciting journey ahead. Overnight stay at your lodge.

Day 2: Arusha – Tarangire - Karatu

After an early breakfast, your safari adventure begins! You'll depart from your accommodation and drive approximately 120 km (around 2 hours) to Tarangire National Park, famous for its towering baobab trees, large elephant herds, and diverse wildlife. Spend the day exploring the park's scenic landscapes and enjoying thrilling game drives, with a packed lunch provided to maximize your time in the wild. In the late afternoon, you'll check out of Tarangire and continue your journey, zigzagging up the dramatic Rift Valley wall towards the Crater Highlands. The drive to your lodge in the Crater Highlands covers about 150 km (roughly 3 hours). Upon arrival, settle in, savor a hearty dinner, and unwind overnight, surrounded by the cool, elevated atmosphere of the highlands.

Day 3: Karatu – Lake Manyara – Karatu

You will depart early in the morning for a cultural exploration in Mto wa Mbu, a vibrant town known for its rich cultural diversity and local traditions. Here, you will have the opportunity to interact with different ethnic communities, visit local markets, see traditional banana plantations, and learn about local craftsmanship, offering a unique glimpse into the daily life of Tanzanian people. By early afternoon, you will enter Lake Manyara National Park for an exciting game drive with a picnic lunch. This beautiful park is famous for its breathtaking scenery, lush groundwater forests, and diverse wildlife. The alkaline Lake Manyara itself attracts thousands of flamingos, while the surrounding park is home to elephants, giraffes, hippos, and the famous tree-climbing lions. As you drive through the park, you will witness the incredible variety of birdlife and stunning landscapes that make this a must-visit destination. In the late afternoon, drive back to crater highlands for dinner and overnight.

Day 4: Karatu – Central Serengeti

Starts early with a departure to Central Serengeti [approx. 335 km, 7-8 hours driving time, including stops and game drives]. The journey traverses the scenic Crater Highlands and the Ngorongoro Conservation Area, offering glimpses of breathtaking landscapes. The day is primarily dedicated to game drives and exploration within Central Serengeti. A packed lunch will be enjoyed en route, with dinner and overnight at your camp, nestled within the heart of the park.

Day 5: Central Serengeti

You will spend the day exploring the vast and wildlife-rich landscapes of Central Serengeti through further game drives. This region is teeming with diverse wildlife, including large prides of lions, elusive leopards resting in acacia trees, and massive herds of elephants roaming the plains. The Seronera River, a lifeline for many animals, attracts a variety of species, making it a prime location for wildlife viewing. As you traverse the open savannah and rocky outcrops, you will have the chance to witness the daily dramas of the wild, from predator hunts to herds of grazers moving across the plains. After a full day of adventure, you will return to your camp to relax, enjoy a delicious dinner, and spend another night immersed in the serenity of the Serengeti.

Day 6: Central Serengeti – Ngorongoro Crater – Kilimanjaro Airport

Begins with an early breakfast and a game drive en route to the Ngorongoro Crater [approx. 180 km, 3-4 hours driving time], a UNESCO World Heritage Site. This natural wonder is a massive caldera, a collapsed volcano, home to an incredible concentration of wildlife, often referred to as "Africa's Garden of Eden." The day includes a descent into the crater floor for a unique game drive experience, accompanied by a picnic lunch amidst this breathtaking landscape. Late afternoon sees the ascent back up the crater rim, followed by a drive back to Arusha [approx. 3 hrs 37 min, 214.5 km] for your international flight onward.
