2 Day Saadani National Park Safari

Where the beach meets the bush - Experience Tanzania's only coastal wildlife sanctuary

2 Days / 1 Night | From $1,450

2-Day Saadani National Park Safari

Embark on an unforgettable 2-day safari adventure to Saadani National Park, Tanzania's only wildlife sanctuary with an Indian Ocean beachfront. This unique destination offers a spectacular blend of classic safari experiences and tropical coastal beauty, creating an unparalleled African adventure where the beach truly meets the bush.

Experience the thrill of game drives through diverse habitats while searching for giraffes, buffalo, warthogs, and numerous antelope species, all against the backdrop of the sparkling Indian Ocean. The highlight of your journey will be the combination of traditional safari activities with coastal experiences, including boat safaris along the Wami River and opportunities to spot dolphins in the ocean.

Our expert guides will share their extensive knowledge of this unique ecosystem, where wildlife roams freely just meters from the beach. With comfortable lodge accommodation and seamless logistics, you can focus entirely on this immersive coastal wilderness experience that combines the best of safari and beach in one extraordinary destination.

This compact journey is perfect for travelers seeking a unique safari experience, beach lovers, and those wanting to combine wildlife viewing with coastal relaxation. Experience the magic of Saadani's unique "bush and beach" environment in an efficient 2-day itinerary.

Detailed 2-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Dar es Salaam to Saadani National Park

Distance/Time: ~120 km | Approx. 3-4 hours drive from Dar es Salaam

Morning
Depart Dar es Salaam at 8:00am to Saadani National Park with lunch packed. Saadani is where the beach meets the bush - the only wildlife sanctuary in East Africa to boast an Indian Ocean beachfront. It possesses all the attributes that make Tanzania's tropical coastline and islands so popular with European sun-worshippers, yet it is also a thriving national park with abundant wildlife.

Afternoon
Upon arrival, enjoy your first introduction to this unique park where wildlife roams just meters from the beach. Check into your chosen accommodation and take some time to settle in and appreciate the stunning coastal setting.

Evening
Enjoy sunset game viewing in the park as the golden light bathes the landscape. Watch as animals come to drink at waterholes and rivers, creating perfect photographic opportunities. Return to your lodge for dinner and overnight, listening to the sounds of the African bush mixed with the gentle rhythm of ocean waves.

Activities: Scenic drive to Saadani, sunset game drive

What to Expect: Introduction to Saadani's unique ecosystem, wildlife sightings against coastal backdrop, and comfortable accommodation.

Day 2: Full Day Saadani National Park and Return to Dar es Salaam

Distance/Time: ~120 km | Approx. 3-4 hours drive to Dar es Salaam

Morning
Early morning sunrise game viewing when wildlife is most active. The cool morning hours provide excellent opportunities to spot animals as they begin their daily activities. Return to your camp/lodge for a delicious breakfast overlooking the ocean or wilderness.

Full Day Activities
Enjoy a full day of diverse activities including game drives and boat safaris with a picnic lunch. A surprisingly wide range of grazers and primates can be seen, among them giraffe, buffalo, warthog, common waterbuck, reedbuck, hartebeest, wildebeest, red duiker, greater kudu, eland, sable antelope, yellow baboon and velvet monkey.

Coastal Experiences
You can enjoy the beach of the Indian Ocean, but also go out for a sea trip to the sand banks off-shore which provides good snorkeling and some chances of meeting dolphins. This unique combination of safari and beach activities makes Saadani truly special.

Departure
Later after your spectacular game drive and coastal experiences, you will set off to Dar es Salaam where you will be dropped at the agreed point, filled with incredible memories of your unique "bush and beach" safari adventure.

Activities: Sunrise game drive, boat safari, beach activities, snorkeling

What to Expect: Diverse wildlife sightings, coastal experiences, and scenic return journey.

Meal Plan

Breakfast, Lunch
