4 Day Family Safari Adventure

Experience the wild beauty of Tanzania with your family, exploring the magnificent Serengeti plains and the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater.

4 Days / 3 Nights | From $1,200

4-Day Serengeti & Ngorongoro Family Safari

Embark on an unforgettable 4-day family adventure that combines Tanzania's most iconic wildlife destinations into a perfect family-friendly safari experience. This carefully curated itinerary is designed with families in mind, offering exciting wildlife encounters, comfortable accommodations, and memories that will last a lifetime.

Experience the raw beauty of the African wilderness as you explore the endless plains of the Serengeti, home to the Great Migration during certain seasons. Witness incredible wildlife spectacles, from majestic lions and graceful giraffes to enormous elephant herds and elusive leopards. The Serengeti offers endless opportunities for family wildlife viewing and photography.

Your adventure continues at the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater, one of the world's largest intact volcanic calderas and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Descend into this natural wonder to encounter the Big Five in their natural habitat, all within the safety of this enclosed ecosystem. The crater's steep walls create a natural sanctuary that's perfect for family wildlife viewing.

This journey is designed for families seeking adventure, education, and quality time together in nature. With expert guides who understand family dynamics and comfortable accommodations suitable for all ages, this itinerary offers the perfect introduction to Tanzania's wildlife wonders.

Detailed 4-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Drive to Serengeti National Park

We head towards the Serengeti National Park. We will arrive at lunch time and enjoy our packed lunches. After lunch, we drive into the Serengeti itself and the rest of the afternoon is spent game viewing in the vast plains that are broken only by stands of acacia trees and the occasional kopje. In June, July and August we will normally meet a number of animals in the pack since the pack is at its driest level so it's easy to spot wildlife since the grass is shorter and as you drive to some water sources you can see animals, such as giraffe, buffalo, and elephant, and many large predators such as lion, cheetah, and leopard. Later we drive to our special tented camp/lodge for the night.

Accommodation

Serena Safari Lodge

Day 2: Game Drive in Serengeti National Park

We have another full day in this wonderful park, rising early to make the most of our time. Our guides will choose the best spotting locations for the time of year; we can spend time at the Hippo pool watching these majestic animals laze about in the cool water happily living alongside the crocodiles, watch a big pride of lions, be in the middle of the migration, sometimes surrounded by wildebeest, sometimes by zebras, which travel with the wildebeest. We journey from the wide open plains to the kopjes: these volcanic rocky outcrops provide protection and shelter for a wide variety of animals and from the top, we can look out across the vast grasslands of the Serengeti. This diverse and interesting landscape will provide us with the ultimate in game viewing, we will hopefully see all of the plain games; elephants, giraffe, zebra, wildebeest, lions and if you are lucky the elusive leopard and cheetah, as well as huge amounts of interesting birdlife, from the elegant secretary birds to the flightless ostrich. We return to our special tented camp for the night.

Accommodation

Serena Safari Lodge

Mid-Range • Family-friendly lodge with excellent facilities

Day 3: Serengeti to Ngorongoro Conservation Area

Early morning game drive in the Serengeti plains trying to capture what was missed on yesterday's drive. Enjoy an unforgettable sunrise game drive across the Serengeti and get a chance to appreciate the view as the sun set fire to the savannah while predators such as leopards and lions hunt for their morning meal. In the afternoon we will drive to Ngorongoro Conservation Area. Dinner and Overnight in Ngorongoro Conservation Area.

Accommodation

Ngorongoro Farm House Lodge

Mid-Range • Comfortable lodge with farm experience

Day 4: Ngorongoro Crater and Departure Day

On this day you will drive to fascinating volcanic landscape, the Ngorongoro Crater known as the largest unbroken caldera, you will drive around the rim of the Crater itself, and from here we can look down and see the herd of wildebeest or buffalos. Finally, you descend into the crater. The crater walls provide a natural sanctuary for the animals, which means there is plenty to see: lions, elephants, buffalo, rhino and leopard (if you are lucky). The only animal you won't see in here is a giraffe, whose long legs are unable to cope with the steeps sides of the crater. In the afternoon you will be driven to the airport for your flight back Home.

End of Itinerary
