7 Day Southern Tanzania Safari

Experience the wild beauty of Nyerere, Ruaha, and Mikumi National Parks with diverse wildlife and stunning landscapes

7 Days / 6 Nights | From $2,725

7-Day Southern Tanzania Safari

Embark on an unforgettable 7-day journey through three of Tanzania's most spectacular wildlife destinations - Nyerere National Park, Ruaha National Park, and Mikumi National Park. This carefully curated safari offers an authentic African experience across diverse ecosystems, from riverine forests to baobab-studded plains.

Experience the raw beauty of the African wilderness as you track the Big Five in Nyerere National Park, explore Tanzania's largest national park in Ruaha with its dramatic landscapes and rare antelope species, and discover the accessible wildlife haven of Mikumi. Witness the incredible biodiversity of the Rufiji River system, the Great Ruaha River, and vast savannahs teeming with wildlife.

Our expert guides will share their extensive knowledge of the ecosystems and wildlife behaviors, ensuring you gain deep insights into Tanzania's natural wonders. With comfortable accommodations and seamless logistics including domestic flights, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking safari experience across these three distinct wilderness areas.

This comprehensive journey is perfect for those seeking an authentic African adventure combined with the comfort of well-appointed lodges and camps. Experience the best of southern Tanzania's wildlife in an immersive week-long itinerary.

Detailed 7-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrive Dar es Salaam

Upon your arrival at Nyerere International Airport (time to be advised), you will be warmly welcomed by your guide, who will assist you with your luggage and ensure a smooth transfer to your accommodation. As you settle in, you will have the opportunity to relax after your journey and take in the surroundings. In the evening, a delicious dinner will be served, allowing you to savor local flavors and prepare for the exciting days ahead. After dinner, you can unwind and rest comfortably, enjoying a peaceful overnight stay in preparation for the adventures to come.

Accommodation Options

Day 2: Dar es Salaam – Nyerere National Park

We begin our adventure with an early morning departure from Dar es Salaam, heading southwest to Nyerere National Park, formerly known as the Selous Game Reserve. The journey spans about 230 kilometers and takes approximately 3 to 4 hours, including time for stops and park entrance formalities.

Upon arrival, we enter the park and begin our first exciting game drive, exploring the diverse habitats ranging from woodlands and grasslands to the river systems of the mighty Rufiji River. Nyerere is one of Africa's richest ecosystems and home to the Big Five, including the elusive black rhino. We enjoy a picnic lunch inside the park to maximize our time in the wild.

In the late afternoon, we head to our lodge or tented camp within the park for dinner and an overnight stay, immersed in the untouched beauty of Tanzania's wilderness.

Day 3: Nyerere National Park

We spend the entire day immersed in further game drives and exploration within the vast expanse of Nyerere National Park. This park, with its rich network of rivers, open plains, and dense woodlands, offers some of the most diverse wildlife sightings in Tanzania.

As we continue our search for the Big Five, including the elusive black rhino, we also have the chance to encounter wild dogs, hippos, crocodiles, and a wide variety of bird species, particularly around the Rufiji River. The day includes a picnic lunch in one of the designated areas, allowing us to remain deep in the bush without returning to camp.

After an exciting day of wildlife encounters and scenic landscapes, we return to our accommodation within the park for a well-earned dinner and overnight stay, enjoying the serenity of the African wilderness.

Day 4: Nyerere National Park – Ruaha National Park

After an early breakfast, we will depart from Nyerere National Park and begin our journey towards Ruaha National Park. The drive is long and scenic, covering approximately 600 km, which can take around 10 to 12 hours, depending on road conditions and stops along the way.

We will carry packed lunches to enjoy en route while taking in the diverse landscapes of southern Tanzania, transitioning from the lush lowlands of Nyerere to the rugged, semi-arid terrain of Ruaha. Upon entering Ruaha, we'll begin our first game drive within the park, offering a chance to spot elephants, lions, and other wildlife unique to this vast wilderness.

As the sun begins to set, we'll game-drive our way to our special camp inside the park, arriving in time for a warm dinner and overnight stay surrounded by the sounds of the African bush.

Day 5: Ruaha National Park

Today is dedicated to exploring the vast and untamed wilderness of Ruaha National Park, one of Tanzania's largest and most remote safari destinations. After an early morning wake-up, we will head deep into the bush with our packed lunch, allowing for an uninterrupted day of game drives and wildlife viewing.

Spanning over 20,000 square kilometers, Ruaha offers a dramatic landscape of baobab-studded plains, rocky escarpments, and the winding Great Ruaha River — a lifeline that attracts a high concentration of wildlife. Throughout the day, we will search for iconic species such as elephants, buffalo, lions, leopards, and possibly even the endangered African wild dog. Bird enthusiasts will also enjoy the park's rich avian diversity.

As the day winds down, we'll make our way back to the lodge or camp in the early evening for a well-deserved dinner and a restful overnight stay under the stars.

Day 6: Ruaha National Park

Today is fully dedicated to game drives and exploration within the vast and remote Ruaha National Park, Tanzania's largest national park and one of its best-kept secrets. Covering over 20,000 square kilometers, Ruaha is famed for its dramatic landscapes, baobab-studded plains, and the winding Great Ruaha River, which attracts a wide variety of wildlife.

As we venture deeper into the park, we search for its iconic inhabitants, including large prides of lions, herds of elephants, leopards, and rare species such as the sable and roan antelopes. The park is also a birdwatcher's paradise, with over 570 bird species recorded. We enjoy a picnic lunch inside the park to maximize our time in the bush.

After a thrilling day of discovery and photography, we return to our lodge or tented camp for dinner and an overnight stay, surrounded by the natural sounds of the African wilderness.

Day 7: Travel Day

After an early morning breakfast and checkout from your camp or lodge in Ruaha National Park, we prepare for your departure. You'll be transferred to the nearby Ruaha airstrip, a scenic drive through the park that may offer final glimpses of wildlife along the way.

From the airstrip, you will board a domestic flight back to Dar es Salaam, marking the end of your unforgettable southern Tanzania safari. As you soar above the rugged terrain, take a moment to reflect on the extraordinary landscapes and wildlife encounters that made this journey truly special.
