19 Day Kenya Tanzania Zanzibar

Explore Kenya's top wildlife destinations, Tanzania's Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater, and relax on Zanzibar's beaches in this 19-day East African safari.

19 Days / 18 Nights | From $8,890

19-Day Kenya Tanzania Zanzibar Safari

Embark on the ultimate 19-day East African adventure that combines thrilling wildlife safaris with a relaxing beach escape. This comprehensive journey takes you through Kenya's most famous wildlife destinations, Tanzania's iconic Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater, and ends with a blissful beach stay on the exotic island of Zanzibar.

Begin your adventure in Nairobi with a full day exploring the Giraffe Centre, David Sheldrick Elephant Orphanage, and Nairobi National Park. Continue to Lake Nakuru to witness the famous flamingos and rhinos, then experience the magical Lake Naivasha with an evening boat ride. Immerse yourself in the wildlife spectacle of Masai Mara National Reserve for three unforgettable days.

Cross into Tanzania to explore the endless plains of Serengeti National Park for two full days, descend into the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater, discover the tree-climbing lions of Lake Manyara, and experience the elephant herds of Tarangire National Park. Your adventure culminates with five relaxing days on the beautiful beaches of Zanzibar, where you can unwind after your safari or explore the island's rich culture and history.

This journey offers accommodation options ranging from budget to luxury, ensuring every traveler can experience the best of East Africa in comfort and style. With expert guides throughout and seamless transfers between destinations, this is the ultimate East African experience.

Arrive at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport, Nairobi, where you'll be met by our representative. You'll then be briefed and afterwards transferred to the hotel where you'll relax and rest after your journey. Enjoy a comfortable overnight stay in Nairobi.

Day 2: Full-Day Nairobi: Giraffe Centre, Elephant Orphanage, Nairobi National Park

Visit three Nairobi wildlife destinations on a private tour that goes from conservation work to a national park with free-roaming animals. It's the chance to see Kenya's magnificent giraffes, elephants, and other wildlife without traveling to distant game reserves. In just one day, tour Giraffe Manor, the David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust, and Nairobi National Park, where you'll set out for an afternoon game drive in a safari van. See wildlife up close at two wildlife conservation landmarks, watch for free-roaming wildlife on an afternoon game drive. Convenient pickup and drop-off at Nairobi hotels with private tour ensuring personalized service from the driver.

Afternoon at leisure and have an evening game in the plains of Masai Mara in search of the big five and other game which roam freely at the plains of Mara. Dinner and overnight at the Camp/lodge.

Days 9 & 10: 2 Full Days in Serengeti National Park

We have 2 full days in this wonderful park, rising early to make the most of our time. Our guides will choose the best spotting locations for the time of year; we can spend time at the Hippo pool watching these majestic animals laze about in the cool water happily living alongside the crocodiles, watch a big pride of lions, be in the middle of the migration, sometimes surrounded by wildebeest, sometimes by zebras, which travel with the wildebeest. We journey from the wide open plains to the kopjes: these volcanic rocky outcrops provide protection and shelter for a wide variety of animals and from the top, we can look out across the vast grasslands of the Serengeti. This diverse and interesting landscape will provide us with the ultimate in game viewing, we will hopefully see all of the plain games; elephants, giraffe, zebra, wildebeest, lions and if you are lucky the elusive leopard and cheetah, as well as huge amounts of interesting birdlife, from the elegant secretary birds to the flightless ostrich. We return to our special Camp for the night.

Day 11: Ngorongoro Crater

After breakfast, taking picnic lunches with you and drive to Ngorongoro Crater descend down to the crater floor for a full day of tracking game with your guide. The crater is 610m deep and 260km² wide, making it the largest Caldera in the world. The crater contains all the big five animals (lions, elephants, rhinos, leopard and buffaloes), hippos, cheetah, antelopes and spotted hyena. Also in the crater you can see different species of birds. Other game such as velvet monkey, jackal, foxes, rock and tree hyraxes, warthog, gnu, zebra and hare. After your amazing game drive from Ngorongoro Crater to Lake Manyara for your Dinner and Overnight.

Day 12: Lake Manyara National Park

After breakfast follows pick up and drive to Lake Manyara National Park with lunch. The park is home to the tree climbing Lions, Hippo, Elephants, Zebras, Buffalos, Giraffes, large groups of Baboons, Blue and Velvet monkey, Warthog and more. About 350 species of birds have been recorded. The Manyara National park is also famous for its large flocks of flamingos. You will start with a treetop walkway experience - this is a great way to see the park from a new perspective. Later proceed with game drive and after the game you will be transferred to the airport and connect you with your flight to Zanzibar.

Day 13: Tarangire National Park to Zanzibar

Drive in the Park after early breakfast. Tarangire National Park is known for its elephant herds of up to 300, migratory wildebeest, zebra, buffalo, impala, gazelle, hartebeest and eland grazing and browsing in the dry river bed. There is Silale swamp that attracts 550 varieties of birds, the most breeding species you'll see in one habitat anywhere in the world! The park is also home of tree climbing pythons, as well as lions and leopards. You will enjoy game drive and game viewing with picnic lunch. Later transferred to the airport for your flight to Zanzibar. Upon your arrival in Zanzibar you will meet our representatives who will pick you up and drive you to hotel for dinner and overnight.
