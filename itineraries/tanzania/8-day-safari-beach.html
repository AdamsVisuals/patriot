8 Day Tanzania & Beach Getaway

Experience the wild beauty of Serengeti and Relax at the prestine Beaches of zanzibar

8 Days / 7 Nights | From $2,570

8-Day Tanzania Safari & Zanzibar Beach Experience

Embark on an unforgettable 8-day journey that combines the best of Tanzania's wildlife safari with the pristine beaches of Zanzibar. This carefully curated experience takes you from the vast plains of the Serengeti and the magnificent Ngorongoro Crater to the tropical paradise of Zanzibar's white sandy beaches.

Experience the raw beauty of the African wilderness as you track wildlife across Tanzania's most iconic national parks, then transition to the turquoise waters and rich cultural heritage of Zanzibar. Each day brings new adventures, from thrilling game drives to relaxing beach activities.

Our expert guides will share their extensive knowledge of the ecosystems and wildlife behaviors, ensuring you gain deep insights into Tanzania's natural wonders. With comfortable accommodations and seamless logistics, you can focus entirely on this diverse and breathtaking experience.

This journey is designed for those seeking the perfect balance between authentic African adventure and tropical beach relaxation. Whether you're a first-time visitor or a seasoned traveler, this itinerary offers an ideal combination of wildlife viewing and coastal tranquility.

Detailed 8-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Arusha

Upon your arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) at a time to be advised (TBA), you will be warmly welcomed by your personal guide, who will be waiting to greet you at the arrivals area. After a brief introduction and assistance with your luggage, you will be transferred in a comfortable private vehicle to your accommodation, where you can settle in and unwind after your journey. Upon arrival at the lodge or hotel, you will check in and have some time to relax and refresh. In the evening, enjoy a delicious welcome dinner prepared with local and international cuisine. After dinner, your guide will provide a detailed briefing about your upcoming safari adventure, covering key information and answering any questions you might have. The night will be spent at your accommodation, allowing you to rest and prepare for the exciting days ahead.

Day 2: Arusha - Tarangire National Park - Karatu

You will depart early in the morning from your lodge in Arusha and head to Tarangire National Park for an exciting game drive. The drive covers approximately 120 km and will take approximately 2 hours. A packed lunch will be provided, allowing you to enjoy a meal while admiring the breathtaking wildlife around you. Tarangire is renowned for its large herds of elephants, impressive baobab trees and diverse wildlife including lions, giraffes and various bird species. The park is particularly impressive during the dry season when the animals congregate around the Tarangire River. After an exhilarating day of exploration, you will depart in the late afternoon and begin your transfer to the Rift Valley Escarpment Wall. This scenic drive offers stunning views of the landscape, leading you to the Crater Highlands, where you will arrive for dinner and an overnight stay at your lodge. The drive from Tarangire to the Crater Highlands is approximately 150km and takes approximately 3 hours.

Day 3: Karatu – Central Serengeti

After an early breakfast, we depart from the Crater Highlands and make our way to the central part of Serengeti National Park. The journey covers approximately 150 kilometers and takes around 4 to 5 hours, including game viewing along the way. Our route passes through the stunning Ngorongoro Conservation Area, offering breathtaking landscapes and a chance to spot wildlife even before reaching Serengeti. With packed lunches provided, we continue the drive as the scenery transitions from the highlands to the endless plains of the Serengeti. Upon entering the park, we begin game drives en route, witnessing the incredible diversity of wildlife that the Serengeti is famous for, including large herds of wildebeest, zebras, predators, and a variety of bird species. By late afternoon, we arrive at your special camp or lodge in the central Serengeti, where you will enjoy dinner and an overnight stay under the vast African skies.

Day 4: Central Serengeti

You will spend the full day in the central Serengeti, exploring this iconic and vast national park, known for its incredible wildlife and spectacular scenery. After breakfast, we will embark on a morning game drive, where you will have the opportunity to observe the rich diversity of animals, including the famous "Big Five": lions, elephants, leopards, buffalo and rhinos. Depending on the time of year, you may also be lucky enough to witness the incredible wildebeest migration if you visit during this season.

A packed lunch will be provided and you can enjoy it either in the park or at a viewpoint, immersing yourself in the serenity of the Serengeti. In the late afternoon, we will continue with another game drive, exploring different parts of the park and spotting more wildlife. After a day of adventure, we will return to our accommodation in the Serengeti for dinner and overnight, where you can relax and reflect on the day's experiences.

Day 5: Central Serengeti – Ngorongoro Crater – Arusha – Zanzibar

On our final day, we'll have an early start and embark on a game drive through to the magnificent Ngorongoro Crater. We'll descend into the crater floor for an unforgettable experience, exploring this unique ecosystem and its abundant wildlife. We'll spend the morning immersed in the crater's wonders. In the late afternoon, we'll ascend back up the crater rim and begin our drive back to Arusha. From Arusha, board your local flight to Zanzibar. Upon arrival in Zanzibar, you will be met by your driver and transferred to your beach accommodation, where you can relax and enjoy the stunning views of the coast. After checking in, you will relax and enjoy the peaceful surroundings. Dinner will be served at your hotel and you will spend the night, ready to explore the beautiful beaches and attractions of Zanzibar.

You will spend a full day relaxing at your beach accommodation. Take this opportunity to unwind and enjoy the beautiful surroundings. This day is all about relaxation, allowing you to recharge your batteries and enjoy the beauty of Zanzibar at your own pace.
