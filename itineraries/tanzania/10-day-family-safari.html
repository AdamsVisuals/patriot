10 Day Tanzania Safari & Zanzibar Adventure

Experience the wild beauty of Tanzania's national parks and the tropical paradise of Zanzibar in one unforgettable journey.

10 Days / 9 Nights | From $4,085

10-Day Tanzania Safari & Zanzibar Experience

Embark on an unforgettable 10-day journey that combines Tanzania's most iconic wildlife destinations with the tropical paradise of Zanzibar. This carefully curated adventure offers the perfect blend of thrilling safari experiences, unique wildlife encounters, and relaxing beach moments, creating memories that will last a lifetime.

Experience the raw beauty of the African wilderness as you explore Tarangire National Park with its majestic elephant herds and spectacular baobab trees. Enjoy unique experiences like night game drives and walking safaris accompanied by armed rangers. Descend into the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater—one of the world's largest intact volcanic calderas—and explore the endless plains of the Serengeti, home to incredible wildlife concentrations.

Following your comprehensive safari adventure, unwind on the pristine white sands of Zanzibar. Experience the famous Blue Safari adventure with snorkeling in crystal-clear waters, explore historic Stone Town—a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and relax on beautiful beaches. This itinerary offers the perfect balance between adventure and relaxation.

This journey is designed for travelers seeking both wildlife encounters and beach relaxation, combining comfortable accommodations with personalized service throughout. Whether you're tracking elephants in Tarangire or swimming in the Indian Ocean, this itinerary offers the ultimate Tanzanian experience.

Detailed 10-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival Day

You will arrive at JRO (Kilimanjaro International Airport), where you'll be warmly welcomed. Afterward, you'll transfer to your accommodation in Arusha, where you'll have a pre-safari briefing to go over the details of your upcoming adventure. Following the briefing, enjoy a delightful dinner before settling in for an overnight stay, ready for the exciting days ahead.

Accommodation Options

Gran Melia Arusha

Luxury • Ultra-luxury accommodation with premium amenities

Under the Shades Safari Lodge

Mid-Range • Comfortable accommodation with all basic amenities

Day 2: Arusha - Tarangire National Park

You will depart early in the morning for Tarangire National Park, which is approximately 120 km away and takes about 2 to 2.5 hours by road. Known for its stunning landscapes and large elephant herds, Tarangire offers an exciting start to your safari adventure. You'll have a packed lunch as you enjoy a morning game drive, exploring the park's rich wildlife and diverse ecosystems. In the afternoon, you'll return to your lodge for some relaxation and preparation for the evening's night game drive. The night game drive gives you a unique opportunity to see nocturnal animals and experience the park after dark, guided by an experienced driver. Afterward, enjoy a delicious dinner and spend the night at your lodge, situated within the camp, ready for more adventures the following day.

Accommodation Options

Tarangire Kuro Treetops

Luxury • Unique treehouse accommodation with premium amenities

Tarangire Ndovu Tented Lodge

Mid-Range • Comfortable tented lodge experience

Day 3: Tarangire National Park

Wake up in the heart of Tarangire National Park, surrounded by the stunning natural beauty of the area. After a cup of coffee, you'll head out for a thrilling morning walk in the park accompanied by an armed ranger. This walk offers a unique, up-close experience of the park's ecosystem, allowing you to observe wildlife from a different perspective while learning about the environment and safety measures when in the wild. After the walk, return to the lodge for a hearty breakfast, and then depart for further game drives and exploration in the heart of Tarangire. Known for its large elephant herds and spectacular baobab trees, the park offers fantastic opportunities to spot wildlife. In the afternoon, you'll return to the lodge for lunch and have some free time to relax after the game drive. Enjoy the lodge's facilities, whether it's a refreshing swim or simply unwinding in the peaceful surroundings. You may also choose to organize an evening game drive with your guide, offering a chance to spot nocturnal animals. Dinner will be served at the lodge, where you'll enjoy a relaxing evening before an overnight stay.

Accommodation Options

Tarangire Kuro Treetops

Tarangire Ndovu Tented Lodge

Mid-Range • Comfortable tented lodge experience

Day 4: Tarangire – Ngorongoro Crater - Karatu

After breakfast at your accommodation near Tarangire National Park, embark on a scenic drive to the Ngorongoro Crater, often referred to as one of the unofficial "Eight Natural Wonders of the World," a breathtaking caldera teeming with diverse wildlife. Descend into this natural wonder for an unforgettable game drive, where you'll have the chance to spot the "Big Five" and a plethora of other animals in their natural habitat. In the late afternoon, ascend from the crater and journey to the charming town of Karatu, where you'll settle in for an overnight stay, ready for more adventures the following day.

Accommodation Options

The Manor Ngorongoro

Luxury • Exclusive lodge with premium service

Bougainvillea Lodge

Mid-Range • Comfortable lodge with scenic views

Day 5: Karatu – Central Serengeti

After an early breakfast, you'll depart for the Central Serengeti, located approximately 220 km away, which takes about 4 to 5 hours by road. As you drive through the Ngorongoro Conservation Area, you'll have the opportunity to enjoy game drives along the way, spotting wildlife as you journey towards the Serengeti. Central Serengeti is renowned for its rich wildlife and iconic landscapes, particularly during the Great Migration when millions of wildebeest and zebras move through the area. Upon arrival at your special camp, you'll enjoy a delicious dinner and spend the night in the heart of the Serengeti, surrounded by nature's beauty.

Accommodation Options

Day 6: Central Serengeti

Following breakfast, the day offers further opportunities for game drives and exploration in the central Serengeti. Early morning, we depart to maximize our chances of witnessing the incredible wildlife that roams these plains. Continue your search for wildlife, perhaps focusing on specific areas or animals you haven't yet encountered. The vastness of the Serengeti ensures that each game drive offers unique and unforgettable experiences. Late afternoon back to your camp for dinner and overnight.

Day 7: Central Serengeti – Zanzibar

This morning, you will depart early from Seronera Airstrip in the Serengeti to catch your flight to Zanzibar. Upon arrival at Zanzibar Airport, you will be greeted by a representative and transferred to your beach accommodation. The remainder of the day will be free for relaxation, culminating in dinner and an overnight stay at the beach.

Accommodation Options

Mid-Range • Comfortable beach resort with ocean views

Day 8: Zanzibar Blue Safari

You will embark on the unforgettable Zanzibar Blue Safari. This adventure takes you on a boat ride through the crystal-clear waters of the Indian Ocean, where you'll explore the stunning sandbanks, vibrant coral reefs, and peaceful lagoons. The safari includes opportunities for snorkeling, where you can swim alongside colorful fish and other marine life, as well as relax on the pristine beaches of the sandbanks. A fresh seafood lunch will be served on one of the secluded islands, allowing you to savor the flavors of the ocean while surrounded by the natural beauty of Zanzibar. This day offers a perfect blend of adventure, relaxation, and stunning coastal scenery, making it a highlight of your Zanzibar experience.

Accommodation Options

Melia Zanzibar

Mid-Range • Comfortable beach resort with ocean views

Day 9: Zanzibar Stone Town Exploration

You will begin with a fascinating Stone Town exploration. Known for its rich history and stunning architecture, Stone Town is a UNESCO World Heritage site filled with narrow alleys, bustling markets, and beautiful historical buildings. During your tour, you'll visit significant landmarks such as the Lutheran Church, where you can learn about the historical and cultural significance of the church in Zanzibar's diverse religious landscape. After exploring the town's vibrant streets, you will have some time to soak in the sights and sounds of this unique town. In the late afternoon, you will transfer back to your hotel, where you can unwind and enjoy a delicious dinner. Your day will end with an overnight stay at your beach resort, providing a restful end to an enriching day of exploration.

Accommodation Options

Mid-Range • Comfortable beach resort with ocean views

Day 10: Zanzibar – Back Home

This marks the final day in Zanzibar. After checking out of the lodge, you will be transferred to Zanzibar Airport for your onward international flight.

End of Itinerary
