6 Day Kilimanjaro Marangu Route

Trek to the summit of Mount Kilimanjaro via the popular Marangu Route.

6 Days / 5 Nights | From $1,850

6-Day Mount Kilimanjaro Marangu Route Expedition

Embark on an incredible 6-day expedition to conquer Mount Kilimanjaro, Africa's highest peak at 5,895 meters, via the classic Marangu Route. Known as the "Coca-Cola Route," this is the only route that offers comfortable mountain hut accommodations throughout the climb, making it a popular choice for many adventurers.

Experience the majestic beauty of Kilimanjaro as you ascend through diverse ecosystems, from lush rainforests to alpine meadows and the dramatic arctic summit zone. Each day offers spectacular views of Mawenzi and Kibo peaks, unique vegetation zones, and the ultimate challenge of reaching Uhuru Peak.

Our experienced mountain guides will ensure your safety and success throughout the climb. The Marangu Route's gradual ascent profile provides good acclimatization opportunities, while the comfortable hut accommodations offer shelter from the elements and a more comfortable mountain experience.

This expedition is perfect for those seeking to conquer Africa's rooftop with the comfort of mountain huts. The 6-day itinerary provides adequate time for acclimatization and a memorable journey to the roof of Africa.

Detailed 6-Day Kilimanjaro Expedition

Day 1: Arrival in Moshi Town

This might be your arrival day in Tanzania; our driver will pick you from Kilimanjaro International Airport and drive you to the hotel for overnight stay. Settle in, meet your guide for a briefing, and prepare for the adventure ahead.

Accommodation

Honey Badger Lodge

Pre-Climb Accommodation • Bed & Breakfast

Day 2: Marangu Gate (1,980m) - Mandara Hut (2,700m)

Altitude Gain: 720 meters

After breakfast, transfer to Marangu Gate and complete registration formalities. Start the climb through fascinating rainforest filled with exotic birds and wildlife, including the possibility of spotting black and white colobus monkeys.

The trail winds through lush vegetation to reach Mandara Hut, your first mountain accommodation at 2,700 meters. Enjoy your first night on the mountain surrounded by the sounds of the forest.

Accommodation

Mandara Hut

Mountain Hut • Rainforest zone (9,000ft)

Day 3: Mandara Hut (2,700m) - Horombo Hut (3,720m)

Altitude Gain: 1,020 meters

After breakfast, follow the ascending path through alpine meadows as you leave the rainforest behind. The landscape opens up with spectacular views of Mawenzi and Kibo peaks.

As you gain altitude, you'll notice the pace naturally slows down. Take time to enjoy the unique flora and breathtaking mountain scenery. Arrive at Horombo Hut, situated at 3,720 meters, where you'll spend the night acclimatizing to the higher elevation.

Accommodation

Horombo Hut

Mountain Hut • Alpine zone (12,520ft)

Day 4: Horombo Hut (3,720m) - Kibo Hut (4,700m)

Altitude Gain: 980 meters

After breakfast, resume your climb as the landscape becomes increasingly rugged and rocky. Giant lobelias and groundsels begin to appear, marking your transition into the alpine desert zone.

The trail takes you across the "Saddle" between Mawenzi and Kibo peaks, offering spectacular panoramic views. Arrive at Kibo Hut, your base for the summit attempt, and prepare for the early morning ascent.

Accommodation

Kibo Hut

Mountain Hut • Summit base camp (15,520ft)

Day 5: Summit Attempt! Kibo Hut (4,700m) - Uhuru Peak (5,895m) - Horombo Hut (3,720m)

Summit Day: The ultimate challenge begins around midnight. Climb the steep scree slope in the darkness, reaching Gilman's Point (5,681m) at sunrise - the most challenging part of the climb.

From Gilman's Point, continue along the crater rim for about 1-2 hours to reach Uhuru Peak, the highest point in Africa at 5,895 meters. Celebrate your achievement with breathtaking views before beginning the descent back to Horombo Hut for a well-deserved rest.

Accommodation

Horombo Hut

Mountain Hut • Post-summit rest (12,520ft)

Day 6: Return Descent to Moshi

A gentle trek takes you down through the changing landscapes back to Marangu Gate. Complete park formalities and receive your summit certificate - a proud momento of your achievement.

Meet your vehicle for transfer back to your hotel in Moshi, where you can enjoy a welcome shower and celebrate your incredible accomplishment. This marks the end of your Kilimanjaro adventure.

End of Expedition
