12 Day Luxury Safari & Beach

A luxury 12-day journey combining thrilling safaris in Serengeti and Ngorongoro with a relaxing beach escape in Zanzibar.

12 Days / 11 Nights | From $11,645

12-Day Tanzania Luxury Safari & Zanzibar Beach Experience

Embark on an extraordinary 12-day luxury journey that combines Tanzania's most iconic wildlife destinations with the tropical paradise of Zanzibar. This carefully curated experience takes you from the elephant-rich plains of Tarangire and the magnificent Ngorongoro Crater to the vast savannahs of Serengeti, culminating in the pristine beaches and rich cultural heritage of Zanzibar.

Experience the ultimate African adventure with seamless domestic flights between destinations, maximizing your time for wildlife viewing and relaxation. Each day brings new discoveries, from thrilling game drives in world-renowned national parks to cultural explorations in Stone Town and aquatic adventures in the turquoise waters of the Indian Ocean.

Your journey features exclusive high-end accommodations including Oliver's Camp in Tarangire, The Highlands in Ngorongoro, Dunia Camp in Serengeti, and Melia Zanzibar on the beach. Expert guides will share their deep knowledge of ecosystems and wildlife behaviors, ensuring you gain profound insights into Tanzania's natural wonders.

This luxury itinerary is designed for discerning travelers seeking the perfect balance between authentic African safari experiences and tropical beach relaxation. With premium accommodations, seamless logistics, and personalized service, you can focus entirely on creating unforgettable memories in East Africa's most breathtaking destinations.

Detailed 12-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrive Arusha

Upon your arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) at TBA, you will be warmly welcomed by your professional driver-guide who will be waiting to greet you just outside the arrivals terminal. After a brief introduction, you will be transferred to your accommodation in Arusha for dinner and an overnight stay. The drive from the airport to Arusha takes approximately 45min, covering a distance of about 50 kilometers depending on the location of your lodge and traffic conditions. Once at the lodge, you'll have time to relax and settle in. In the evening, your guide will conduct a pre-safari briefing to go over the details of your upcoming adventure. No packed lunch is provided on this day, as dinner will be served at the accommodation.

Day 2: Arusha – Tarangire National Park

After an early breakfast at your lodge in Arusha, you will be transferred to Arusha Airport for your scheduled flight to Tarangire. The flight typically takes around 30 to 45 minutes, offering scenic aerial views of northern Tanzania. Upon landing at the designated airstrip in Tarangire, you will be welcomed by your camp's driver-guide. From here, you'll begin exploring the stunning Tarangire National Park on shared and scheduled game drives. Known for its large elephant herds, ancient baobab trees, and seasonal swamps, Tarangire is a photographer's paradise and a haven for wildlife enthusiasts. A packed lunch will be provided to enjoy during your time in the park. In the late afternoon, you will head to your lodge inside the park for dinner and an overnight stay, surrounded by the sounds of the African wilderness.

Accommodation Options

Oliver's Camp

Day 3: Tarangire National Park

After an early morning breakfast at the lodge, you will embark on a full day of shared and scheduled game drives and exploration in Tarangire National Park. This day is dedicated to discovering more remote areas of the park, known for its high concentration of wildlife, including lions, leopards, giraffes, buffaloes, and the iconic elephant herds that roam among the ancient baobab trees. Depending on wildlife movement and your preference, you may choose to carry a packed lunch and enjoy a picnic in one of the designated scenic spots within the park, or alternatively, return to the lodge for a hot lunch and a short rest before continuing your afternoon game drive. As the sun begins to set, you will head back to your lodge for dinner and another overnight stay, capping off a rewarding day in the wild.

Day 4: Tarangire National Park – Lake Manyara National Park

After an early breakfast and checkout from your lodge, your driver-guide will transfer you to Kuro Airstrip within Tarangire National Park for your scheduled flight to Lake Manyara. The flight is short, typically around 30 minutes, offering scenic views over the Great Rift Valley. Upon arrival at the Lake Manyara airstrip, you will be met by your lodge's driver-guide and begin shared and scheduled game drives and exploration within Lake Manyara National Park. Though compact, the park boasts a remarkable diversity of habitats and wildlife, including tree-climbing lions, large troops of baboons, hippos, and a rich variety of birdlife along the lake's shoreline. A packed lunch will be provided to enjoy during your time in the park. Later in the afternoon, you will depart the park and drive approximately 1 hours (covering around 60–70 kilometers) to your lodge in the Ngorongoro Crater Highlands. Upon arrival, you'll settle in for dinner and an overnight stay at your lodge perched along the highlands, offering cooler temperatures and stunning views.

Day 5: Ngorongoro Crater

Today, after an early breakfast at your lodge, you will depart for the Ngorongoro Conservation Area and descend into the world-famous Ngorongoro Crater for a full day of shared and scheduled game drives and exploration. Often referred to as the "Eighth Wonder of the World," the crater is home to an extraordinary concentration of wildlife, including the Big Five – lion, leopard, rhino, elephant, and buffalo – all thriving within the caldera's lush ecosystem. As you explore the crater floor, you will enjoy a picnic lunch at one of the scenic designated sites, often near a hippo-filled lake or under a shady tree. In the late afternoon, after a rewarding day of wildlife viewing, you will ascend the crater walls and return to your lodge in the crater highlands for dinner and an overnight stay. The round-trip drive from your lodge to the crater floor and back typically covers about 60–80 kilometers and takes approximately 1 to 1.5 hours each way, depending on your lodge's location.

Day 6: Crater Highlands – Central Serengeti

After an early breakfast and checkout from your lodge, your driver-guide will transfer you to the nearest airstrip in Manyara for your scheduled flight to the iconic Serengeti National Park. The flight to Central Serengeti typically takes about 45 to 60 minutes, offering breathtaking aerial views of the vast savannahs and wildlife below. Upon arrival at the airstrip in Central Serengeti, you will be met by your camp's driver-guide and begin shared and scheduled game drives in this wildlife-rich region. Central Serengeti, particularly the Seronera Valley, is renowned for its abundant resident wildlife and frequent predator sightings, including lions, leopards, and cheetahs. A packed lunch will be provided to enjoy while out in the bush. In the late afternoon, you'll drive to your camp located inside the park for dinner and an overnight stay, allowing you to experience the magic of the Serengeti under starlit skies.

Day 7: Central Serengeti

After an early breakfast at your camp, you will head out for a full day of shared and scheduled game drives and exploration in the heart of the Central Serengeti. This region is known for its year-round concentration of wildlife, especially big cats like lions, cheetahs, and leopards, often seen prowling the plains or resting in the shade of acacia trees. Depending on the day's wildlife activity and your personal preference, you may choose to carry a packed lunch and continue your game drive uninterrupted, or return to camp midday for a hot lunch and a short rest before resuming your afternoon safari. As the sun begins to set over the golden savannah, you will return to your camp for dinner and another peaceful overnight stay surrounded by the sounds of the Serengeti wilderness.

Day 9: Central Serengeti - Zanzibar

After an early morning breakfast and checkout from your camp, your dedicated driver-guide will transfer you to the Seronera Airstrip for your scheduled flight to Zanzibar. Upon arrival in Zanzibar, you will be warmly welcomed by your local driver, who will assist with your luggage and provide a comfortable transfer to your beach accommodation. Once you arrive at the coastal retreat, you can unwind and take in the serene views of the Indian Ocean. Enjoy a relaxing evening with dinner served at the accommodation, followed by an overnight stay in this idyllic beach setting.

Day 10: Zanzibar

After breakfast at your beach accommodation, you will embark on the unforgettable Safari Blue experience—one of Zanzibar's most iconic and adventurous full-day excursions. You'll be transferred to the departure point in Fumba village, where a traditional dhow boat awaits. Set sail across the crystal-clear waters of Menai Bay, a protected marine area known for its vibrant marine life and stunning sandbanks. The day includes snorkeling among colorful coral reefs, spotting dolphins if you're lucky, and relaxing on pristine sandbanks surrounded by turquoise waters. A delicious seafood feast will be served on a remote island, featuring grilled fish, lobster, calamari, and tropical fruits. After lunch, you can swim in the natural lagoon known as the "Blue Lagoon" before sailing back to shore in the late afternoon. Return to your beach accommodation for a relaxing evening, dinner, and overnight stay.

Accommodation Options

Day 11: Zanzibar

After breakfast, your day begins with a guided exploration of Stone Town, the historical heart of Zanzibar and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Wander through the winding alleys and bustling markets as your guide shares stories of the island's rich Swahili culture, Arab influence, and colonial past. You'll visit key landmarks such as the Old Fort, House of Wonders, Freddie Mercury's birthplace, and the former slave market. After soaking in the vibrant history and architecture of Stone Town, you'll continue on to a Spice Tour—a journey into Zanzibar's fragrant past. Walk through a local plantation where you'll see, smell, and taste an array of spices and tropical fruits, including cloves, nutmeg, cinnamon, vanilla, and more. Learn how these spices are cultivated and their traditional uses in medicine and cooking. After the tour, return to your beach accommodation for dinner and your final overnight stay in Zanzibar.

Day 12: Travel Day

Today marks the end of your unforgettable journey. After an early morning breakfast and checkout from your beach accommodation, your driver will transfer you to Zanzibar International Airport in time to catch your onward international flight. Take one last look at the island's tropical beauty as you reflect on the incredible experiences and memories made—from the wildlife of the Serengeti to the spice-scented breezes of Zanzibar. Safe travels and we hope to welcome you back to Tanzania someday!
