Trek to the summit of Mount Kilimanjaro via the popular Machame Route.

9 Days / 8 Nights | From $2,015

9-Day Mount Kilimanjaro Machame Route Expedition

Embark on an extraordinary 9-day expedition to conquer Mount Kilimanjaro, Africa's highest peak at 5,895 meters, via the scenic Machame Route. Known as the "Whiskey Route" for its challenging yet rewarding nature, this journey takes you through diverse ecosystems from lush rainforests to alpine deserts and the dramatic arctic summit zone.

Experience the majestic beauty of Kilimanjaro as you traverse through breathtaking landscapes including the Shira Plateau, Barranco Wall, and the stunning Karanga Valley. Each day offers spectacular views, unique vegetation zones, and the ultimate challenge of reaching Uhuru Peak at sunrise.

Our experienced Patriot Tours guides will ensure your safety and success throughout the climb. The Machame Route's excellent acclimatization profile and scenic variety make it one of the most popular routes with a high success rate for summit attempts.

This expedition is designed for adventure seekers looking to conquer Africa's rooftop through one of its most beautiful and challenging routes. The 9-day itinerary provides optimal acclimatization and an unforgettable journey to the roof of Africa.

Detailed 9-Day Kilimanjaro Expedition

Day 1: Arrival Day

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), you will be warmly welcomed by your Patriot Tours guide. After a short meet-and-greet, you will be transferred to your accommodation in Moshi or Arusha, where you can relax and recover from your journey.

In the evening, you will have a pre-trekking briefing, where your guide will go over the Machame Route itinerary, safety protocols, altitude acclimatization tips, and a final gear check to ensure you are fully prepared for the adventure ahead. Enjoy a delicious dinner and a good night's rest in anticipation of your trek.

Accommodation

Weruweru River Lodge

Pre-Climb Accommodation • Comfortable lodge

Day 2: Machame Gate (1,800m) → Machame Camp (3,000m)

Distance: 11 km (~5–7 hrs)

Terrain: Rainforest

After breakfast, the journey begins with a drive to Machame Gate, where registration is completed. The trek starts through lush rainforest with a steady ascent. The trail can be muddy and slippery, so trekking poles are helpful.

The day ends upon arrival at Machame Camp, where climbers rest and acclimate to the altitude while surrounded by the beautiful rainforest environment.

Accommodation

Machame Camp

Mountain Camp • Rainforest zone

Day 3: Machame Camp (3,000m) → Shira Camp (3,840m)

Distance: 5 km (~4–6 hrs)

Terrain: Moorland

After breakfast, the trek continues with a steep ascent through moorland, leaving the rainforest behind. The trail offers breathtaking views of Mount Meru and the Shira Plateau.

By mid-afternoon, climbers arrive at Shira Camp, where they have time to relax, explore, and enjoy the sunset over the vast plateau. This marks your entry into the high-altitude moorland zone.

Accommodation

Shira Camp

Mountain Camp • Moorland plateau

Day 4: Shira Camp (3,840m) → Lava Tower (4,630m) → Barranco Camp (3,960m)

Distance: 10 km (~6–8 hrs)

Terrain: Alpine Desert

After breakfast, the trek heads toward Lava Tower for high-altitude acclimatization. The landscape changes to a barren alpine desert, and the climb becomes steeper.

Reaching Lava Tower, climbers take a break before descending to Barranco Camp, passing through the stunning Barranco Valley. This "climb high, sleep low" strategy is essential for proper acclimatization and summit success.

Accommodation

Barranco Camp

Mountain Camp • Acclimatization camp

Day 5: Barranco Camp (3,960m) → Karanga Camp (4,035m)

Distance: 5 km (~4–5 hrs)

Terrain: Rocky, Alpine Desert

After breakfast, the day starts with a climb up the Barranco Wall, a challenging but rewarding scramble. The climb is not technical, but it requires careful footing and some use of hands for support.

After reaching the top, the route descends into the beautiful Karanga Valley before a final short ascent to Karanga Camp. This shorter day allows trekkers to rest and acclimatize before the summit push.

Accommodation

Karanga Camp

Mountain Camp • High-altitude camp

Day 6: Karanga Camp (4,035m) → Barafu Camp (4,680m)

Distance: 4 km (~4–5 hrs)

Terrain: Alpine Desert

After breakfast, the trek continues through a dry, rocky landscape with increasing altitude. The air becomes thinner, and climbers must take a slow and steady pace.

Upon reaching Barafu Camp, trekkers prepare for the midnight summit push, eating an early dinner and resting as much as possible before the final ascent to Uhuru Peak.

Accommodation

Barafu Camp

Mountain Camp • Summit base camp

Day 7: Barafu Camp (4,680m) → Uhuru Peak (5,895m) → Mweka Camp (3,100m)

Distance: 17 km (~12–16 hrs total)

Terrain: Arctic → Alpine Desert → Rainforest

The toughest and most rewarding day begins around midnight. Trekkers hike in the dark, navigating steep switchbacks toward Stella Point (5,756m). From there, it's a final push to Uhuru Peak, the highest point in Africa.

Reaching the summit at sunrise provides a once-in-a-lifetime moment with breathtaking views. After taking photos and celebrating, the long descent back to Barafu Camp begins, followed by a further descent to Mweka Camp for a well-deserved rest.

Accommodation

Mweka Camp

Mountain Camp • Post-summit camp

Day 8: Mweka Camp (3,100m) → Mweka Gate (1,640m) → Moshi

Distance: 10 km (~3–4 hrs)

Terrain: Rainforest

After breakfast, the final descent takes trekkers through the lush rainforest, where they may spot wildlife and exotic plants. Upon reaching Mweka Gate, climbers receive their summit certificates.

You will be transferred back to Moshi, where you can celebrate your achievement with a well-earned meal and rest, reflecting on your incredible accomplishment.

Accommodation

Weruweru River Lodge

Post-Climb Accommodation • Celebration stay

Day 9: Departure – Transfer to Kilimanjaro International Airport

After an incredible adventure on Mount Kilimanjaro, your journey comes to an end. Early in the morning, after breakfast, you will be transferred to Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) for your onward international flight.

This is a moment to reflect on your achievement, from the breathtaking landscapes to the challenge of reaching Uhuru Peak. If time allows before your flight, you may have a chance to pick up some souvenirs or simply relax before your departure.

End of Expedition
