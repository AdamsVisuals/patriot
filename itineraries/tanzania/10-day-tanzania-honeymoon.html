10 Day Honeymoon Adventure

Experience the wild beauty of Tanzania, where romantic days unfold from the Serengeti's golden plains to the pristine beaches of Zanzibar.

10 Days / 9 Nights | From $5,850

10-Day Tanzania Safari & Zanzibar Beach Experience

Embark on an unforgettable 10-day romantic journey that combines Tanzania's iconic wildlife destinations with the tropical paradise of Zanzibar. This carefully curated honeymoon adventure offers the perfect blend of thrilling safari experiences and relaxing beach moments, creating memories that will last a lifetime.

Experience the raw beauty of the African wilderness as you explore the endless plains of the Serengeti, witness the incredible biodiversity of Ngorongoro Crater, and marvel at the elephant herds of Tarangire National Park. Each day brings new wildlife encounters and breathtaking landscapes that will leave you in awe.

Following your safari adventure, unwind on the pristine white sands of Zanzibar. Explore historic Stone Town, swim with tropical fish in crystal-clear waters, encounter giant tortoises, and discover the island's rich culture and natural wonders. From forest exploration to sunset dhow cruises, Zanzibar offers the perfect romantic conclusion to your African adventure.

This journey is designed for couples seeking both adventure and relaxation, combining luxury accommodations with personalized service throughout. Whether you're watching wildlife on the Serengeti plains or enjoying a romantic dinner on the beach, this itinerary offers the perfect honeymoon experience in Tanzania.

Detailed 10-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Airport Pick Up

You will be picked up from Kilimanjaro International Airport by our staff and transferred to Arusha for resting after a long flight. Our staff will answer all your queries regarding your upcoming safari and provide you with a short briefing as well. Afterwards, you will be taken to your accommodation.

Accommodation Options

Arusha Gran Melia Hotel

Luxury • Premium hotel with all amenities

Arusha Planet Lodge

Semi-Luxury • Comfortable lodge experience

Day 2: Arusha to Serengeti National Park

After breakfast you will travel to Serengeti National Park, get in the beautiful endless plains of Serengeti take a breath and enjoy the beautiful scenery rolling into the distance as far as your eyes can see. You will head in the central part of the park known as the Seronera circuit which is very rich for wildlife habitat in the park, the place is featured by the Seronera River that provides water for the animals around this area. You will arrive around evening and enjoy your game drive.

Accommodation Options

Serengeti Gran Melia Safari Lodge

Luxury • Premium safari lodge with excellent game viewing

Serengeti Sound Silence Tented Lodge

Semi-Luxury • Authentic tented lodge experience

Day 3 & 4: Full Days in Serengeti Plains

We have 2 full days in this wonderful park, rising early to make the most of our time. Our guides will choose the best spotting locations for the time of year; we can spend time at the Hippo pool watching these majestic animals laze about in the cool water happily living alongside the crocodiles, watch a big pride of lions, be in the middle of the migration, sometimes surrounded by wildebeest, sometimes by zebras, which travel with the wildebeest.

We journey from the wide open plains to the kopjes: these volcanic rocky outcrops provide protection and shelter for a wide variety of animals and from the top; we can look out across the vast grasslands of the Serengeti. This diverse and interesting landscape will provide us with the ultimate in game viewing, we will hopefully see all of the plain games; elephants, giraffe, zebra, wildebeest, lions and if you are lucky the elusive leopard and cheetah, as well as huge amounts of interesting birdlife, from the elegant secretary birds to the flightless ostrich.

Day 5: Ngorongoro Crater

After breakfast, taking picnic lunches with you and drive to Ngorongoro conservation crater descend down to the crater floor for a full day of tracking game with your guide. The crater is 610m deep and 260km² wide, making it the largest Caldera in the world. The crater contains all the big five animals (lions, elephants, rhinos, leopard and buffaloes), hippos, cheetah, antelopes and spotted hyena. Also in the crater you can see different species of birds. Other game such as velvet monkey, jackal, foxes, rock and tree hyraxes, warthog, gnu, zebra and hare. You will have your lunch and later drive to Lake Manyara.

Day 6: Tarangire National Park & Flight to Zanzibar

Drive from your hotel 8.00am to Tarangire National Park after early breakfast. Tarangire National Park is known for its elephant herds of up to 300, migratory wildebeest, zebra, buffalo, impala, gazelle, hartebeest and eland grazing and browsing in the dry river bed. There is Silale swamp that attracts 550 varieties of birds, the most breeding species you'll see in one habitat anywhere in the world! The park is also home of tree climbing pythons, as well as lions and leopards. You will enjoy game drive and game viewing with picnic lunch. Later transferred to the airport for a flight to Zanzibar.

Day 7: Zanzibar (Prison Island and Sandbank Tour)

Prison Island and Stone Town tour in Zanzibar is a full day tour starting and ending at your hotel. The main attractions include historical sites and monuments, Zanzibar tortoises, swimming, beach relaxation, and exploring historical sites. You'll visit Prison Island (Changuu Island) to see the giant Aldabra tortoises, enjoy swimming and snorkeling in crystal-clear waters, and relax on pristine white sandbanks.

Day 8: Zanzibar (Jozani Forest, Kuza Caves and Paje Beach)

Visit Jozani Forest, the only national park found in Zanzibar. The park is famous for Red Colobus Monkey, the rare species which is endemic to Zanzibar. The forest accommodates variety of tree species, mangrove swamp being one of them as the home to few unique species like Sykes monkey, bush babies and over 50 butterfly and 40 species of birds. Later you will tour the Kuza Caves and go to relax in Paje Beach, known for its turquoise waters and perfect swimming conditions.

Day 9: Zanzibar (Safari Blue Tour)

Safari Blue tour in Zanzibar is a full day tour starting and ending at your hotel. The main attractions include snorkeling, swimming, and beach relaxation on the sandbank. Safari Blue trip in Zanzibar is one among the best sea adventures in the Zanzibar island, not only sailing along with the traditional dhow but also you can have snorkeling, swim, and relaxing on a white sandy beach on a sandbank.

In addition to all, there are plenty of sea foods whom are all waiting for you to be tested. The dhow departs from the Fumba Beach at 09:00am and we get back around 03:30pm. With your Safari Blue guide, you will explore the mangrove lagoon hidden inside the Kwale Island.

Day 10: Zanzibar - Back Home

Relax at your lodge and later transferred to Zanzibar Airport and fly to Julius Nyerere International Airport in Dar es Salaam to connect with your International flight back home.

End of Itinerary
