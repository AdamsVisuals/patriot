14 Day Honeymoon Adventure

Experience the wild beauty of Tanzania, where romantic days unfold from the Serengeti's golden plains to the pristine beaches of Zanzibar.

14 Days / 13 Nights | From $4,921

14-Day Tanzania Safari & Zanzibar Beach Experience

Embark on an unforgettable 14-day romantic journey that combines Tanzania's most iconic wildlife destinations with the tropical paradise of Zanzibar. This carefully curated honeymoon adventure offers the perfect blend of thrilling safari experiences, cultural immersion, and relaxing beach moments, creating memories that will last a lifetime.

Experience the raw beauty of the African wilderness as you explore Arusha National Park, witness the tree-climbing lions of Lake Manyara, marvel at the endless plains of the Serengeti, descend into the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater, and encounter the elephant herds of Tarangire National Park. Each day brings new wildlife encounters and breathtaking landscapes that will leave you in awe.

Following your comprehensive safari adventure, unwind on the pristine white sands of Zanzibar. Explore historic Stone Town, encounter giant tortoises on Prison Island, discover aromatic spice plantations, swim with tropical fish, and experience the famous Safari Blue adventure. With dedicated relaxation days, this itinerary offers the perfect romantic conclusion to your African adventure.

This journey is designed for couples seeking both adventure and relaxation, combining luxury accommodations with personalized service throughout. Whether you're walking through Arusha National Park or enjoying a romantic dinner on the beach, this itinerary offers the ultimate honeymoon experience in Tanzania.

Detailed 14-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Airport Pick Up

You will be picked up from Kilimanjaro International Airport by our staff and transferred to Arusha for resting after a long flight. Our staff will answer all your queries regarding your upcoming safari and provide you with a short briefing as well. Afterwards, you will be taken to your accommodation.

Day 2: Arusha National Park

Your driver will pick you at your hotel around 08:00 AM, by then you will have had your breakfast already. You will drive from your hotel to the nearest national park which is Arusha National Park. Despite the small size of the park, common animals include giraffe, Cape buffalo, zebra, warthog, the black-and-white colobus monkey, the blue monkey, flamingo, elephant, bushbuck and many other African animals. Birdlife in the forest is prolific, with many forest species more easily seen here than elsewhere on the tourist route.

To make this more interesting you will get a chance to explore the park on a walking safari. Get a chance to be up close with nature's beauty with loving sounds of birds, sight of animals such as zebras and giraffes and the beautiful landscape of the park. Don't worry you will be escorted by a trained ranger on this execution so all is well with your exploration. After the thrills of the day you will be taken back to your hotel for the night.

Day 3: Arusha to Lake Manyara National Park

On your drive from Arusha sit back and enjoy the scenic views of the changing vegetation. The drive will approximately take you two hours. You will be able to see different human activities such as farms and stones quarries from the roads. You might also see Maasai boys with their cattle grazing or moving in search of water and grass.

Lake Manyara National Park though another small park is perfect for bird watching. Game in Lake Manyara include elephants, wildebeests along with so many giraffes, and buffaloes. In addition, the park is also very famous for its large number of zebras, impalas, warthogs and waterbucks. After the adventure of the day you will head to Karatu for overnight.

Day 4: Karatu to Serengeti National Park

This day starts with early breakfast at your hotel followed by a drive through Ngorongoro to Serengeti National Park. Take a breath and enjoy the beautiful scenery rolling into the distance as far as your eyes can see. You will head in the central part of the park known as the Seronera circuit which is very rich in wildlife habitat, the place is featured by the Seronera River that provides Water for the Animals.

Accommodation Options

Day 5 & 6: Full Days in Serengeti Plains

We have two full days in this wonderful park, rising early to make the most of our time. Our guides will choose the best spotting locations for the time of year; we can spend time at the Hippo pool watching these majestic animals laze about in the cool water happily living alongside the crocodiles, watch a big pride of lions, be in the middle of the migration, sometimes surrounded by wildebeest, sometimes by zebras that travel with the wildebeest.

We journey from the wide open plains to the kopjes: these volcanic rocky outcrops provide protection and shelter for a wide variety of animals and from the top; we can look out across the vast grasslands of the Serengeti. This diverse and interesting landscape will provide us with the ultimate in game viewing, we will hopefully see all of the plain games; elephants, giraffe, zebra, wildebeest, lions and if you are lucky the elusive leopard and cheetah, as well as huge amounts of interesting birdlife, from the elegant secretary birds to the flightless ostrich.

Day 7: Serengeti to Ngorongoro

Early morning game drive in the Serengeti plains trying to capture what was missed on yesterday's drive. Later in the afternoon start heading towards the Ngorongoro conservation area with a game drive en route. Your driver will drive on a different route from the one you did on day three. This drive gives you an opportunity to glimpse what the two world heritage sites have to offer from the golden savannah grasslands of Serengeti to the montane forest of Ngorongoro.

Day 8: Ngorongoro Crater to Tarangire

Taking picnic lunches with you, your drive today will lead you to a descend down the crater floor for a full exploration of what the crater has to offer by tracking game with your guide. The crater is 610m deep and 260km² wide, making it the largest Caldera in the world. The crater contains all the big five animals (lions, elephants, rhinos, leopard and buffaloes), hippos, cheetah, antelopes and spotted hyena. Also in the crater you can see different species of birds. Other game such as velvet monkey, jackal, foxes, rock and tree hyraxes, warthog, gnu, zebra and hare. Later you will drive to Tarangire for the night.

Day 9: Tarangire National Park & Flight to Zanzibar

After Breakfast you will drive to Tarangire National Park which is known for its vast elephant herds of up to 300, migratory wildebeest, zebra, buffalo, impala, gazelle, hartebeest and eland grazing and browsing in the dry river bed. There is Silale swamp that attracts 550 varieties of birds, the most breeding species you'll see in one habitat anywhere in the world! The park is also home to tree climbing pythons, as well as lions and leopards. You will enjoy game drive and game viewing with picnic lunch. Later drive to Arusha airport for your flight to Zanzibar.

Day 10: Zanzibar (Prison Island and Sandbank Tour)

Prison Island and Stone Town tour in Zanzibar is a full day tour that starts and ends at your hotel. The main attractions include historical sites and monuments, Zanzibar tortoises, swimming, and beach relaxation. You'll visit Prison Island (Changuu Island) to see the giant Aldabra tortoises, enjoy swimming and snorkeling in crystal-clear waters, and explore the historical sites of Stone Town.

Day 11: Zanzibar (Jozani Forest, Kuza Caves and Paje Beach)

Jozani Forest is the only national park found in Zanzibar. The park is famous for Red Colobus Monkey, the rare species which is endemic to Zanzibar. The forest accommodates variety of tree species, mangrove swamp being one of them as the home to few unique species like Sykes monkey, bush babies and over 50 butterfly and 40 species of birds. Later you will tour the Kuza Caves and have leisure time relaxing on the beautiful Paje Beach.

Day 12: Zanzibar (Safari Blue Tour)

Safari Blue tour in Zanzibar is a full day tour that starts and ends at your hotel. The main attractions include snorkeling, swimming, and beach relaxation on the sandbank. Safari Blue trip in Zanzibar is one among the best sea adventures, not only will you be sailing along with the traditional dhow but you can also get to snorkel, swim, and relax on the white sandy beaches.

In addition to this, there are plenty of sea foods awaiting your to taste, the dhow departs from Fumba beach at 09:00am and we get back around 03:30pm, with your Safari Blue guide, you will explore the mangrove lagoon hidden inside the Kwale Island.

Transfer to Zanzibar Airport for your International flight back home. Depending on your flight schedule, you may have time for some last-minute souvenir shopping or a final breakfast overlooking the ocean before departing with unforgettable memories of your Tanzanian adventure.
