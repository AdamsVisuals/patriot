3 Day Tarangire & Ngorongoro Safari

Experience the majestic wildlife of Tarangire National Park and the wonders of Ngorongoro Crater

3 Days / 2 Nights | From $655

3-Day Tarangire & Ngorongoro Crater Safari

Embark on an unforgettable 3-day journey through two of Tanzania's most spectacular wildlife destinations - Tarangire National Park and the Ngorongoro Crater. This carefully curated safari offers an authentic African experience with diverse wildlife viewing in some of Africa's most stunning landscapes.

Experience the raw beauty of the African wilderness as you explore Tarangire National Park, famous for its ancient baobab trees and large herds of African elephants. Then descend into the world-renowned Ngorongoro Crater, the largest intact caldera in the world, home to an incredible concentration of wildlife including all the Big Five.

Our expert guides will share their extensive knowledge of the ecosystems and wildlife behaviors, ensuring you gain deep insights into Tanzania's natural wonders. With comfortable accommodations and seamless logistics, you can focus entirely on the breathtaking safari experience across these two distinct but equally magnificent destinations.

This comprehensive journey is perfect for those seeking an authentic African adventure combined with the comfort of well-appointed lodges and camps. Experience the best of northern Tanzania's wildlife in a compact yet rewarding itinerary.

Detailed 3-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrive Arusha

You will arrive at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), where you will be warmly greeted by your dedicated safari guide. They will assist you with your transfer to your accommodation, ensuring a smooth and comfortable journey. Once at your lodge or hotel, you'll have time to relax and settle in before enjoying a delicious dinner.

Afterward, you'll have an overnight stay, allowing you to rest and recharge for the exciting safari ahead. In the evening, there will be a pre-safari briefing, where your guide will provide essential information about the safari, covering safety protocols, the upcoming itinerary, and what to expect during the adventure.

Day 2: Arusha – Tarangire – Karatu

Early in the morning after breakfast, we will depart for Tarangire National Park for a full day of game drives and exploration. Tarangire is one of Tanzania's largest and most beautiful parks, famous for its ancient baobab trees and large herds of African elephants. The park is home to a wide variety of wildlife including lions, giraffes, zebras, and many bird species, offering an incredible safari experience.

The drive from Arusha to Tarangire is approximately 120 km, taking about 2 hours. You will enjoy your packed lunch inside the park, surrounded by the stunning scenery and wildlife. In the late afternoon, we will check out of the park and drive towards Karatu for dinner and overnight. The drive from Tarangire to Karatu is around 100 km, taking approximately 2 to 2.5 hours, passing through picturesque landscapes, local villages, and along the dramatic Rift Valley escarpment.

Day 3: Karatu - Ngorongoro Crater - Arusha

After breakfast, taking picnic lunches with you and drive to Ngorongoro Crater. Descend down to the crater floor for a full day of tracking game with your guide. The crater is 610m deep and 260km² wide, making it the largest Caldera in the world.

The crater contains all the big five animals (lions, elephants, rhinos, leopard and buffaloes), hippos, cheetah, antelopes and spotted hyena. Also in the crater you can see different species of birds, and some animals such as velvet monkey, jackal, foxes, rock and tree hyraxes, warthog, gnu, zebra and hare.

In the late afternoon, after your game drive and exploration in Ngorongoro Crater, you will ascend from the caldera and begin your drive back to Arusha. The journey from Ngorongoro to Arusha covers approximately 190 km and takes about 4 hours, passing through scenic landscapes and local villages. Upon arrival in Arusha, you will be transferred directly to Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) for your onward international flight.
