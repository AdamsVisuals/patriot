About Patriot Tours and Safaris

Patriot Tours and Safaris is a premier safari company operating across Africa's most spectacular destinations. From the vast savannahs of East Africa to the pristine wilderness of Southern Africa, we bring you unparalleled access to the continent's most extraordinary wildlife experiences.

Africa offers some of the world's richest wildlife diversity, with countless national parks, game reserves, marine sanctuaries, and UNESCO World Heritage sites. Our continent boasts the Serengeti's Great Migration, the Okavango Delta's waterways, the Victoria Falls' thunderous beauty, and iconic landmarks like Mount Kilimanjaro and Table Mountain.

We welcome you to go beyond what you see in travel brochures and Google searches. Experience Africa in its raw, authentic beauty - from globally famous landmarks to hidden gems known only to local experts. At Patriot Tours, we believe in immersive experiences that connect you deeply with Africa's soul.

Our Mission

Protecting Africa's wildlife for future generations while delivering exceptional safari experiences

Crafting extraordinary African safari experiences with unparalleled luxury and expertise since 2019.
